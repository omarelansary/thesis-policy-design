### 3.9 (`05` line 40; CH-0063): REPLACEMENT ADDS A CLAIM (location and "what is wrong" confirmed)
- (i) Location: `05` line 40 holds "Direction (weak): abstracting beats archiving full history in Apple and PREFINE. **This does not support "tool memory only" ...**". Confirmed.
- (ii) "Overstates; the cited result is cost, not completion" is true for Apple: `evidence/literature-20261019/arxiv-2607.09493.txt` lines 32-35 ("selective memory completes 12/12 at 3.9K, and full conversation history completes 8/12 at 7.7K") and 41-43 ("indistinguishable on completion (p = 0.125) and separate on price"); also line 845 "Selective vs. full history does not (p = 0.125)". CH-0063 line 18 quotes the same figures correctly. "No-memory arm fails by design": lines 29-31 and 43-44 ("Every no-memory failure is a specification failure"; the specification is "established once and then withheld"): supported.
- (iii) The proposed replacement says "abstracting costs less than archiving full history in Apple **and PREFINE** (3.9K vs 7.7K input tokens), while completion is not distinguishable (p = 0.125)". The figures and p-value are Apple's only. PREFINE (`arxiv-2604.17886.txt`) does not report them: its abstract (lines 25-29) says "PREFINE outperforms all baselines and alone surpasses [full-history prompting] on Preference Transfer", an accuracy claim in which a difference is claimed, and its token section (lines 1592-1603) compares memory construction cost with Mem0 and A-MEM and inference memory tokens "26 ... versus 97-1,083 for the retrieval and memory baselines", not against full-history prompting (searched "token|cost|efficien" in that file; a `Full-History` token count was not found in the places searched, appendix D.4 not read, so this is "not found", not "absent"). So attaching "(3.9K vs 7.7K)" and "completion is not distinguishable" to both studies is a claim the sources do not make for PREFINE, and the "completion not distinguishable" half contradicts PREFINE's Transfer result. Suggested repair (an edit, not a design choice): state the cost and p-value for Apple only, and for PREFINE say only what its abstract says (abstracted preference memory outperforms full-history prompting on one sub-task; token cost against full history not found).
- The note's own caveat "(figures as quoted by CH-0063 line 18; to be checked against the study before applying)" is now discharged for Apple: the figures match the study.
