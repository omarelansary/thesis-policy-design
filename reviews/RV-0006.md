## 2. Answers to the lead's seven attack points (short)
1. **Overreach.** No number is wrong. Overreach is in framing: LOMEKWI (CH-0058), KAIST as prior (CH-0059), selective memory "beating" full history (CH-0063), "an instructed channel" for vendors (CH-0060), "no number in tokens" (CH-0065).
2. **Verdicts.** A: PARTLY is right but for a different reason than stated (mechanism is documented for two providers; third-party numbers are missing). B and C: the exact question is NO; PARTLY is a generous label (CH-0068). No topic is "actually answered". The gap for B is real, but its shape is mis-specified (CH-0061).
3. **Relevance of LOMEKWI s6 and KAIST.** Both are weak. Visible tools, sub-35B Qwen, hand-solvable task (LOM); no search channel and prompted yes/no (K). They are not a prior against initiation; they show prompt dependence and false-positive calling (CH-0058, CH-0059).
4. **Pilot 2 reframing.** Sound in substance, not new, and incompletely stated: keep the tool-description-only cell as primary, charge statement tokens, pre-register statements (CH-0062). It dodges nothing if the unprompted cell is kept.
5. **Selection bias.** Real; the queries are in CH-0067.
6. **Pilot 1.** Right for the narrow question, understated for a cost claim (CH-0066).
7. **Corrected sentences.** Still stronger or stale: CH-0064.
