## Was the uniform concession warranted?
Yes on the evidence read, for all ten records: no cited extract or governing passage contradicts the objection, and every edit is present in the current DPR text. Two sub-items were conceded on thinner ground than the records state:
- CH-0004 item 2: S4G L26 says the replay messages bear "on comparability with live servers, not on recovery"; the objection's "second reference-route channel" for policies is an extension. The edit (mark S2/S7 vehicle-contaminated) is cheap and harmless.
- CH-0008 item 1: the extracts do not say the JIT stage is agent-driven or a compact-list-plus-upgrade; they call it an "additional selection stage". DPR-0002 L65 states "agent-driven schema loading" as fact. This is the one place where a concession introduced a statement the cited evidence does not carry. Resolving it needs the source passage that defines the JIT mechanism (arXiv 2511.03728, method section), which is stored (source id `s4f-2b-ondevice-adaptive-2511-03728`, total 1685 lines) but which I could not read: no execution tool in this session.
