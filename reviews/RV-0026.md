# Review of the landscape record (thread T-20261028-126, AUTH-020 fourth amendment)

Labels: [F] stated in the stored file, [O] observed by me, [I] my reading, [U] unknown. I read stored pages and records directly; I ran no search, no script, sent no message. "Not found" means not found in the places I looked.

## 1. Verdict
The record is careful in many places. It marks unchecked rows as "lead", keeps vendor numbers as claims, lists eleven corrections to the scouts (s5, all of which I could trace to the stewards' notes), says no V5 decision is changed, and uses [I] labels for its own readings. The tally 45 / 19 / 1 reproduces from the nine SUM counts. I found no case of an unchecked scout claim presented as checked. The weaknesses are of selection and of proposal logic: the checked set is the supportive subset by construction; the strongest contrary rows in the scout reports were never checked or mentioned; three proposals (PL-2, PL-3, PL-5) contradict evidence or a sibling proposal in the same record; the "gap stands" and "no vector" statements are worded more strongly than the pages allow. Twelve challenges (CH-0127..CH-0138): 0 blocking, 7 should-fix, 5 note. None changes the direction of a proposal on its own; together they say the record should be read as an inventory of what the stored pages say plus a set of untested proposals, and PL-2, PL-3 and PL-5 should be reworded before the human sees them as recommendations.

The record should state both (CH-0131).
- **Model-written summary as the default writer (reject PL-2):** favoured by REQ-O21-03, ACE's itemised, counter-based curator (D22) and Copilot's cited, verified memories; against: D27 self-generated skills, G10, MEMDRIFT.

- **Fixed expiry without renewal (alternative to PL-3):** favoured by E22's over-call result; add as an arm.

## Overall view and uncertainty
Not a recommendation to adopt or reject any proposal. The record is usable as an inventory of what the stored pages say, provided the 45 / 19 / 1 tally is read as "the scouts' wording matches the page" for a supportive subset, and PL-2, PL-3 and PL-5 are read as untested ideas that currently conflict with evidence the record itself cites. The lean I find is toward keeping V5 as it is, produced mainly by selection (supportive claims checked, contrary ones left as unread leads) and by wording ("stands", "nothing found argues"), not by any false figure I found.
