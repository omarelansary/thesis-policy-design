# Methods review of the benchmark register, sections 1 to 6

Labels: [F] stated in a stored file, [O] observed by me, [I] my reading, [U] unknown. I read the stored summaries in full and opened stored pages for the points below; I ran no search or script and sent no message. "Not found" means not found in the places I looked. Sections 7-10 of `17` are still "to be filled", so I reviewed no ranking, no candidate list and no completion check. After the interruption I re-checked the challenges against their cited pages and corrected two (below).

## 1. Verdict
Sections 1-3 are careful in tone: the lead withdraws its own rigid rules, labels the reading as [I] where no source prescribes it, and states in s3 what the methods literature does not settle. Sections 4-6 are where the problems are. Nineteen challenges (CH-0139..CH-0157): 0 blocking, 16 should-fix, 3 note. The register's usable core is intact (construct-first, native versus modified, the four components, the provenance idea, the independent ranking); the objections concern (a) cell values that are stale or mixed across versions, (b) rules and hard requirements that say more than their sources or are not properties of a candidate, (c) triage reasons that do not match the construct they are applied to, (d) a floor/ceiling bias the register names and then leaves unconstrained, and (e) risks that are in no rule.

## 2. Objections, most important first
1. CH-0139 should-fix: MCP-Atlas card says "paper v3" but takes its open-weight range (12-34%) and judge figure (78%) from v1; v3 reports GLM-5.1 at 75.6%, a three-judge design, and a public-private gap (+3.8 pp for open models) the card omits.
2. CH-0140 should-fix: floor and ceiling are named as a bias toward "nearly the same" (s1.4) but are only a scored property; the open-weight range column is older-generation models, not the route's.
3. CH-0148 should-fix: P1's seed hit rate h is set by the history generator; the seed rule needs tool-call records that most candidates lack; ATRBench (the named closest design) needs a provider-layer change and closed-model simulators; effective n is users.
4. CH-0142 should-fix: "paired arms put scorer error on both arms" is false where the arms differ in call patterns (BFCL penalises redundant calls; MCP-Atlas judges the final response only; tau2 drops malformed calls).
5. CH-0141 should-fix: R4's preference for deterministic scorers is in none of its cited sources, and s6's own audit figures do not show the deterministic class to be more reliable.
6. CH-0143 should-fix: s6 verdicts mean "the wording is on the page"; the closing reading turns heterogeneous audit measures, one with a stopping rule and 5-15 items per category, into "one in ten to one in two", over a selected set of audited benchmarks.
7. CH-0145 should-fix: SWE-bench Verified and Terminal-Bench are triaged out of every item for lacking a tool catalogue, which P4's own requirements do not need.
8. CH-0147 should-fix: P3 H1 (equivalent-alternative gold, basis by extension only), P3 H3 (a V5 harness rule), P1 H5 (conflates memory benchmarks with tool seeding), P5 H4 (licence undefined) are not necessary safeguards as written.
9. CH-0149 should-fix: E2E reports bill per turn but does not require more than one user turn or define a turn; 13 of 22 cards are single-prompt tasks; no held-out reserve requirement.
10. CH-0156 should-fix: no rule for multiple comparisons, judge self-enhancement and the judge replacement problem, route drift between paired runs, or combining stage results into a claim about the package.
11. CH-0154 should-fix: R1, R3, R7, R9 and R5 say more than their sources; the Verdict column reads as verification of the rule.
12. CH-0152 should-fix: the properties s1.4 says drive the bill (tokens per definition, tool-result size, turns, volatile content) and the human's "prerequisite dependencies" are not card columns.
13. CH-0150 should-fix: task counts are treated as independent units; the cluster unit is not a field; the accuracy and the bill-ratio sample questions are not separated.
14. CH-0151 should-fix: "version-locked" heading is not met by most rows; the tau2, MCPMark and MCP-Atlas criticisms were measured on other versions.
15. CH-0146 should-fix: triage mixes unassessed with out of scope, uses the synthetic-data criterion unevenly (ATRBench kept), gives an unconfirmed licence reason for MetaTool, and gives EvoMemBench a line.
16. CH-0144 should-fix: provenance labels use two standards (absence of tie gives I, outside knowledge gives X); several I labels belong to reporters with their own repositories or to anonymous sources.
17. CH-0153 note: sample counts written as totals, systematic block sample, inspected rows not logged against the held-out rule.
18. CH-0155 note: the reason for demoting "independently evaluated" is contradicted by s6 and partly speculation (the conclusion is within the human's text).
19. CH-0157 note: four statements beyond the pages (cache-accounting sentence, MPT licence, ATRBench "no time", MCP-Bench "ceiling").

## 5. Alternatives the register did not choose, and what would favour them
- *Constructed options for P3 and P5:* favoured if no candidate has definitions files (MCP-Atlas, MCPVerse, Toolathlon have none; AppWorld's are non-MCP) and if scorers behave differentially across call patterns (CH-0142).
- *A coding benchmark for P4:* favoured if its tool-result sizes and turns are measured and a scorer check passes (CH-0145); against: Epoch's "Flawed" verdict.
- *EvoMemBench CrossEp-Tool or a constructed history for P1:* favoured by H1 (recurrence) if call records exist or can be added with a documented method (CH-0146, CH-0148); against: BFCL-sized catalogue [U].
- *Established, scrutinised benchmarks over young ones for E2E:* favoured by s6 itself, which shows documented defects and fixed versions (tau2-Verified, Toolathlon-Verified, MCPMark Verified) against unknown error rates in the youngest (CH-0155).
- *A deterministic-scorer preference:* would be favoured by an audit that applies one protocol to both scorer types and finds lower error for deterministic ones; none was found in the stored material.

## 6. Preserved disagreements (none yet answered)
No proposer or lead response has been recorded to any challenge (0 of 2 rounds used). Positions as I hold them:
- The register treats floor/ceiling as a scored property; I hold it must be a hard requirement for accuracy constructs, measured on a baseline pilot on the route's models (CH-0140). The lead may hold that a baseline pilot is itself an outcome look; my answer is that it is an outcome of the baseline arm on disjoint items, which s1.1 allows.
- The register asserts that pairing handles scorer error; I hold it does not when arms differ in calls (CH-0142).
- The register prefers deterministic scorers (R4); I hold the cited sources and s6 do not support the preference (CH-0141).
- The register keeps ATRBench as the closest P1 design; I hold its route fit and tool-record gaps must be on the card first (CH-0148).

## Overall view and uncertainty
Not a recommendation for or against any benchmark. The register is a good inventory when its cells are read with their version labels; the parts that would mislead a ranking are the stale MCP-Atlas range, the floor/ceiling treatment, the scorer rule and the pairing sentence, the P1 requirements, and the SWE-bench/Terminal-Bench triage for P4. Severity is should-fix, not blocking, because sections 7-10 can still absorb all of them.
