## What remains uncertain or only answerable by an experiment
- Whether any restriction pays in billed cost under caching (HYP-0005), and whether a second stage pays (HYP-0002): no measurement in the extracts; the nearest measurements (Toollery via the S4I erratum, EXT-0053, ComplexMCP) are single-step, single-model or ±8-point; they neither support nor refute a cascade.
- Whether models initiate discovery when a needed tool is absent (HYP-0004): every extract on non-use concerns visible tools (EXT-0047/0048); the vendor statements (EXT-0010 "trained to search those surfaces", EXT-0009) are unmeasured.
- Who initiates re-retrieval in FitText: the extracts give an agent-emitted pseudo-tool description (EXT-0050/0051) and a system-side similarity condition (EXT-0052); section 4.2 has not been read.
- The size of the replay, reachability and turn-budget effects on any recovery estimate on the closest vehicle (S4G section 2; EXT-0042, EXT-0053).

- None of this decides between the alternatives.

## Overall view and uncertainty
The proposals have moved from a design-shaped structure towards a hypothesis class with explicit comparators and falsifiers; my remaining objections are mostly consistency and one dispute about wording that would, if left, present vendor-shipped forms as following from the record. The strongest unresolved scientific points are (i) the system-run cascade is not written out as a comparator (16), (ii) the FitText trigger evidence is incomplete (17), and (iii) the evidence base for the staging conclusion is the extracts chosen, not the literature (bounded negative). This is not a recommendation to adopt anything; adoption belongs to the human.
