## 1. Verdict
The record is honest about its own limits in many places (it corrects the scouts in s2, states that the challenger step and the scouts' reports are outstanding, and does not change V5-R10 or V5-R11). The counts add up and the numbers I traced are on the pages. The weaknesses are of framing and attribution: the tally reads as support for the ideas when it measures how faithfully scouts read pages; one "measured" result is true by construction in the source; the BR-6 gains are bundled pipelines; the bill-term paragraph rests on an uninterpretable table; "copyable" and "no source contradicts" go further than the pages. Twelve challenges (CH-0115..CH-0126): 7 should-fix, 5 note, none blocking. The direction of the bias is toward keeping the ideas, but mostly by omission and by wording, not by false numbers.

## 3. Objections (one line each)
1. CH-0115 should-fix: "partly" is defined narrowly; at least 14 "confirmed" rows carry the same defects; the tally measures reading fidelity, not support; lead-checked rows have no SUM.
2. CH-0116 should-fix: "No checked source contradicts keeping them" has no test; X9's authors prefer the no-JIT variant; vendor's offsetting claims and the EX-4/BR-6 coupling omitted.
3. CH-0117 should-fix: closed-world "measured, 322 rejected" is by construction in the paper; false-reject rate not independently measured.
4. CH-0118 should-fix: BR-6 gains are ensemble or online-LLM pipelines (Toolshed over BM25; Re-Invoke intent extractor; EasyTool Success vs Pass); offline assumption partly contradicted.
5. CH-0119 note: "semantic dilution" quoted as a harm; the paper calls the effect asymmetric and beneficial.
6. CH-0120 should-fix: Speakeasy bill-term table's token columns do not reconcile; one preliminary vendor run; "not monotone" from two points.
7. CH-0121 should-fix: "copyable" pattern is server-side; no stored client implementation on Chat Completions; appending to the tools array rewrote 65% on GLM-5.3; proposal (d) omits the probe.
8. CH-0122 should-fix: absence claims have no recorded queries; steward's not-stored list (Anthropic caching page, GitHub issues, BFCL multi-turn) dropped; MCP-Zero "dense only" stated as fact.
9. CH-0123 note: "one small sample" and the MemTool range exceed what the steward found.
10. CH-0124 note: the 44-57% merges two measures (one synthetic, author benchmark); the 347/2,120 figure and the 0.43-0.56 conversion for the 0.4 are omitted.
11. CH-0125 note: abstention score is a joint score; BFCL 48% is not about irrelevance; ScaleMCP 0.02-0.07 has no single baseline.
12. CH-0126 note: source mix (preprints, vendors, own benchmarks) not characterised; scout prompts named the supporting works; no contrary-evidence sweep beyond the scouts' reports.
