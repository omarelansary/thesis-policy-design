## 5. "Depends mainly on searches per session, little on price" (Q5)
At T = 10 the printed grid supports it (D = 10: r moves n* at most 1.4x at fixed w, o; D moves it about 5x). At r = 0.02 the o-spread is real (47-77) but it is within the same band. It is **not** supported for T = 3 (r alone about 2x at D = 10; the section's own bullet 2 says the direction reverses), the maxima in reading 3 come from a cell with more searches than requests, and the seed claim fails at K = 1 for T >= 30 ("never" vs 0.54). CH-0109, CH-0110. The "no dependence on model" consequence additionally leaves out minimum cacheable length, lifetime, d, q and hit rate (CH-0111).

- "No published break-even found": no 27 Oct query log is stored; `search-log-20261022.md` L20 disclaims its own absence claims; Anthropic's rule and the keepalive I_max are nearer than stated (CH-0112).
