## 2. What I tried to break and could not
1. **The 4-tool counterexample** (errata section 3). By hand: savings `s = keep - disc` are 18, 89, 14, 3; total `disc` 111; all deferred has `M = 23 > 18`. Rule as written: Step 1 keeps none (every `keep > disc`); Step 2 index `m/keep` = 0.143, 0.053, 0.348, 0.073, so `t2` moves, `M = 15 <= 18`, cost `111 - 9 + 23 = 125`. Incremental index `m/s` = 0.167, 0.056, 0.571, 2.33: `t3` moves, `M = 16`, cost `111 - 93 + 96 = 114`. Exhaustive check on subsets with `sum m >= 5` (feasible cores): `{t3}` costs 114; `{t1}` 111 + 89 = 200; `{t2}` 125; `{t0,t3}` 132; `{t0,t2}` 143; every other feasible subset adds at least these savings, so none is cheaper than `{t3}`. Counterexample correct. [I]
2. **Correction 1 algebra.** `w_adm = (delta - l^N) / (l^M - l^N)` and `w_C = dc^N / (dc^N - dc^M)` re-derived; correct under the stated conditions (edge cases in CH-0044). The dimensional repair (two estimands, no exchange rate, lexicographic F1) is sound and answers AUTH-006 item 1.
3. **Correction 5.** The two-parameter fit and its out-of-sample checks reproduced from the Table 15 values (CH-0044 for the numbers). No remaining sentence in 01, 02 or 03 treats 0.0088 as a price. Adequate.
4. **Correction 2 conclusions.** `Y_ref - Y_CA <= P(Y_ref=1, Y_CA=0) <= M + I` is correct (CH-0036 says why it needs no assumptions). Withdrawing `epsilon := delta` is right.
5. **Correction 4's direction** at the 10,000-tool scale: I could not find a reason the reference would fit; I attack the generality and the asymmetry, not the direction.
6. **Info-class audit of R1, R3, R4, End:** no use of gold tools, annotations or reference trajectories at run time; I found none. R2, R5: legitimate if selectors and rankers are tuned on a split disjoint from evaluation; "scripted queries" for recall (CH-0041) are the only artefact route found.

## 3. The questions the lead asked, answered plainly
- **Is G a policy or an evaluation protocol dressed as a policy?** Both, separately: R1-R6 is a run-time mechanism (essentially C-A's A2-A5 plus two optional stages); D1-D4 is an offline arm-selection protocol. D4 is **experimental arm selection / hyperparameter tuning**, a legitimate deployment procedure but not a policy decision node; it is not a learned selection rule because nothing about it is trained or tested across settings. It restores the N0 issue that RV-0003 settled by keeping arm selection outside the policy (CH-0039).
- **Does G answer the supervisor's three needs?** Conditionally. Context (request-level) exists only with R2; staged narrowing only with R2/level 2; reintroduction by R4-R5 is the model's search. The "stage count is an output" reading makes any design satisfy the requirement; REQ-O14-12 is "unclear" in the register, so this is not a violation, but 02 section 1's claim of answering needs reads stronger than the realised configuration at large catalogue (search-only).
- **Did G invent a stage, threshold or benefit?** No numeric threshold, no empirical benefit asserted (checked: none found). Stages R2, R2 level 2 and R6 come from AUTH-004 candidates and are conditional. Items imported without support: the level-2 inequality (from DPR-0002, symbols undefined, recall term dropped); "bounds only" for EXT-0063/0064 overstates (it adds turns, not a capability); the claim that G is "not a family with open slots" is false by its own table (CH-0041).
- **Perverse optima under the token objective:** CH-0042 (tail dilution is the main one; package A's F7 flagged it and F1 does not guard it).
- **Strongest rival to G on the primary criterion [I, not a design preference]:** a small hand-picked core plus the vendor's own search (the 3-5 non-deferred tools, EXT-0009 l.31) with add-only discovery: lowest component count, no selector, no tournament; G's added content over it (R2, R6, D4) has no stored evidence of paying (03 admits this in section 4). At small catalogues the rival is expose-all in the regime where the tool block is not dominant; at catalogues where tail mass is below `delta` it is a static core without recovery. **Experiment that would separate them** (none authorised): per arm, complete-trajectory tokens and success on a disjoint test set, stratified by trajectory length and by tool tail mass; initiation rate of the model when a needed tool is absent (HYP-0010); selector recall at the same `kappa`; the D4 procedure's pick against each setting's best arm in held-out settings.

## 4. Was C-A dismissed too fast, or kept too long?
Neither clearly. Demoting C-A as principal for the **beyond-window regime** is defensible on correction 4 (subject to CH-0038 scope and symmetry). But the reasons given (heuristic ordering, known pattern, no safety property) apply to G's D2 and to the vendor pattern, so they do not favour G over C-A; they favour a portfolio. C-A retains one thing G lacks: it is a *rule from named inputs to a surface* that passed the N0 test and 19 challenges (read the STATE caveat on concession), while G has had none. I do not argue for C-A; I record that 03 does not show G ahead of it.

## 5. My objections that may be overstated
- **CH-0035 point 1 magnitude** ("factor of about four") rests on an assumed 20,000-token context [S]; the structural omission is not in doubt, the factor is. Point 4 (`tau` correlation) and 5 (unreached-case searches) may be second-order.
- **CH-0036 point 5** (rule-of-three sum): the lead may reply that D2 skips the core at large `n`, so A3 is moot there. True for the large-catalogue branch; the point stands for the regime where D2 runs but then `n_u / N` is smaller. I rate it conditional.
- **CH-0037 point 1** relies on my memory of min-knapsack approximation results, not on the record; grade minor accordingly.
- **CH-0039** is decisive against the *label*; the lead can resolve it by relabelling, so "decisive" does not mean the package is unusable. AUTH-006 itself asks for a "testable conditional decision", which G supplies in form.
- **CH-0040 point 1** assumes arm truths where cheap arms are likelier inferior; reasonable but a simulation could show a modest inflation.
- **CH-0042 points 2, 3, 5** rest on no data about the trajectory shape; they are risks, not findings. Point 6 is a single natural-replay cell with an interval including zero (RV-0003 quotes EXT-0061), so "can raise billed cost" is a possibility, not an established regime.
- **CH-0043 point 5** is about status and tone; the lead states that its drafts are unreviewed.
