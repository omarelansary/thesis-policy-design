- Method: each Stage 2 pivotal claim was handed to a **fresh Opus 5.5 High reviewer** as a bounded statement with source texts and location hints, with Stage 2 assessments, challenger reports and corrections withheld (`audits/stage3-handoff-20261012/`). The coordinator spot-checked 18 reviewer-cited passages and numbers against the stored texts by deterministic search (all confirmed) and checked the hashes of the 70 stored texts used (0 mismatches; 9 carry extraction warnings).

"Promoted" claims are the wording in column 2. A location containing the same number is not support; the reviewers checked setup, population, comparison and who produced each figure.
- Epistemic labels: F fact reported by a source at its stated verification; O observation; I interpretation; U uncertainty; S speculation. Locations are `text_id p.N L.x` in the stored text unless noted.
