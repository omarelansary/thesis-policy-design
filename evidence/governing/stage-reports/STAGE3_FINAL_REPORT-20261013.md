## 7. Methodological limits (S3-3)
Mixed measurement bases (projected vs measured, list-price reconstruction vs billed, local tokenizer vs provider); single runs and no uncertainty in most filtering comparisons; oracle or hand-specified filters in the strongest positive results; synthetic and mocked environments; model-, harness- and provider-specific effects; vendor self-reporting; prices and tokenizers that change by model (Claude 4.7+ ≈ +30% tokens); candidate sets of 2–5 tools for reliance studies; ToolTweak in-sample optimisation; benchmark evaluators with large variance; extraction layout errors in many PDFs; reviewers' partial reads; bounded corpus; the Stage 2 search caps. Claim wording was given to reviewers, and reviewers were instances of the same model family as the coordinator.

## 8. Provenance and reproducibility findings (S3-4)
- Integrity: 70 stored texts used; sha256 of text and source files match manifests for all; 9 manifests carry extraction warnings (replacement characters or near-empty pages).
- Versions and identity: arXiv version/title differences (Tool Preferences v2 vs "Gaming Tool Preferences"; Imagine Before Acting vs record title); RACK's 2016 date not established (arXiv v1 2018); ToolBench size reported as 47k (ToolGen) vs 16,464 APIs (others); pinned commit for Executor; HTML/markdown vendor pages are dynamic-content tag-strips with no first-publication dates, and the HTML tag-stripper removed angle-bracket content from the Executor TypeScript source.
- Artifact availability: code released for MCP-SafetyBench, the scaffolding study and Tool Preferences; restricted-access for Shi; "upon acceptance" for ToolTweak and several skill papers; "will release" for the validity audit; no link found for ShareLock and SciAgentGym.
- Reproducibility hazards: proxy-instrumented harnesses may alter the feature under test (Claude Code fallback); dollar results are time-bound (pricing, cache multipliers, tokenizers); judge-pipeline differences (the validity audit's rerun used a non-recommended judge); stochastic outputs with single runs.
- Process provenance: all reviewer reports saved verbatim; handoff builder and hashes preserved; the Stage 2 logs, audits and freeze manifest unchanged; Stage 3 follow-ups logged separately (`searches/stage3`).
