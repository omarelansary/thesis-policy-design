The default OpenBench scorer performs two stochastic LLM calls for each task. First,
identify_key_points(task) regenerates evaluation criteria from the task text. Second,
grader_model.generate(...) evaluates the trajectory and final response against the gener-
ated criteria and returns a success or failure verdict. Human-authored step annotations are used only
as a fallback when key-point generation fails; under normal execution, they do not determine the
rubric.

This design introduces two sources of evaluator variability. First, regenerated key points can differ in
specificity and strictness across runs, causing the same underlying behavior to be assessed against
different criteria. Second, the final LLM verdict is itself stochastic and can flip on borderline traces.
