<<<BEGIN-VERBATIM

Not pre-registered. Reproduce: `uv run python scripts/cr_reconcile_e811.py`.

E8.11 and this report both put `qwen/qwen3.7-max` under `--pool full
--architecture rag --rag-k 8` over "150 tasks, 50/50/50 by chain depth, seed 0"
drawn from the same released 750-task slice, and they print different numbers:
**36.7 retrieval against 57.3 curated** there, **30.7 against 54.0** here. Two
tables of the same condition in one paper is exactly the kind of thing a reviewer
finds first, so it is worth settling which is which.

The curated column decides it. Both reports read curated verdicts from the same
released file, `leaderboard_api/verdicts/qwen3.7-max.jsonl` — no model is
run to produce them. Identical task sets would therefore give identical curated
rates. 57.3 ≠ 54.0, so **the two draws are different task sets**, despite both
recipes reading "seed 0". E8.11's shards are git-ignored and no longer on disk, so
the two sampling implementations cannot be diffed directly; what can be checked is
whether the difference matters.

The committed `manifests/subsets/cr150.jsonl` is exactly `cr_subset.py(seed=0)`
(verified by rebuild), and its curated rate is 54.0 — this report's slice is
reproducible from the committed script. Redrawing the balanced 150 from the same
750-task pool 2000 times places both figures inside the ordinary spread:

| quantity | value |
|---|---|
| mean curated over 2000 balanced redraws | 57.0 |
| sd | 3.51 |
| 95% of draws fall in | [50.0, 63.3] |
| e8.11's 57.3 sits at percentile | 50.2 |
| e9.1's 54.0 sits at percentile | 22.6 |
| separation | 3.3 points = **0.94 sd** |

Under a point of separation. Neither table is anomalous; e8.11 drew almost exactly
the median slice and this report drew a mildly harder-than-typical one, which is
worth knowing when reading e9.1's absolute rates — they run low against the whole
750-task slice (58.4 curated) largely because the 50/50/50 balance over-weights the
long bucket (44.2 curated) relative to its natural share.

The quantity each report actually claims is the paired deficit, and it is insensitive
to draw difficulty:

| report | retrieval | curated | deficit |
|---|---|---|---|
| e8.11 | 36.7 | 57.3 | **−20.6** |
| e9.1 | 30.7 | 54.0 | **−23.3** |

**2.7 points apart.** So the two tables do not contradict each other: they are
different draws from one slice, and the finding — an open catalog at k=8 costs this
model roughly twenty points — replicates across both. The paper must nonetheless
**caption the two tables with their distinct slices** rather than presenting either
rate as *the* number, and should quote the deficit, not the rate, whenever the two
are compared.
