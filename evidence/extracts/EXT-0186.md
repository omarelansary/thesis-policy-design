4.2 RQ1: Do Official Labels Match Human Judgments?

Table 3 reports evaluator–human agreement across the four benchmark families. Among 496 audited
tasks, 92 official labels disagree with expert judgments, yielding an aggregate misalignment rate of
18.5%. The error rate ranges from 9.8% on τ 2-Bench Retail to 30.5% on LiveMCPBench. BFCL v4
exhibits a 20.0% error rate, while MCP-Atlas exhibits a 13.5% error rate.
