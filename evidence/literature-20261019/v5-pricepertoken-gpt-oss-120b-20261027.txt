GPT OSS 120b is priced at $0.030 per million input tokens and $0.100 per million output tokens. It has a context window of 131,072 tokens. It is available from 25 providers, with AkashML the cheapest at $0.030 per million input tokens (input prices range from $0.030 to $0.350). It runs at roughly 265 tokens per second with a 0.47s time to first token. On benchmarks, GPT OSS 120b scores Intelligence 10.2 (43rd percentile), Coding 21.2 (31st percentile), Math 66.7 (64th percentile), MMLU Pro 77.5 (59th percentile).
