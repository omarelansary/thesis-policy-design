# Title: "No official page lists r = 1.0" is literally true but hides that several official pages list no cache price, which is the no-discount case in practice

**Objection.** `11` s2 says "No official page stored lists r = 1.0" and s6 says "The r = 1.0 case ... has no official page behind it"; `13` s2 repeats "no official page at 1.0" and says the aggregator's CoreWeave r = 1.0 is "unsupported by the provider's page". The stored official pages show something different from "the case is unsupported":
(a) Fireworks' own page says that for "any text or vision model not listed individually" the size-based prices "apply uniformly to input and output (no separate cached-input rate)". That is a provider statement of no cache discount (and of o = 1.0, see CH-0106).
(b) For gpt-oss-120b, five stored official pages list no cache-hit price at all: CoreWeave (Cache hit "-"), Together (no cached line), DeepInfra, Baseten, Cerebras (FND-0043 A12, A13, lead addendum). Silence is not r = 1.0, but it is the case a V5 bill would meet if `cached_tokens` is billed as input on such a host.
(c) Artificial Analysis treats a missing cache price as the input price. I recomputed its blended prices with the 7:2:1 weights: Cerebras (7 x 0.35 + 2 x 0.35 + 0.75) / 10 = 0.39, which is the page's figure; CoreWeave (7 x 0.03 + 2 x 0.03 + 0.17) / 10 = 0.044, shown as 0.04. Both are reproduced only with cache price = input price. With a cache price of zero CoreWeave would show 0.02.
So the no-discount extreme is not an "aggregator-only" case; it is what the official pages of the cheapest gpt-oss-120b hosts imply, and gpt-oss-120b is the model the university route serves. Reading "official range 0.02 to 0.50" as the range V5 faces understates the upper end.

**Evidence.** `v5-lit-fireworks-pricing-20261027.txt` L90 ("no separate cached-input rate"), L92-97; `v5-lit-wandb-inference-pricing-20261027.txt` L346-358 (GPT OSS 120B, Cache hit "-"); `v5-lit-together-pricing-20261027.txt` (gpt-oss-120B row, no "(cached)" line; FND-0043 A12); `v5-aa-gpt-oss-120b-providers-20261027.txt` L49 ("CoreWeave $0.04"), L65, L465 (Cerebras $0.39); Cerebras $0.35 / $0.75 from `v5-lit-cerebras-gptoss-20261027.txt` L36. Not checked: how AA treats "-" is my inference from the arithmetic [I], not a statement on the page.

**What would resolve it.** Reword the r row and s6: "r = 0.02 to 0.50 on hosts that list a cache price; hosts that list none (Fireworks other base models, CoreWeave/Together/DeepInfra/Baseten/Cerebras for gpt-oss-120b) imply r = 1.0 if hits are billed as input; whether they discount is not stated." Keep r = 1.0 in D-04 and say it is supported by absence of a listed discount, not unsupported. Remove "unsupported by the provider's page" for CoreWeave or add that the page is silent.
