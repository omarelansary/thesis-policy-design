# Title: correction 1 and correction 5 are right; residual points (strata undefined in core-less G, dominance by points, orphaned from D4, multiplier wording)

**Grade: MINOR. I recomputed the algebra and the Table 15 fit and found them correct (RV-0004 section 2).**

**Objection.** (design/package-v3/01-errata-AUTH-005.md sections 1 and 5; 02.)
1. **Strata after the core is skipped.** The pre-treatment partition of package-v2/03 section 2 is defined by tasks whose required set lies outside a dev-frequency core of a named size. In G at large scale the core stage is skipped (no need statistics), so "miss" has no definition; `w`, `l^s`, `w_adm` and `w_C` cannot be computed. The errata should say how M and N are defined when no core exists, or restrict correction 1 to the core-present regime. As written correction 1 does not connect to any decision in G (D4 does not use strata or `w_hat`).
2. **Dominance on point estimates.** "`S` dominates `M` at `w_hat` iff `DeltaY >= 0` and `DeltaC <= 0` with one strict" is a statement about true values; on estimates it needs intervals (noninferiority on `Y`, a strict bound on `C`). "Intervals are carried through" appears for the F1 rule but not here.
3. **`w_adm` edge cases.** Correct for `l^M > l^N`. If `l^N > delta` the formula gives a negative `w_adm` and recovery is needed everywhere; if `l^M <= l^N` the direction reverses (inadmissible for `w < w_adm`). Only the first is stated.
4. **Multiplier (correction 5).** Adequate. One residual: "reproduces the six cells ... within 2%" counts as six confirmations, but two of the six cells define the two parameters (the cache-suppressed full-library row sets the base; the forced-warm full-library row sets `r`), and the cache-suppressed Toollery row is the base price times tokens, so it is nearly a restatement. Independent checks: natural full-library (2.5% from the warm estimate), forced-warm Toollery, natural Toollery. Say "three out-of-sample cells" rather than six reproduced. The 0.144 hit rate in the Toollery natural row is itself unexplained in the stored text.
5. **Dangling references.** 01 section 0 points to `04-objections-and-experiments.md` and `05`, which do not exist yet; 03 cites "S1 of G" (see CH-0043).
