# Title: Topic B is presented one-sidedly: the lead redefines the relevant condition as "channel plus scope statement" and then discounts the only evidence under that condition as "instructed"

**Grade: SERIOUS (internal inconsistency in the argument; affects what pilot 2 is for).**

**Objection.**
1. `05` section 2.1 says the relevant condition for initiation is "with the discovery tool and our scope statement ... since that is what a deployment (and MCP-Zero, and the vendors) provide". Section 1 row B then sets the published successes aside because the request is "**instructed**". Both cannot hold. If a provided channel plus instruction is the deployment condition, MCP-Zero (95.19 vs 69.23 for schema injection on APIBank, EXT-0101 lines 88-92), the vendor accuracy gain (Opus 4 49% -> 74% with tool search, EXT-0074 line 72, vendor-internal, no method) and SING (agent action set includes DISCOVER, SING:496-510; Global SR 45.1% vs 40.3% when only the ground-truth servers are given, steward SING:953-956) are the **closest evidence under that condition**. A success gain over a no-search baseline cannot arise without the model initiating searches at some rate. None reports the rate (so the gap is real), but `05` tilts the reader to "initiation is not a safe assumption" using KAIST and LOMEKWI, which are further from the condition, and does not mention the indirect evidence in the other direction.
2. The vendor sentence "Our models have primarily been trained to search those surfaces" (OpenAI tool-search page, EXT-0010 line 47) is relevant to initiation (training for the behaviour) and is absent from `05`.
3. "instructed" is shown for MCP-Zero (EXT-0101 lines 71-72, 140-142; the cookbook at 93-100 is advice to adopters, not the experiment's prompt). For vendor tool search the stored texts show a provided tool, not an instruction (EXT-0074, EXT-0007 lines 83-86, EXT-0010 line 33). `05` section 2.3 says both give "an instructed channel"; for the vendor this is [U].
4. Under the lead's reading the gap is narrower and sharper than stated: not "does it initiate", but "at what rate, for which models, with which scope statement, and when the target tool is truly absent from the catalogue" (see CH-0061). A balanced block would list: evidence for initiation under a provided channel (indirect, instructed or vendor-internal), evidence against (none directly; KAIST and LOMEKWI are different constructs), rate (absent).
5. SING is described as "discovery is a prescribed step" (`05` row B (v)). SING's formalism lets the agent choose DISCOVER, INVOKE or RESPOND at each step (SING:496-519), although the first discovery request is formed by a prescribed decomposition (SING:528-530). Whether the prompt tells the agent when to discover is in appendices I did not read [U]. "Prescribed" is stronger than the main text shows.

**Evidence.** As cited above. I did not read SING appendices, the MCP-Zero Section 3 prompt, or the Anthropic internal tests.

**What would resolve it.** Add to `05` a row "indirect evidence that models do initiate under a provided channel (success gain vs a no-search baseline): MCP-Zero, vendor, SING, with what each omits" and state explicitly that "instructed" is shown for MCP-Zero only. Drop or relabel the sentence about vendors giving an "instructed channel".
