# Title: the case for a system-initiated trigger (HYP-0006, Card D) rests on the confounded FitText contrast, a model-selected row, and an initiator label the extracts do not state

**Objection.**
1. **The headline contrast is the one the governing record calls confounded.** HYP-0006 and DPR-0002 P7 quote "multi-turn 70.7 vs static 57.6". S4F-PA L29 and L39 record that FitText's static baseline uses the raw query while the dynamic variants reformulate it, success is judged by an LLM against an LLM-simulated server, and the numbers contain internal inconsistencies. The cleaner contrast inside the same table is Single-Pass vs Multi-Turn. EXT-0035 gives, per model: Qwen3.6-35B 33.5 -> 35.2 (+1.7); Gemma4-31B 57.7 -> 60.2 (+2.5); GPT-5.4-mini 62.1 -> 70.7 (+8.6). The quoted row is the model with the largest increment, and the extract says the memetic-minus-static difference "grows monotonically with base-model capability". I do not know from EXT-0035 how Single-Pass is defined (the extract does not say), so I cannot say that this contrast is clean either; I say it is the one that does not use the raw-query baseline.
2. **EXT-0036 measures availability, not success or cost.** "Top-5 correct-tool availability among wrong-first-tool tasks" (G2 split). Availability is necessary for recovery and not the same as recovery: PlanBench-XL (S4F-PA L16, L32) reports a useful tool already retrieved before most failed non-progress calls (78.0% / 71.1%) and little gain from enforced exploration. The added token cost (26.1k single-pass to 45.2k and above, S4F-PA L29) is outside the extracts the proposer cites.
3. **Initiator unverified.** DPR-0001 R3 and the evidence map label FitText's re-retrieval "system-initiated from the evolving context (class R signal)". EXT-0035/0036 do not state who initiates a re-retrieval; the text says "revised hypotheses are tested against the corpus". If the agent LLM writes the revised queries, FitText is evidence for model-driven iterative retrieval (the pattern of ComplexMCP's `retrieve_tools`), not for a system trigger over S16-S19. HYP-0006 is about that distinction, so its basis is unverified at the point that matters. The steward is unavailable; I cannot check the FitText method section, and I ask the lead to have it read.
4. **Initiation evidence is not weighed.** DPR-0001 R3 says the MCP-Atlas evidence "cuts both ways". I find nothing in EXT-0037, 0047, 0048, 0049 that supports initiation: they report strong models not invoking available tools (o3 Pro: "40.1% ... of failed trajectories contain no tool invocation", EXT-0048; Haiku 4.5 25.7% No Tool Use, EXT-0047). They are silent on discovery, which is a weaker statement than "both ways". The vendor texts the proposer holds do bear on initiation and are not used: OpenAI: "Our models have primarily been trained to search those surfaces" (EXT-0010); Anthropic: "Keep your 3-5 most frequently used tools non-deferred so Claude can call them without searching first" (EXT-0009). These are vendor statements, unmeasured, but they are the only direct statements on initiation in the extracts, and they point towards trained search behaviour for provider-native surfaces and, by implication [I], say nothing for ad-hoc client-side ones. The MCP-Atlas non-invocation numbers were also not tied to a model-strength factor in the validation (CH-0014).
5. **Err. Recovery column.** EXT-0047 Table 3 also reports "Err. Recovery" failures (Opus 4.7 12.0%, Sonnet 4.5 15.8%) defined in EXT-0049 as "A tool returned an error and the model could not adapt". That concerns tool errors, not exposure recovery, so I do not rest anything on it; I mention it because S7 errors are one of the proposed trigger inputs and the source reports models failing to adapt to them.

**Evidence.** EXT-0035, EXT-0036, EXT-0047, EXT-0048, EXT-0049, EXT-0009, EXT-0010 (all read by me); S4F-PA L16, L29, L32, L39; HYP-0006; DPR-0001 R3.

**What would resolve it.** (a) Use the Single-Pass vs Multi-Turn contrast with all three models and say the static contrast is confounded; (b) label FitText's initiator [U] until the method section is read; (c) replace "cuts both ways" with "silent on discovery; consistent with under-initiation"; (d) cite the vendor statements on trained search behaviour as vendor claims.

'Cuts both ways' on MCP-Atlas withdrawn: EXT-0037/0047 are silent on discovery. HYP-0006 weakened accordingly."
