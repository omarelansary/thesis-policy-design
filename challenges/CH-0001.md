# Title: the objective's central term "unnecessary tool exposure" is never defined, and `c_tools` is rejected as a primary quantity by a non sequitur

**Objection.**
1. DPR-0001 quotes the working statement ("reducing unnecessary tool exposure ... preserving task performance ...") but then defines nothing for "unnecessary". Every formulation F1-F6 minimises `C(pi)` over the section-5 quantities; none minimises or constrains "unnecessary exposure". At least three non-equivalent definitions are available and they sit in different signal classes: (i) not required by any successful trajectory (needs success set or gold: class P at runtime, legitimate only in the scorer); (ii) not called in the run (class X, and policy-dependent: calls are conditional on `E`, so the denominator moves with the policy); (iii) the share of `c_vis` beyond some sufficient set (needs a definition of "sufficient"). The proposal must say which one the scorer uses and that it is evaluation-only; otherwise "unnecessary" can leak into a runtime rule through the back door (a policy that learns "tools like this are rarely called" is S10-class D and inherits (ii)'s policy-dependence).
2. "Alternatives considered" rejects "the objective as `c_tools` alone" because "the user states it is distinct from tokens and cost". AUTH-004 says the four quantities are distinct and that the formulation "remains a scientific choice requiring justification". Distinctness does not make `c_tools` non-primary. The supervisor's restatement of the research question (REQ-O14-01, E1) is "the most optimal way to reduce the count of tools while keeping the accuracy as high as possible ... We don't need to change it". DPR-0001's `basis` list omits REQ-O14-01, and REGISTER G.3 Q1 (does E1 restate or replace the token-based formulation) is open. By rejecting `c_tools` as primary the draft silently resolves Q1 in favour of tokens/cost. It may be the better resolution; it is not the proposer's to make, and it is not argued.

**Evidence.**
- DPR-0001, "Alternatives considered", second bullet (quoted above); section 6 formulations F1-F6 (no "unnecessary" term).
- `requirements/intake/2026-10-16-supervisor-meeting-2026-10-14-excerpts.md` E1 (INT-T-001): "most optimal way to reduce the count of tools while keeping the accuracy as high as possible. Okay, that's the research question. It's simple. We don't need to change it."
- `requirements/REGISTER.md` G.3 Q1 (open); REQ-O14-01 note: "Unit ("count of tools") differs from the earlier token-based wording".
- `ops/AUTHORIZATIONS.md` AUTH-004 working interpretations: "The exact optimization formulation remains a scientific choice requiring justification."
- Not checked: whether another agent's later DPR defines "unnecessary"; at the time of reading only DPR-0001 existed.

**What would resolve it.** (a) A stated definition of "unnecessary exposure" for the scorer, its signal class, and a sentence that no runtime component may use it; (b) `c_tools` kept as a live candidate primary quantity in section 6 (or an argued reason, citing evidence, for demoting it), and Q1 recorded as open in the DPR; (c) REQ-O14-01 added to the basis.
