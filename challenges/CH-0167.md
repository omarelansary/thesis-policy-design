# Title: Sessions and the R-large regime in the top E2E option are both constructed from a single-prompt benchmark, and the construction can favour the policy under test

**Objection.**
1. *Native evidence for the session axis is nil.* The bill is "per turn" averaged over sessions including returns after gaps (STATE; REQ-U21-10). AppWorld tasks are single-instruction tasks (one prompt, about 42-47 API calls; `v5-bm-b13` L739-741). E2E #1 and P1 #1 create sessions by chaining tasks "sharing apps or tools" and varying a recurrence grid. Whether AppWorld tasks of one chain can share a user (each task has its own supervisor and database in the stored README's task setup, L392 onward) is not checked [U]. If they cannot, the "returning user" is a sequence of unrelated worlds sharing a catalogue; recurrence is then what the experimenter sets. s7 states that results are "conditional on r", which is correct, but E2E #1 is offered as the primary vehicle for a headline claim ("one (AppWorld) for a claim limited to what AppWorld represents", L215), and what it represents with respect to returning users is nothing native.
2. *R-large padding.* E2E #1 reaches R-large "by labelled padding with captured real MCP definitions and size-matched constructed ones". Padding with definitions from unrelated domains or with synthetic names is easier for a search to discriminate than a catalogue with confusable tools; `04` itself records that first-stage retrieval is weak on realistic catalogues (ToolRet). The padding method therefore sets the quantity that decides whether deferral works at R-large (P3, and E2E accuracy). INT-T-021 s2 requires "documented construction methodology, and appropriate controls against bias" for constructed material; s7 states only "labelled". The bias runs in favour of the staged policy that this workspace designed, which is the "our own selection bias" of s1.4.
3. *Single-task variants for P3 #3.* The constructed single-need tasks (P3 #3) are said to have "gold by construction, an independent check of gold". Who checks (the same agent family, or a human) and on what sample is not stated (R7 says constructed variants may be easier).

**Evidence.** [F] `v5-bm-b13` L739-741; register L100, L103, L194, L211, L215; `04` R-mid/R-large rows. [I] the direction of the padding bias, based on the ToolRet point recorded in `04`; not measured.

**What would resolve it.** Add to E2E #1 and P1 #1 a bias control for padding (include confusable near-duplicates of the task's real tools, or report results separately for padding that is irrelevant and padding that is confusable), name who checks constructed gold and on what sample, and say that the E2E #1 session axis has no native evidence.
