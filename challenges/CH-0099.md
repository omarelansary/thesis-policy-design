# Title: 02 reports the staged-graph requirement as met by "six stages", though only three of them decide which tools are offered

**Objection.** 02 L14 answers REQ-O14F-06 (staged graph, one question per stage, open count, a filtered tool may come back) with "Six stages with a stated question and answerer (04 section 3)". The six in 04 L52-L57 are S1 (deployment filter), S2 (regime switch, once per deployment), M2 (memory-informed offering), D (discovery), H (history clearing) and W (memory write). H acts on history, W on memory at session end, S2 selects a prefix layout. Only S1, M2 and D narrow the tools. The supervisor's staged graph is a tool-filtering graph (REQ-O14F-05 gives 1,000 to 800 to 500 to 200 to 10). Both blind designs cut multi-step narrowing (01 L28), and 02's own row at L16 reports it as NONE FOUND and not adopted. So the requirement is met in a reduced form (two session-level filters, then on-demand search), and the row's wording hides that. The row at L30 (REQ-O14F-03, "the staged graph is your novelty") then calls the supervisor's assertion "Consistent" because no source measures the combination, while V5-R's staged part is the smallest part of the combination (and sits on the weakest negative, CH-0090).
Smaller: 02 L9 says the memory is "a 3-4 line summary". REQ-O21-03 says "a short summary"; "3-4 lines" comes from blind A (01 L25) and is not the supervisor's. 04 L3 says "No parameter is given a value" but W (L45) and the diagram carry "3-4 lines".

**Evidence.** 02 L9, L14, L16, L30; 04 L45, L52-L57; 01 L28; REGISTER L76 (REQ-O21-03), L88-L89.

**What would resolve it.** In row L14 say "met in reduced form: S1, M2 and D filter; S2, H and W are not filtering stages; multi-step narrowing is not adopted", and move the claim of "six stages" out. Mark "3-4 lines" as a blind-design suggestion or remove the number.
