# Title: "no extract shows a second stage pays" is a statement about the extraction set, and its one piece of positive evidence (EXT-0045/0046) tests a different structure that equally counts against the proposer's own comparators P3 and P4

**Objection.** The proposer asks whether its conclusion is overread. Yes, in three ways.
1. **EXT-0045 is not a filtering cascade.** It describes "JIT schema loading": a compact tool list, then the full schema of a selected tool is loaded ("When the required tool is not selected initially, its full schema is unavailable during argument generation"). That is graded exposure with an agent-driven upgrade, the pattern of P4 and in part P3, not a retrieve-then-rank funnel over candidates. Its recall cost therefore bears on the simpler comparators the proposer offers as non-straw, not (only) on cascades. DPR-0002 section D uses it only against cascades; P4's own "Failure handling: a tool never requested is never usable" is the same defect, and is not tied to EXT-0045.
2. **The source's own placement of the staged step is omitted.** EXT-0046 (which the proposer's log lists as not re-read) continues: Combined "represents a more aggressive deployment operating point when the static tool bank itself is also constrained. The latter is particularly helpful with a large number of tools that could not be otherwise supported, and low-latency requirement settings." That is the authors' stated regime for the staged step, and it is the large-`n` regime the proposer elsewhere says it has no data for. The metric in the source is tool-invocation F1 under an LLM simulator with a 3B-class backbone (S4F-PA L59), not task success.
3. **Extraction coverage.** The area (d) extracts are skills (EXT-0039-0041), Speakeasy (EXT-0023), and the on-device paper (EXT-0045/0046). Staged-selection evidence that sits in the governing records, readable without new extraction, was not weighed: Enrich-Retrieve-Rank's measured retrieval x rerank decomposition (S4F-PA L15); ITR's ablation of re-ranker and hybrid retrieval, and its 74/57/45 vs 84/82/76 tools-correct (S4F-PA L17 and L60; no intervals, "deltas inconsistent with the overall gain", worker-reported); MCP-Zero's hierarchical embedding routing (STAGE4_PRIOR_ART_COMPARISON L22, worker-reported, needle test only); DynamicMCPBench Appendix N `rag` vs `hier` vs `flat` at 150 tasks (S4G L72; the `hier` class is "one-server hierarchical exposure", vehicle report L55; results not in the records I read). None shows a stage paying net of cost; several are confounded. But "no extract supports a second filtering stage" (DPR-0003, Alternatives) and "the evidence does not show that any second filtering stage pays" (DPR-0002 section D) read as findings about the literature, when they are findings about EXT-0007..0049. DPR-0002 section F says "not found in EXT-0007..0049", which is the right scope; sections D and DPR-0003 should say the same.

**Evidence.** EXT-0045, EXT-0046 (read in full by me); DPR-0002 sections B (F4), C (P4), D, F; DPR-0003 Card B and Alternatives; S4F-PA L15, L17, L59, L60 (read); STAGE4_PRIOR_ART_COMPARISON L22 (read, worker-reported); S4G L72, vehicle report L55.
