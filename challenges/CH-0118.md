# Title: The BR-6 "gains" are results of bundled pipelines (several with a query-time LLM step), not of enriched searchable text alone

**Objection.** BR-6 is "enrich each tool's searchable text offline while showing the model only the compact text". `14` lists as "gains": Re-Invoke 0.6522 to 0.7821, Toolshed 46%, 56%, 47%, EasyTool 15.0 to 52.8. None isolates the idea:
(a) Toolshed: the 46/56/47 are "absolute improvements over BM25" of the whole "ensemble-based Advanced RAG-Tool Fusion" with pre-retrieval, intra-retrieval planning and post-retrieval reranking (`v5-xp-arxiv-2410.14594.txt` L204-209; L752-760). Against the other published system, Re-Invoke, the paper reports +5 (ToolE single) and +9 (multi) points (L756-757). `14` omits the BM25 baseline and the ensemble.
(b) Re-Invoke: 0.6522 is plain dense retrieval, 0.7821 the full pipeline (text-bison, ToolE single-tool column; `v5-xp-arxiv-2408.01875.txt` L595, L609). The pipeline has an intent extractor that works "during online inference" with an LLM (L287, L531), so part of the gain costs a model call per query, which is the opposite of "offline". Its ablation table (L900-904) exists and was not used. Other Re-Invoke rows are 0.7705 and 0.7770; `14` quotes the maximum.
(c) EasyTool: 15.0 to 52.8 is ChatGPT "Success" averaged over ToolBench I2 and I3 (DFSDT versus DFSDT-EasyTool; `v5-xp-arxiv-2401.06201.txt` L707-720). On the same rows "Pass" rises only from 62.3 to 69.8 (L712, L719). EasyTool rewrites the documentation that the model reads, which is compact model-facing text, not enriched hidden text; it bears on EX-2 more than on BR-6. SUM-0053 states the RestBench 103-token figure and the table numbers 3/4/5 were not located.
(d) `14` says "no checked paper reports the cost of generating the enriched text, so 'one-time offline cost' remains an assumption". Given (b), the stored text shows that at least one enrichment pipeline has an online LLM cost; the record should say the offline assumption is not just unsupported but partly contradicted for Re-Invoke.

**Evidence.** As cited. Steward notes: SUM-0053 Z3, Z4; SUM-0055 none. Lead check Z1 in `14` s3 BR-6.

**What would resolve it.** Name the baseline and the bundle for each number (Toolshed over BM25 as an ensemble; Re-Invoke with its online intent extractor; EasyTool on Success versus Pass), show the Re-Invoke ablation rows, and state that the offline-only part of the idea has no isolated stored measurement. Move EasyTool to EX-2 or label it.
