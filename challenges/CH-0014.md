# Title: the validation plan conditions on post-treatment strata, has no headroom or model-strength arm, and lists the vehicle threats without the datum in the record that bears on one of them

**Objection.**
1. **Strata are post-treatment.** N2's test is "recall measured scorer-side against U1; report by stratum (miss / no-miss)"; HYP-0003's falsifier uses "the stratum of tasks where nothing needed was removed". DPR-0001 R1 defines U1 through "a successful reference or successful run", so "needed" depends on the run, and "miss" depends on what N2 removed. Comparing arms within these strata conditions on a variable the treatment affects. It is defensible for the recovery ablation (the same N2 output in both arms, so the stratum is fixed by `E_1`), and not for comparisons across N2, P0, P1, P3 (P0 has no misses by definition). On the replay vehicle the "needed" tool is the single reference route, so "miss" means "the reference tool was absent", which is also where the replay bias acts (S4G L21, L66: "replay may inflate the measured value of recovery"). The plan lists replay bias as a threat but does not say how strata are defined so that they are arm-independent.
2. **No headroom arm.** There is no scorer-side oracle-exposure arm (exposure restricted to the gold-required set, used only to bound the best case) to show how much a perfect selector could save under each cost convention. If it saves little in billed cost in a cached regime, no selector, however good, can pay, and the experiment could stop there. Without it, a negative result on N2 cannot be told from a weak selector. (This is a missing falsifier-supporting control, not a design of mine: it exists because P-class information is legitimate in the scorer.)
3. **No model-strength, repeat or provider factor.** The validation lists models as a "dependency fixing numbers", but not as a factor. The evidence the proposer cites varies with model strength (EXT-0026: two strong models "largely mitigated" the decline, GPT-4o and DeepSeek-V3 did not; EXT-0047: No Tool Use 25.7% for Haiku 4.5 vs 9.1% Opus 4.7; FitText gain grows with capability, EXT-0035), and with provider (cache behaviour is vendor-specific: Anthropic "Entire cache", OpenAI "from that point forward", EXT-0008, EXT-0011). S4G's minimum designs require "at least two models of different strength" and repeats (S4G L65, L74). A result on one model and one provider supports no regime claim.
4. **EXT-0042 not used on the 12-turn budget.** DPR-0003 and DPR-0002 E.4 cite the turn budget as a handicap of unknown size (S4G L27). EXT-0042, which the proposer's HYP-0004 basis cites, is the e9.1 budget-30 control: pass 20.0% to 18.0%, tasks reaching the budget 5/50 to 0/50, "the agent stops itself at ~5 successful calls out of 30 permitted" (exploratory, one model, one cell). The threats list should carry it as the only datum in the record on the question, with its limits, rather than "magnitude unknown".
5. **Initiation measurement.** The plan measures "initiation rate (when needed tool absent)". For P3 the model sees no tool list, so "needed tool absent" is every non-core task and the rate is the search-call rate; its denominator is the number of tasks that need a non-core tool, which is a scorer-side U1 quantity on a single-reference vehicle. Say so.

**Evidence.** DPR-0003 N2 and section 3; HYP-0003; DPR-0001 R1; S4G L21, L27, L65, L66, L74; EXT-0008, EXT-0011, EXT-0026, EXT-0035, EXT-0042, EXT-0047 (read by me).

**What would resolve it.** Strata defined from an arm-independent source (or comparisons restricted to ablations that share `E_1`); an oracle-bound arm named as scorer-side only; model strength and provider as stated factors with repeats; EXT-0042 in the threats; initiation denominator defined.

**Outcome.** resolved (DPR-0003 section 3 "Added arms", "Strata", "Threats" checked). Item 5 (initiation denominator) not addressed; note level.
