# Title: The tally "46 confirmed, 13 partly" is looser than the record's own definition of "partly", and it measures the scouts' reading, not support for the ideas

**Objection.** (a) `14` s1 defines "partly" as "a number or quote in the scout's reading was not verbatim, merged two statements, or an absence claim rests on the stored pages only". Applying that definition to the steward notes, at least 14 of the 46 "confirmed" rows carry exactly such a note and were still counted confirmed: X6 (cap 50 holds for the tool parameter only), X7 (absence in two files only), X9 (6.1x is against the minified context), X11 (quote is a compression of two clauses), X12 (steward's own verdict: PARTLY for "extra LLM calls not costed"; tallied as confirmed), X14 (the gpt-o3 + Cohere pairing "not separately confirmed"; "Concat beats TDWA" holds only for plain vector search), Z3 (dataset names for 56% and 47% "not read line by line"; baseline not stated), Z5 (row headers "not read"), Z6 (type-checking clause "not read"), Z7 (mechanism "not read"), Z8 (quotation uses "ability", page says "capability"), Z10 (stored line cut), Z13 ("requires" shown by examples only), Z14 (threshold only after calibration), Z17 ("rejected" is the scout's word; table cells not read), Z22 ("may" unverified). Y12 is "CONFIRMED" although the source table is wrong. The honest split is nearer "confirmed as stated" about 30, "confirmed with a caveat" about 16, "partly" 13.
(b) "0 contradicted, 0 not found" is a statement about how faithfully the scouts read pages they themselves picked (the claims files are scout leads), not about whether the literature supports the exposé ideas. Placed in a table headed "Result", it can be read as 46 supporting findings. The scouts' prompts also asked for contrary evidence, but no claim batch is labelled as a contrary-evidence batch, so the proportion of adverse items among the 62 is unknown.
(c) Z1, Z2 and Z15 were checked by the lead, who also wrote `14`. No SUM file records them and they enter the 62 on the same footing as steward checks. (I re-read the Z1, Z2 and Z15 figures myself; they are on the pages: Re-Invoke 0.6522 and 0.7821 at `v5-xp-arxiv-2408.01875.txt` L595, L609; Tool-DE 36.41 / 39.35 / 40.02 / 38.85 in `v5-xp-arxiv-2510.22670.txt` L323, L454, L375, L506; ToolRet 33.83 to 28.92 at `pdf-toolret-2503.01763v2.txt` L1488-1489. The objection is about process, not those numbers.)

**Evidence.** SUM-0050 X6, X7; SUM-0051 X9, X11, X12, X14 and its "Counts" paragraph; SUM-0052; SUM-0053 Z3, Z5, Z6, Z7, Z8; SUM-0054 Z10, Z13, Z14, Z17; SUM-0055 Z22; SUM-0049 Y12. Recount of the 59 steward rows: 46 + 13 = 59 and 59 + 3 lead = 62 hold.

**What would resolve it.** Report three classes (as stated / with caveat / partly) or restate "partly" so that the 16 rows above are not counted as plain confirmed; add one sentence that the tally measures reading fidelity of scout leads; list Z1, Z2, Z15 as lead-checked and store a SUM for them.
