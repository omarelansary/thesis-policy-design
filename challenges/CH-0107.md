# Title: the sentence comparing hosted prices with frontier prices contradicts the r list above it, and "w = 1.0 where no write fee is listed" is treated as "no fee"

**Objection.** (a) `11` s2 ends: frontier vendors have "r of 0.1 to 0.025 and w of 1.25 to 2.0; hosted open-weight prices have a weaker or similar cache discount and almost no write fee". The same section lists r = 0.02 (DeepSeek V4.1-Flash, Fireworks) and 0.033 (DeepSeek V4-Pro), a stronger discount than 0.025 and 0.1 respectively, and r = 0.10 at three hosts. The open-weight set is wider, not weaker. Alibaba explicit has w = 1.25, the same as the frontier 5-minute class.
(b) The w row sets w = 1.0 "wherever no write fee is listed". Per the stewards, "no write fee appears on the page" is an absence (FND-0043 A1, A4, A5); AA says cache write and storage "are billed separately and vary by provider" (aa-caching L22, A15), so "-" means not listed. Z.ai's storage is "Limited-time Free", a charge that may start; MiniMax-M3's write price is unstated. The upper bound 1.25 is therefore the highest value on the stored open-weight pages, not a bound: the same section quotes 2.0 for the frontier 1-hour class, and a storage or time-based charge is not in the D-04 formula at all.
(c) DeepSeek's own page doubles every price at peak hours (01:00-04:00 and 06:00-10:00 UTC on weekdays, off-peak is half). This does not change the ratios, but `11` s4 says dollar levels "span about 50x" without this 2x time-of-day factor, which matters for a bill "on average over sessions".

**Evidence.** `11` s2 last paragraph and r row; `v5-lit-deepseek-pricing-20261027.txt` L32-43; FND-0043 rows A1, A4, A5, A15; `v5-lit-zai-pricing-20261027.txt` L46 ("Limited-time Free").

**What would resolve it.** Replace the sentence by "open-weight hosts span a wider r range (0.02 to 0.50) than the frontier vendors (0.025 to 0.1) and mostly list no write fee; whether none is charged is not stated." Label w = 1.0 as "no fee listed", note storage "Limited-time Free", and add the DeepSeek peak factor to s3 or s6.
