# Title: Floor and ceiling are named as a bias in s1.4, then treated only as a scored property, and the card column that is meant to judge them uses older models than the route serves

**Objection.** s1.4 says that if tasks are too hard or too easy for the open-weight models on the route, "accuracy differences become invisible and bias the reading toward 'nearly the same'". That is the register's own statement of a bias that favours V5's headline (accuracy nearly the same). Yet in s4 floor appears only under *Scored properties* ("difficulty", "floor for the route's models", "difficulty for the route's models"), never as a hard requirement, for P2, P4 and E2E, which are the accuracy constructs.
Second, the s5 column "Open-weight range" is supposed to let a reader judge floor and ceiling, but its cells are snapshots of models that were open-weight at the time of each paper: MCPMark 4.72% (gpt-oss-120b) to 24.80% (paper v1, 2025-09), MCP-Universe 11.26-24.68%, Toolathlon 13-20% (paper v2, 2026-02, before "Verified" of 2026-06-30), LiveMCPBench 30.5-48.4% (Qwen3-32B to DeepSeek-R1/Qwen3-235B, judge DeepSeek-V3), MCPVerse about 45-59%. The route serves GLM-5.3, MiniMax-M3 and gpt-oss-120b (STATE; `10`). The same benchmark's open-weight reading moved from 12-34% to 75.6% between paper versions (CH-0139), so these ranges do not transfer. Several rows say "not established" or "-" (BFCL, tau2, ToolSandbox, ATRBench, Momento, VitaBench, MPT, ETAPP), so the column is empty exactly for the multi-session candidates.
Third, at the sample size `05` s1 states (about 20 tasks, SE about 10 points), a baseline at 5% (gpt-oss on MCPMark) or near 80% cannot show an accuracy difference of a few points at all; MCPMark issue #272 (SUM-0068 A23) says two single runs per service must differ by about 11 points before the gap exceeds repetition noise.

**Evidence.** `17` s1.4 L32, s4 L82-87, s5 L96-117; `05` s1; SUM-0068 A23, A25; SUM-0069 A32 (MCP-Bench 0.668-0.692 is an LLM-judge composite, not a pass rate, so "ceiling" there is the register's reading); `a14` L229-326 (LiveMCPBench Table 2, judge DeepSeek-V3); `i13` L95, L298. I checked that the MCPVerse Max-Scale 31.6 is the Qwen3-235B-2507 row (`a06` L419-430, Oracle 44.8, Standard 53.2, Max-Scale 31.6), so that cell holds.
[F] numbers as above. [I] that route-model accuracy on the baseline arm is the only reliable floor/ceiling evidence. [U] the route models' accuracy on any candidate.

**What would resolve it.** (a) Make "baseline-arm accuracy on at least two route models is measured on a pilot subset, disjoint from the confirmatory set, and lies between named bounds" a hard requirement for P2, P4 and E2E (the bounds are the human's to choose; the register can state the sample size at which a given difference becomes visible instead). Pilots on the baseline arm look at an outcome of the *baseline*, not of the contrast, which s1.1's corollary allows. (b) Relabel the column "open-weight range as reported, model generation and date", and mark it not transferable to the route. (c) Where the cell is empty, say that the floor/ceiling for the route is unknown rather than leaving a dash.
