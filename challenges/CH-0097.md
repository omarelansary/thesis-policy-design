# Title: the "in tension" verdict on the supervisor's caching assertion omits his hedge, his own return-case remark, and leaves "negligible" and "explicit cache" undefined

**Objection.** 02 L27 rates REQ-O21-04 "Consistent per request, in tension over a session and across gaps". The rule at 02 L3 is to treat assertions "fairly" and call nobody right or wrong; the register's own wording is "a claim to test ... not a contradiction resolved here" (REGISTER L107). Points the row leaves out:
1. In the transcript the supervisor states the whole picture as "in theory that is all in theory" (INT-T-002 file L240), then moves to "practical".
2. He addresses the return case himself: when the person comes back and says hello, "do you give all the context at the same time ... No, you shouldn't" (L230-L238). So the gap issue 02 raises is one he already treats as requiring selective re-supply, which is what V5-R's seeding does. The row does not say this.
3. "Negligible" is not given a threshold. The row's comparison is cost of the cached addition against "not adding it" (0.1 of a fresh send per request). That assumes the information has no value when absent. The supervisor's claim is that it costs little to keep information that is needed. A fairer comparison is the added read as a share of the per-request bill, or against re-fetching the information.
4. "Explicit cache" is the supervisor's term. In the stored provider pages "explicit" fits Gemini's explicit caches (storage per hour) as well as Anthropic's breakpoints. The row computes Anthropic arithmetic (1.25x write, 0.1x read) and mentions Gemini storage from FND-0039. The search log says the stored Gemini page is a 29-line stub (L14), so the Gemini part rests on older records.
5. He said "for 1 hour that is still negligible" (L228). The row's numbers use a 1.25x (5 min) write; the 1 h write is 2x and the lifetime question for a conversation with gaps is not computed for his own 1 h case.
The arithmetic in the row (0.215 of re-sending after 10 requests; 2.15 and 11.15 fresh sends after 10 and 100) is correct for what it computes.

**Evidence.** requirements/intake/2026-10-21-supervisor-meeting-2026-10-21-notes-and-transcript.md L226-L240 (file lines); REGISTER L77, L107; 02 L3, L27; search log L14.

**What would resolve it.** Quote his hedge and his return remark in the row; define "negligible" as a stated share of the per-request bill; mark the provider whose "explicit cache" is computed and say the Gemini side is unchecked until the page is refetched; change the verdict to "consistent per request under Anthropic and OpenAI prices; the size over a session depends on what negligible means".
