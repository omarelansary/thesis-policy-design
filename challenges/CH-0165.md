# Title: Fixes to sections 1-6 were added as overlays and left the original text in place, so several accepted challenges are only partly applied

**Objection.** s5 and s4 state that the original tables are "kept as written" and that corrections apply "over" them. That keeps the history, but a reader of the tables and of s7 meets the old text, and some corrections were not made at all although s8.2 says "Accepted ... applied".
1. *CH-0145 / CH-0146.* The correction (L113) says SWE-bench Verified and Terminal-Bench are in scope for P4 and that MetaTool's licence reason is dropped. The triage paragraph itself (L144) still reads "out of scope for every item" and lists "Terminal-Bench, SWE-bench Verified (no tool catalogue ...)", "MetaTool (CC BY-NC-SA, selection only)", and puts MCP-AgentBench and DynamicMCPBench (snippets only, "not assessed") in the same out-of-scope list. The triage is the text a reader uses.
2. *CH-0157.* Four statements were challenged. The correction (L112) addresses three (MPT licence, ATRBench "no time", MCP-Bench "ceiling") and does not address the first: s4 L82 still says "No candidate reports the bill ... only ATRBench and Claw-SWE-Bench state cache accounting", although the cards show Toolathlon "cost with caching on", SWE-ContextBench cache-read above 97% and tau2 leaderboard costs with caching discounts (SUM-0076 D23, D30; SUM-0077 E7). The MPT answer is incomplete (CH-0164).
3. *CH-0139.* The MCP-Atlas row still carries "12.0-34.0% (v1)" and the 78% judge agreement in the table; the correction is a bullet above. s7 uses the corrected reading, but its "no floor problem" misreads the correction (CH-0162).
4. *CH-0147 / CH-0149.* s4's table still lists P3 H1 (equivalents), P3 H3, P1 H5, E2E H1-H4 as written; revision 2 changes them in a list below. s7 and the P3/P1 rejections are consistent with revision 2, but the table's reject-on-requirement logic is no longer usable as written. A one-table consolidation would remove the risk of someone applying revision 1.
5. *s8.2 column "Where applied".* It names "section 5 corrections" or "section 4 revision 2" for 15 of 19 rows, which are the overlays above, and "R4 revised" and "R11-R14" in s3 (these were edited in place). The statement in s8.2 that all 19 were applied is true of the overlays and not of the underlying cells.

**Evidence.** [F] register L82, L107, L112-113, L119, L144, L226-246; SUM-0076 D23, D30; SUM-0077 E7. [O] by reading the lines above.

**What would resolve it.** Edit L144 (move SWE-bench Verified/Terminal-Bench to a P4 line; separate "not assessed"; drop MetaTool's licence reason), L82 (state "no per-request fresh/cached/write split"), the MCP-Atlas cell; fold revision 2 into the s4 table (keep revision 1 in a history note). Or say in s8.2 that overlays are the application and that the tables are historical.
