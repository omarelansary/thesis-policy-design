# Title: "One small sample" and the MemTool range state more than the steward could establish

**Objection.**

SUM-0049 Y13 says the number of failed trajectories "is not stated in the lines I read" and that the 1/60 multiples "would fit 60 failures; I did not find that count". "Small" is the lead's inference. The page text I read says the errors were classified for Claude-Opus-4 and Claude-Sonnet-4 trajectories and "Each erroneous trajectory was uniquely classified" (`v5-xp-arxiv-2508.01780.txt` L981-986); the count is not in those lines. Also the four-way split there is of agent-run errors, which `14` notes as not matching the exposé's split.

Neither changes a conclusion; both are stated more firmly than the stored text allows.
