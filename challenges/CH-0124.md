# Title: The "nearest measured cuts" merge two different measures, one on synthetic catalogues, and omit the stored name-and-description figure

**Objection.**
(a) "compressing schemas cuts about 44-57% of schema tokens" joins two papers: 2605.26165 reports 44-50% (a format comparison on 28 tools, 14 models) and 2605.04107 (TSCG) reports 52-57% "on synthetic catalogs" (`v5-xp-arxiv-2605.04107.txt` L261; abstract L42), authored by the repository's owner with "TAB is the author's benchmark" (SUM-0051 X11). `14` mentions "the author's own benchmark" but not that the 52-57% is on synthetic catalogues, and gives a merged range as if one measurement.
(b) "lifted accuracy only where the context budget binds" is X10's finding ("confirming the effect is purely budget-driven", four of five models at 32K with Delta <= 1 pp, SUM-0051 X10). For TSCG the headline is Phi-4 14B from 0% to 84.4% at 20 tools (X11); that is not shown to be budget-bound in the lines read. The "only" generalises one paper.
(c) The 0.4 compact fraction. The measured cuts in (a) correspond to compact fractions 0.43-0.56, so 0.4 lies just outside the measured range, which is more informative than "no source". Separately, X9 reports a name-plus-concise-description context of 347 tokens against 2,120 minified (about 0.16; `v5-xp-arxiv-2511.03728.txt`, SUM-0051 X9), the nearest stored figure for an L1 level; `14` does not use it. The mapping of any of these to D-03's levels is [I] and none defines L0-L3.

**Evidence.** As cited; SUM-0051 X9, X10, X11.

**What would resolve it.** Report the two ranges separately with their setting (format comparison; synthetic catalogues, author benchmark), restrict "only where the budget binds" to X10, and add the 347 / 2,120 ratio and the 0.43-0.56 conversion next to "no source for 0.4".
