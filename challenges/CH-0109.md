# Title: "depends mainly on searches per session, little on price" holds at T = 10 only; at T = 3 the cache ratio r alone roughly doubles n*, and the extreme cells have more searches than requests

**Objection.** I recomputed two cells by hand and both match the printout (RV-0024 s4), and the numbers quoted in `11` s5 match the grid. What the grid does not support is the headline.
(a) Table B (T = 3), D = 10, fixed w = 1.0: n* goes 54 -> 123 (o = 2.1), 70 -> 129 (o = 5), 85 -> 134 (o = 7.5) as r goes 0.02 -> 1.0, i.e. 1.6x to 2.3x from r alone; at D = 1 it is 11 -> 18 (1.6x). Over D, at fixed r = 1.0, w = 1.0, o = 2.1, n* goes 18 -> 123 (6.8x); at r = 0.02, 11 -> 54 (4.9x). So D is larger than r, but r is not "little" at T = 3: it is a factor of about 2. Table A (T = 10) is where "little" is true: at D = 10, fixed w = 1.0, r moves n* by at most 1.4x (77 -> 55 at o = 7.5; 50 -> 52 at o = 2.1). `11` bullet 2 then states the reversal at T = 3, so the section contradicts its own lead sentence for short sessions, which are the supervisor's "return after a gap" case (REQ horizon, N-3).
(b) The comparison "far more than with price" depends on the spans chosen: D spans 10x (1, 3, 10) and r spans 50x (0.02 to 1.0), with no stated reason for either. Only a rank statement restricted to T = 10, or elasticities at stated points, is supported.
(c) Cells T = 3, D = 10 mean ten client-side searches inside a three-request session; the model charges `t + disc - 1` requests, so it is defined, but it is not a session unless searches are batched. Reading statement 3 draws its maxima from this cell: "w by at most about 10 tools" (T=3, D=10: 85 vs 75, 89 vs 79, 95 vs 85 at o = 7.5) and "o by up to about 30" (54 -> 85 at r = 0.02). With D <= T (T = 3, D = 3) the w effect is about 3 tools (30 vs 27 at r = 0.02, o = 7.5) and the o effect about 9 (21 -> 30).
(d) The effect of price is not nil at the typical cell: D = 3, T = 10 gives 19-28 over the grid (1.5x).

**Evidence.** `calc/price_sensitivity.stdout.txt` L5-16 (Table A), L20-33 (Table B); hand reading of those rows.

**What would resolve it.** Reword s5 bullet 1 and the script reading: "at T = 10 n* depends mainly on D (r moves it at most 1.4x at D = 10); at T = 3 r roughly doubles n* while D multiplies it 5-7x"; mark D > T cells as outside a plausible session or impose D <= T; recompute the w and o maxima over D <= T.
