# Title: The triage cannot be audited by a reviewer, and two rows it marks relevant are never closed in the body

**Objection.** (a) The two reports are untracked (`.workspace/`, by the human's instruction) and named in `15` only as "the two reports"; the triage rows are the lead's interpretation of them. I read the two reports' headings and executive summary; a reviewer without them cannot check whether anything relevant to the bill or to which tools are offered was dismissed, and `15` does not say what share of each report the table covers. I found no claim in the parts I read about tool selection or lazy loading that the table drops (a grep of both reports for tool-selection, lazy loading, progressive disclosure, dynamic tool discovery found nothing), but I did not read the reports in full.
(b) Two rows are marked relevant and then have no body counterpart. "Procedural memory and generated skills, autonomous or human-gated (Voyager, OMP skills, Antigravity `/learn`)": scout A could not confirm that `/learn` exists (A section 4: two searches, the codelab has no `/learn`, "only third-party pages"). The report states `/learn` as documented by Google. That a headline claim in the human's report was not corroborated is a finding for the human and `15` does not report it. "Recall only on the first model turn, not in subagents (OMP with Hindsight)": the body covers Mnemopi (B12) and says nothing on Hindsight or subagents; the Hindsight blog was "identified but not read" (scout B s4).
(c) The "No" rows are consistent with the human's complaint of bias toward a local setup, but "Storage engines ... ACLs, GDPR" is dismissed while PL-5 and PL-8 propose pooled data (CH-0129).

**Evidence.** `15` s1; `inputs/scout-reports-landscape-20261028/A-large-companies.md` s4 (Antigravity); `B-open-source-agents.md` s4; `.workspace/deep-research-report broader landscape.md` L21 (the report's `/learn` claim).

**What would resolve it.** Add to s1 a line "not corroborated: Antigravity `/learn`; not read: Hindsight, subagent behaviour", and state coverage of the reports.
