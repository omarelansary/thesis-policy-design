# Title: The MCP-Atlas card locks "paper v3" but takes its open-weight range and judge figure from paper v1, and omits what v3 reports on exactly those properties

**Objection.** The card header says "paper v3". Three cells come from v1 (stored `a01`, arXiv 2602.00933v1) and are not in v3 (stored `i13`, arXiv 2602.00933v3), while v3 says things that bear directly on the scored properties and are absent from the card.
(1) "Open-weight range 12.0-34.0% (v1)": the label "(v1)" is honest, but a card locked to v3 gives a floor reading that v3 contradicts. v3 reports 20 models on all 1,000 tasks; "the open-source GLM-5.1 (75.6%) is the highest-ranked open model", "statistically tied with GPT-5.5", and "the frontier is compressed: the top three models span 78.2-82.2%". The v1 range (GLM-4.5 Air 34.0, Qwen3-235B 12.0) is a floor reading; the v3 reading for a GLM-class model is near the ceiling. The route's models are GLM-5.3, MiniMax-M3 and gpt-oss-120b (STATE), so v3 is the nearer reading.
(2) "paper's judge-human agreement 78% (100 tasks)": the 78% is in v1 (`a01` L385-387, "random sample of 100 tasks", three annotators). I searched `i13` for "78%", "agreement" and "human" and found no judge-human agreement figure. v3 describes a different design: three LLM judges (Gemini 3.1 Pro Preview primary, GPT-5.4, Claude Opus 4.6), cross-judge ranges of 2.1-4.6 points, and the sentence "small rank swaps near the top should be read as evaluator uncertainty". The s6 text ("different measure from the paper's 78% judge agreement on 100 tasks") has the same problem.
(3) The card has no field for the contamination or exposure evidence that v3 gives and that s1.4 and s2.2 score: `i13` L729: "Most models score higher on public tasks, with gaps from -0.8 to +9.6 pp ... open-source models +3.8 pp"; the authors call these "audit signals", not contamination. The public/private split (500/500) is in the card, the measured gap is not.
(4) Tools per task: the card gives "6-37, mean 15.2; 2-8 required" as the paper's figure (found in `i13`, v3, L93 and L272 by grep; I did not read those long lines in full). The stored v1 says "10-25 tools per task, including 3-7 required tools and 5-10 distractors" (`a01` L421) and the HF card says "10-25" (`a03` L197). The paper versions and the dataset card disagree and the card does not say which version each number is from.

**Evidence.** `17` s5 MCP-Atlas row; `evidence/literature-20261019/v5-bm-i13-arxiv-html-2602.00933v3.txt` L95, L274, L282, L298, L722-731, L735; `v5-bm-a01-arxiv-html-2602.00933v1.txt` L385-387, L421, L483-493; SUM-0067 A1, A3, A4; inspector I1 item 1(a) ("paper read as v3").
[F] the figures above are on the stored pages. [I] the card was assembled from the scouts' v1 table (SUM-0067) and the inspector's v3 reading without reconciling them. [U] whether 6-37 tools per task is also in v1.

**What would resolve it.** Redo the MCP-Atlas row from `i13` alone for paper-derived cells (or label every cell with its version), replace the open-weight range by the v3 figures with the model names, add the v3 judge design and cross-judge range in place of the v1 78%, and add the public-private gap to the contamination field. State that the 78% is a v1 figure that v3 does not repeat.
