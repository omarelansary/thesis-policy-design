# Title: The E2E construct measures "bill per turn" but its requirements do not require more than one user turn, and "turn" is not defined for the candidates

**Objection.** `05` s3 and the human's answer (Q-U30) fix the headline as total bill over total turns (A) with the mean of per-session rates (B) beside it, averaged over many turns including a return after a gap (STATE: N-3). `01` L16 distinguishes D1 (each user turn) from D2 (each model request inside a turn). The s4 E2E row lists H1 realistic tasks with gold, H2 catalogue coverage, H3 a session axis by declared extension, H4 repeats; no hard requirement says a candidate task has more than one *user* turn. In the s5 cards, 13 of the 22 detailed rows are "single task"/"single turn" (MCP-Atlas, MCPVerse, Toolathlon, TheMCPCompany, MCPMark, MCP-Universe, MCP-Bench, LiveMCPBench, LiveMCP-101, MCPEvol-Bench, AppWorld, ToolMATH, Fail-TaLMs). For these, one user prompt starts an agentic loop of up to 20-100 requests (MCP-Atlas: "single-turn interactions"), so "per turn" equals "per task", the mean-of-sessions measure B equals the mean of tasks, and the effect that depends on user-turn count (cache accumulation across turns, the gap after which the cache is cold) has to be added by the extension in H3. The register never says whether, for these candidates, a "turn" is a user turn or a model request; the two readings give different bills per turn and different winners between A and B.
Related: the cache lifetime result (cold after 4 h on gpt-oss-120b, warm on MiniMax-M3 and GLM-5.3, `10` s11) means a "gap longer than the cache lifetime" is a wall-clock property of the route; no card field says whether gaps in a candidate are stored timestamps (Momento, VitaBench) or ordering (ATRBench), and none can supply real elapsed time. It is again the extension.
Also missing from E2E: the held-out reserve that s1.1 requires. s1.1's corollary (held-out items disjoint from everything looked at in pilots) is not a hard requirement for E2E, and E2E has no minimum pool: the pool available and the difference resolvable at that size are promised for s7 (still "to be filled"), not required of a candidate.

**Evidence.** `17` s4 L87; s5 L96-117 (Sessions column); `01` L16; `05` s3; `10` s11; SUM-0067 A2 ("single-turn ... to isolate core tool-use competency").
[F] the row texts. [I] that these absent requirements make E2E under-specified. [U] DPR-0006's exact definition of a turn for single-prompt tasks; I read `01` L16 only.

**What would resolve it.** Add to E2E: H5 "tasks contain at least k user turns, or the register states how user turns are formed by extension" (with k for the human to choose, or reported as the stratum); define "turn" in s4 as one user message and map each candidate's native unit to it; add H6 "a reserve of items disjoint from the pilot and the exploratory inspections, large enough for the chosen contrasts at the stated resolvable difference". Say that the gap axis is always an extension.
