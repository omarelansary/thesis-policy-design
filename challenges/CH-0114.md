# Title: the AA spread of 8.9x is reproducible and rests on pricing a missing cache price as input; `11` s5a mixes a cached/input rate with a share of all tokens

**Objection.** (a) `13` s4 says the 8.9x is "real" but rests on unrounded values (0.39/0.04 = 9.75 on rounded ones). I can reproduce it: CoreWeave (7 x 0.03 + 2 x 0.03 + 0.17) / 10 = 0.044 and Cerebras (7 x 0.35 + 2 x 0.35 + 0.75) / 10 = 0.39; 0.39 / 0.044 = 8.86, i.e. 8.9x. This also shows how the number is built: both hosts list no cache price, AA prices the cache term at the input price (see CH-0105), and 9/10 of the weight then sits on the input price, which differs 11.7x (0.35 vs 0.03). So the spread is mostly an input-price spread among hosts that have no listed cache discount; it says little about hosts with a discount. Neither `11` s5a nor `13` s4 says this; the stored text can be read as "same model, 9x price difference between hosts" generally. The step "so a single model price is not a stable input" is supported at the blended level only (the steward said the same, FND-0044 Q3).
(b) `11` s5a: "other sources measure a cache-read share of 46-92% of tokens, which brackets AA's 70%". Requesty's 46-92% is cached_tokens / input_tokens (FND-0044 B10), not a share of all tokens; AA's 70% is a share of all three buckets, and relative to input it is 7/9 = 78%. The bracket holds either way, but the sentence mixes two definitions that `13` s4 keeps apart.
(c) "Output share of about 0.5-1% of tokens": the sources are a sweep, a newsletter, a mean run and a vendor blog's input:output ratio of 100:1 (Manus), which is a ratio, not a share, and has no method. `13` says units differ; `11` states a flat 0.5-1%.

**Evidence.** `v5-aa-gpt-oss-120b-providers-20261027.txt` L49, L65, L465; Cerebras price `v5-lit-cerebras-gptoss-20261027.txt` L36; CoreWeave `v5-lit-wandb-inference-pricing-20261027.txt` L346-358; FND-0044 B2, B9, B10, B11 and the Q2 table; `11` s5a bullets 1 and 3.

**What would resolve it.** Add one sentence to s5a: the 8.9x is built from hosts that list no cache price, priced at the input price; change "of tokens" to "of input tokens (Requesty) or of all tokens (others)"; state that 0.5-1% is the span across different measures.
