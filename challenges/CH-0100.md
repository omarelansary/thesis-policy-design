# Title: 01 section 1 states interpretations as the decision-makers' positions and omits requirements that bear on the search and the success test

**Objection.**
1. L6: "The supervisor treats the count as the means and tokens as the aim (REQ-O02-03, REQ-O02-09)". The register (L105-L106) says the supervisor "states both" and that "the ledger reads tokens as the objective and the tool count as the means (Q-J02)". That is the ledger's reading, an interpretation. REQ-O14F-01 says the question is the count and "We don't need to change it". The heading "Question." with the quoted sentence also sits next to STATE's "thesis question not yet supplied; entering it is the human's decision". Both are label problems, not content problems, but 01 is the problem statement everything else cites.
2. L6-L7: REQ-O14F-01 says "accuracy as high as possible"; REQ-U21-07 says "accuracy nearly the same", no margin. 01 does not note the difference. It matters for the test in 05 and for what "no improvement established" means.
3. Omitted from the list: REQ-U21-11 ("success is the quality of the work (every scientific and engineering approach considered, nothing missed), not a particular result", REGISTER L123) and REQ-U21-05 (the June exposé's ideas are candidates to be kept or dropped with a stated reason, L117). REQ-U21-11 is the standard against which the unreached frameworks and unexamined benchmarks in the search log (L7, L9) should be judged, and 01 does not mention the gap. The June exposé deferral is correctly recorded (AUTH-020 verbatim, AUTHORIZATIONS L528), but REQ-U21-05 as a requirement is not.
4. L13: the principle "reuse what research, large companies or widely used open source already tested (REQ-U21-03)" is paired with "the earlier 27-method retrieval work stays out (REQ-U21-02)". The register adds that the two are read together: earlier work enters only when the human places it explicitly and it is verified (REGISTER L126). 01 states only the exclusion.

**Evidence.** REGISTER L84, L105-L106, L117, L119, L123, L126; 01 L6-L7, L13; ops/AUTHORIZATIONS.md L528; ops/STATE.md (thesis question line).

**What would resolve it.** Re-label L6 ("ledger's reading, Q-J02; supervisor states both"), add the accuracy wording difference, and add REQ-U21-05, REQ-U21-11 and the REGISTER L126 relation to the list.
