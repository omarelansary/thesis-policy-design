# Title: "A reject-only gate exists and is measured (all 322 hallucinated calls rejected by the full stack)" - the paper says that figure is true by construction, and the only false-reject check is circular

**Objection.** `14` writes that the closed-world gate "is measured: all 322 hallucinated calls rejected by the full stack". The paper says otherwise about exactly this number. What is measured is the count of 322 hallucinations the models emitted. "The two execution rates that follow are not independent measurements but consequences of the threat model ... the full stack executes none (0/322), again by construction (Prop. 3), with (and this part is measured) zero over-rejection of the honest calls the models emitted ... the reader should treat 322 as the datum and the execution rates as definitional" (`lit-closedworld-2609.19425.txt` L489-503). The limitations section then says the 0.00 over-rejection "is a construction-consistency check, not independent evidence", because a well-formed call is defined as one the resolver admits, and that a genuine false-reject rate on an independent corpus is "the decisive next experiment" (L1078-1084). Also per-model rates are single point estimates at 60 probes per model and surface, on a single synthetic registry (L1088-1092).
Second, "15.25% of fabricated-argument trials stay schema-valid" is the H5 class, defined as a call that borrows the argument shape of a different tool ("borrowed signature", L253-262), measured as 61 of 400 deterministic trials in a controlled ablation on the synthetic registry (L638-664). "Fabricated-argument" is the record's wording, not the paper's.
SUM-0053 marked Z6 CONFIRMED and quoted "(by construction)" only for the gating-only arm, so the construction caveat did not reach `14`. The gate in the paper is name resolution against a registry, not the exposé's validation-triggered expansion; relevance to EX-5 is [I].

**Evidence.** `lit-closedworld-2609.19425.txt` L489-503, L1078-1084, L1088-1092, L253-262, L638-664; SUM-0053 Z6 row.

**What would resolve it.** Replace "is measured ... all 322 rejected" with "the paper reports 322 emitted hallucinations as the measured datum; that the full stack executes none is by construction, and its false-reject rate was not independently measured"; replace "fabricated-argument trials" with "H5 (borrowed-signature) trials in a synthetic-registry ablation".
