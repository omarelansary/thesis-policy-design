# Title: The stated reason for demoting "established, independently evaluated" is contradicted by s6 and is partly speculation

**Objection.** s1.2 says that in this niche "most benchmarks are months old and few have an independent evaluation; the older, more vetted ones tend to have small catalogues and a higher risk of being in open-weight models' training data". The conclusion (independent evaluation is one scored property, not a dominating preference) is within the human's text, which says "prefer ... when they adequately measure the intended construct" and "do not impose a rigid hierarchy" (INT-T-021 L37-39). The reason given is weaker than the conclusion needs:
- "few have an independent evaluation": s6 lists third-party criticism for BFCL (Epoch), tau2-bench (issues, a verified fork), MCP-Atlas (issues #71, #76), MCPMark (#272, #278), MCP-Universe (#45), Toolathlon (#85, #98), LiveMCPBench (#9), AppWorld (issues), ToolBench (StableToolBench). The register's own reading is that it is the *youngest* benchmarks that lack scrutiny (ATRBench, Momento, VitaBench 2.0, MPT, ETAPP). So for the benchmarks that matter for the end-to-end claim the opposite holds: they are scrutinised, and the scrutiny gives documented defects, fixes and versions (tau2-Verified, Toolathlon-Verified, MCPMark Verified), i.e. information about scorer error that unscrutinised ones lack.
- "higher risk of being in open-weight models' training data": no source for this is cited in s1-s6. Bean et al. (SUM-0065 M14) say reuse of datasets "raises contamination risk"; MCP-Atlas v3 gives a measured public-private gap of +3.8 points for open models, an exposure signal for a benchmark that is only months old (CH-0139). Without citations the sentence is [S].
- "older ... small catalogues": plausible for BFCL, tau, ToolSandbox; AppWorld (2024) has 457 API definitions as files, larger than MCP-Atlas's per-task sets.
I do not claim the conclusion is wrong. The demotion could still be right for P5 and P3 (controlled-variable experiments, where the human also said constructed items may be preferable).

**Evidence.** `17` s1.2 L17, s6 L126-153; INT-T-021 L37-39; SUM-0065 M14; `i13` L729; inspector I1 item 6.
[F] quoted text. [I] the contradiction. [S] contamination of older benchmarks.

**What would resolve it.** Replace the sentence by what s6 shows: scrutiny is concentrated on the established benchmarks, and gives known defects and fixed versions; unscrutinised ones have unknown scorer error. Mark the contamination clause [S] or cite a source.
