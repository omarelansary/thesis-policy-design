# Title: Three EX-5 / EX-4 statements stretch what the pages say (abstention score, BFCL audit, a ScaleMCP range)

**Objection.**
(a) "Models abstain poorly unprompted (best paired accuracy 59.5%, Z8)". The page defines paired accuracy as "correct on both the act and abstain sides of each paired task" over 263 generated task pairs and 17 models in 4 harnesses (`v5-xp-arxiv-2607.10059.txt` L24-31), so it is a joint score, not an abstention rate. "Unprompted" is not stated; the harness adds no system prompt beyond the per-task scenario prompt (L2823-2824). The tasks are LLM-generated with a should-abstain twin; they are not "no tool in the catalogue matches" cases. The step to the exposé's no-match rule is [I].
(b) "an audit found defects in 48% of a 50-task sample (Z9, Z10)" sits next to BFCL's irrelevance category. Epoch's sample was stratified over all categories, five tasks per irrelevance/non-live/live category, and category defect rates "ranged from 20% (non-live AST) to 70% (memory)"; no rate is given for irrelevance in the lines read (`v5-xp-epoch-bfcl-review.txt` L15-18). The 48% is not evidence about irrelevance detection. The 39% and 10% shares are correct (SUM-0053 Z9; Epoch L18) but are shares of two different benchmark versions (V2 Live entries; V4 score), as `14` says.
(c) "GPT-4o and Cohere reranking add 0.02-0.07 NDCG (Z16)". From ScaleMCP Table 2 (`v5-xp-arxiv-2505.06416.txt` L446-507), on the VertexAI rows (the ones SUM-0054 lists), Cohere minus vector is +0.02, 0.00, +0.02 and GPT-4o minus vector is +0.04, +0.04, +0.05; minus hybrid, Cohere is +0.07, +0.05, +0.04 and GPT-4o +0.09, +0.09, +0.07. No consistent baseline gives 0.02-0.07. On 3-large rows, GPT-4o minus hybrid is +0.13, +0.07, +0.04. The record's own s5 lists the Z16 rows as unresolved, but the sentence is stated as a result. "Hybrid sits below vector alone" holds at SQ = 0 and 5 and ties at SQ = 10 on 3-large and 3-small (0.63/0.63, 0.64/0.64; L454, L471, L458, L475).

**Evidence.** As cited; SUM-0054 Z16 note.

**What would resolve it.** Word (a) as "the best of 17 models gets 59.5% on joint act-and-abstain pairs"; delete 48% from the EX-5 sentence or move it to a BFCL-reliability remark; replace the 0.02-0.07 range by the table rows with the baseline named.
