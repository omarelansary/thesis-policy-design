# Title: No proof-of-concept item has a decision rule that can tell "nearly the same" accuracy from "lower", yet each is said to "end" a stage

**Objection.** The falsifiers of HYP-0018, HYP-0021 and DPR-0007 (b) read "lower ... beyond run-to-run variation", and `05` gives about 20 tasks (REQ-O02-10) with "several runs per arm" (P2). With about 20 paired tasks and pass/fail outcomes the standard error of a difference in success rate is of the order of 10 points (e.g. p = 0.7, n = 20 per arm gives about 14 points unpaired); the effect sizes the packet itself cites are 2-5 points (EXT-0228). Failing to find a difference is not evidence of equivalence, and REQ-U21-07 (no margin) and DPR-0006:52 ("argued and reported rather than settled by a number set in advance") mean no equivalence test is available. `05` section 1 then states "An item that fails its falsifier removes its stage; it does not trigger a bigger version of the same test": a noisy loop can remove a stage on a false negative (or keep one on a false positive) with no stated error asymmetry, while the staged graph is the supervisor's stated novelty (REQ-O14F-03). I do not challenge "no margin" or the 20-sample start (REQ-O02-10, REQ-U21-06, "not rigorous"). I challenge the column "What it can end": for P2 in particular the design can end only a large accuracy drop, not "nearly the same", and cannot show the possible gain (HYP-0018 "may improve when the set is large"). [I] The arithmetic of the standard error is mine, not the packet's.

**Evidence.** `05-poc-loop-and-e2e.md` section 1 (rows P2, P3, P4, "What it can end" column) and the "Loop rule"; `hypotheses/HYP-0018.md`, `HYP-0021.md` falsifiers; DPR-0007 falsifier (b); `design/proposals/DPR-0006.md:52`; `requirements/REGISTER.md` REQ-U21-06, REQ-U21-07, REQ-O02-10, REQ-O14F-03; EXT-0228:59.

**What would resolve it.** For each item state the smallest accuracy difference that the planned n can show (a power statement), say that smaller differences are reported but not decided, and say who decides keep/drop for those cases (the human, argued); add the asymmetry rule to the Loop rule ("a drop on a noisy result is provisional until the end-to-end study").
