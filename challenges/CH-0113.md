# Title: "the plain-prefix rule ... is the literature's" and "documented by every provider above" are stronger than the stored pages

**Objection.** (a) `13` s3: "the plain-prefix rule behind V5-R02 is the literature's, and the probes on GLM-5.3 and MiniMax-M3 agree with it". The stored documentation shows several rules, and one is not a longest-common-prefix rule: DeepSeek's page says a hit needs a full match of a persisted "cache prefix unit"; request A+B then A+C is a miss and A is persisted only after the second request (kvcache L19-31). OpenAI, Alibaba and Groq state hits are not guaranteed (Alibaba L1111, L71; Groq L45); `13` s3 row 5 itself cites replica-local caches. What is supported: the probes on two models are consistent with a prefix hash chain as in vLLM, SGLang and Mooncake. Whether the University's models follow that rule or DeepSeek's unit rule is not shown; under the unit rule the first request after a tool-array change gets no hit at all, which the scripts do not model.
(b) "The minimum cacheable length (documented by every provider above)": no minimum was found on the stored Z.ai caching page (FND-0043 A3) or the DeepSeek page (I searched "minimum", "64 tokens", "at least" in kvcache: no match). Alibaba gives 1,024 and, for Zhipu-deployed GLM models, 512 (context-cache L1113). Replace by "documented by Groq, MiniMax, Alibaba, OpenAI and Anthropic".
(c) "near Alibaba's and OpenAI's 1,024": 1,550 is 51% above 1,024; `11` s3 says "above most of these", which is accurate; the `13` wording is not.
(d) The Qwen multiple of 32 in `10` is dropped from the s3 first row (FND-0045 Fact 1 lists it as unmatched), and Mooncake's 512 and LMCache's 256 also match none of the three observed sizes; the row presents only the matches.

**Evidence.** `v5-lit-deepseek-kvcache-20261027.txt` L19-31; `v5-lit-alibaba-context-cache-20261027.txt` L71, L1111-1115; `v5-lit-groq-prompt-caching-20261027.txt` L45; FND-0045 Fact 1, Fact 2; FND-0043 A3.

**What would resolve it.** Reword the Consequence ("consistent with a prefix hash chain; DeepSeek's documented unit rule would behave differently and is not excluded"); correct (b), (c); add the unmatched sizes to row 1.
