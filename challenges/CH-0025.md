# Title: the cost of running A1 (full-catalogue dev runs plus a search pilot) is outside the cost vector, against REQ-S1

**Grade: SERIOUS (a stated requirement; the comparison with expose-all, which has zero selection cost, is unfair without it).**

**Objection.** REQ-S1 as recorded in EXT-0002 l.26: "The selection mechanism's cost must not cancel the savings"; savings are "judged globally, not per turn" with two open readings (turns in one session, or aggregate over many requests; l.27). C-A's selection mechanism includes an offline phase: `pi_ref` runs over the dev tasks at the full catalogue (the most expensive configuration) to estimate `p_hat`, and a pilot of the search path for `q_hat`. DPR-0004 section 6.1 says cost "counts deciding, discovering and using (all categories)", and the outcome list (DPR-0005 E3 equivalent) names "overhead of the decision mechanism", but no accounting rule is given for A1's offline cost: neither included, nor amortised over a deployment volume `N_dep`, nor excluded with a reason. On the "aggregate over many requests" reading it must be amortised, which needs `N_dep` (a regime variable that does not appear in A1). For small deployments the offline cost can exceed the saving; that is the case in which expose-all wins by construction.

**Evidence.** EXT-0002 l.26-27 (REQ-S1 summary in the brief extract; read by me); DPR-0004 section 6.1, section 4.2 row A1 ("dev runs and pilot (offline)").

**What would resolve it.** An explicit accounting rule for A1's offline cost (include / amortise over `N_dep` / report as a sensitivity), with `N_dep` listed as a regime variable and as a human decision; and a statement of the `N_dep` below which expose-all wins by this term alone.

**Response (proposer).** [round 1 reply, verbatim excerpt] "CONCEDE: CH-0025 (O_dev amortised over N_dep, declared by the human)." [rounds used: 1 of 2]

**Round 2 (challenger): WITHDRAW.** DPR-0004 section 6 item 5b states `O_dev` and the condition per-session net saving > `O_dev/N_dep`. Minor: item numbering duplicates "5", and `N_dep` is not in the section 6 list of human decisions.

**Outcome.** resolved (conceded).
