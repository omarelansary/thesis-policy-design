# Title: One benchmark carries five of six experiments, the concentration is not named, and agreement of the two rankings is read as corroboration

**Objection.**
1. *Concentration.* AppWorld is rank 1 for P3 (s7 L188), P2 (L200), E2E (L211), part of rank 1 for P5 (L182), and the base for P1 rank 1 ("over the P3 vehicle", L194, which after s8.1's P3 settlement means AppWorld). It is also P4 rank 3-4. That is five of six experiments on one benchmark. Sections 7-10 never say so. DPR-0010 s9 promises "an overall risk I name in section 10", but DPR-0010 s10 does not contain it, and the register's s10 does not either. The failure modes are common-mode: a floor on the route's models, a declared block that does not fit a window (CH-0159), contamination (CH-0162), a scorer defect, or a licence condition would hit P2, P3, P1, E2E and P5 together. The fallbacks named (MCP-Atlas keyless subset, constructed tasks) are not tied to triggers per experiment, and the MCP-Atlas fallback has its own design problems (CH-0163).
2. *Agreement is not corroboration.* s8.1 opens "They agree on the first choice for P5, P2 and E2E and on P1's construction." Both rankings were made from the same cards (s1-s6, I1, I2). The proposer states it did not re-read the stored pages (DPR-0010 s0, assumption 1), and says "any agreement is not evidence of correctness either" (DPR-0010 s10). A card error is therefore common to both rankings (example: the 114k-token figure for AppWorld, CH-0159). The register does not carry that caveat into s7-s8.
3. *"Agree" is overstated in two places.* (a) P5: the lead's frozen rank 1 is AppWorld definitions plus MCP definitions captured from the MCP-Atlas Docker image; the proposer ranked AppWorld plus constructed filler first, the captured MCP schemas third, and wrote that their "cost to run is the highest of the three, for a gain only in format fidelity" (DPR-0010 P5-3). s7 L182 merges them into one rank 1. (b) P1: the lead's frozen rank 1 builds sequences "from the same single-session benchmark as P3", and the lead's P3 was MCP-Atlas; the proposer's P1-1 is built over AppWorld. When P3 flipped to AppWorld in s8.1, the P1 base flipped with it, and "agree on P1's construction" hides this.

**Evidence.** [F] register L182, L188, L194, L200, L211, L219-222; lead-ranking-frozen.md lines 4-6 and 14-15; DPR-0010 s2 (P5-1 to P5-3), s4 (P1-1), s9, s10. [I] the common-mode reading.

**What would resolve it.** (a) A paragraph in s7 or s10 stating which experiments depend on AppWorld, which results would survive if its pilot fails (floor, window, scorer), and the fallback per experiment with its own known weakness. (b) A sentence in s8.1 that the two rankings used the same cards and that agreement is not evidence about the benchmarks. (c) Split P5 rank 1 into its two parts, or say that the lead's merge is a change from both rankings and why. (d) State in P1 that the base follows the P3 choice and that the lead's frozen choice differed.
