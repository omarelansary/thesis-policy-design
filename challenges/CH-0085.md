# Title: "Vendor performance numbers are not relied on" is contradicted by the design text, and EXT-0228's "future work" sentence is overstated as support for memory-informed offering

**Objection.** `04` section 8 says vendor performance numbers are not relied on. Yet (a) the K0 row of section 4 states that a large visible toolset "cost 2-5 points of resolution rate in Copilot's benchmarks (EXT-0228, vendor's own)" as an accuracy risk of the baseline; (b) HYP-0018 takes the same figure as its sole "why plausible"; (c) DPR-0008 section 4 uses the 69.0% static-core coverage as "the strongest evidence against S-min"; (d) HYP-0017, `03` row 69 and DPR-0008 section 7 D say "Copilot ... names remembering previous tool usage as its next step / future work". EXT-0228:71-73 says a model "should ... remember previous tool usage, infer intent from history, and plan multi-step actions over long sessions" and that routing mechanisms "could ... help them reason across thousands of turns"; the post's next step is to "explore how embeddings, memory, and reinforcement signals can combine". That is an aspiration about long-context reasoning, not a design for cross-session memory choosing which tools to offer. It supports "someone expects memory to matter", not the hit-rate premise of M2. Also the figures come from a vendor blog with no method, and the Anthropic preliminary figures (49% to 74%) were correctly excluded (`03` row 41), so the standard is not applied evenly.

**Evidence.** `04-design.md` section 4 (K0 row), section 8; EXT-0228:51, :59, :71-73; `hypotheses/HYP-0017.md` "Why it is plausible"; `hypotheses/HYP-0018.md`; `03-reuse-map.md:41, :53, :69`.

**What would resolve it.** Mark every use of the 2-5 points, 94.5/87.5/69.0 and the "future work" sentence as [vendor claim, motivation only], remove them from the K0 row comparison, and quote the sentence exactly instead of paraphrasing it as a memory plan.
