# Title: 03-comparison is not a fair comparison: G contains its comparators and cannot lose, one criterion row is decided by definition, C-B's evidence is mislabelled, and C-A is dismissed on grounds that apply equally to G's D2

**Grade: SERIOUS.**

**Objection.** (design/package-v3/03-comparison.md.)
1. **G is a portfolio, so "what would make it lose" is vacuous.** Table row "What would make it lose": for G "a simpler arm is feasible and cheaper at every size". But G's D4 returns that arm (including expose-all, OUT0). G then equals the arm and has not lost. G's only possible losses are selection error and the cost of the D4 tournament, neither of which is in the table. Comparing G with expose-all, C-A and vendor deferral as if peers is a category error: G's components are those rivals. The meaningful comparator of G is "the best fixed arm, chosen without a tournament", and the quantity at stake is the tournament's cost and regret (CH-0039, CH-0040).
2. **The tokens row is decided by the criterion.** G "reduced to what D4 selects"; expose-all "whole tool block every turn". Under C_tok an unrestricted catalogue loses by arithmetic once the tool block is large; this follows from the human's objective, not from evidence on G. The row should be flagged "decided by definition at large catalogue; discriminating only through the success constraint".
3. **Asymmetry on applicability (CH-0038 point 2).** "Yes with the core stage skipped" for G versus "no as specified" for C-A. Treated symmetrically, both reduce at large scale to search-only.
4. **Reasons 1-3 for demoting C-A apply to G's D2.** (1) Applicability: D2 is A1; skipped at large scale in both. (2) "A heuristic ordering with no optimality or safety property": G's D2 is that same ordering (`s_t / m_t` with the sweep), as the document says ("S1 of G"). (3) "A known pattern": so are search-only, one-level selection and expose-all. What survives is that G is defined with D4. Demoting C-A for the properties G inherits is not an argument for G over C-A; it is an argument for the portfolio. The document also refers to "the core stage S1 of G"; G has no node S1 (it is D2).
5. **The reviewed-versus-unreviewed asymmetry.** C-A passed 19 challenges (all conceded, see STATE for the caveat on that) and RV-0003; G has had none and was written by the lead. This is not a reason to prefer C-A; it is a reason not to present G's status as better established. I do not read 03 as dismissing C-A *too fast* on the large-catalogue regime (the demotion is defensible there, subject to CH-0038); I read it as *keeping the shape of C-A* (core + search + add-only, now in token form) under a new name while listing the properties of that shape as C-A's weaknesses.
6. **C-B's evidence is mislabelled.** 03 section 2: "an unreached tool is almost a certain failure in the benchmark that measured it (one model, intervals about +-8)". EXT-0053: reachability, on tasks the retriever never surfaced, gives "exactly one passed (0.05%)" across 1,905 attempts and pass^3 of 0.0% "for every model"; the one-model figures are the decomposition (20.0 and 8.0 points on minimax-m3); the ~+-8 is the Wilson interval of the pass-rate cells. Moreover the unreachable condition there has **no recovery path** (forced absence), so it says nothing about a design with search; v2 listed this threat ("forced absence is failure by construction"). And "retrieval-based narrowing lowered success in several settings" (02 section 2; 03 section 2) is, in EXT-0053, relative to a *curated* pool (about 8 tools containing the needed tools), which is a gold-derived pool a practitioner cannot build; relative to flat, rag:32 (36.7) and flat (36.0) tie on the one model that ran flat. "Several settings" other than EXT-0053 I could not check (not given).
7. **Vendor-deferral column.** "Hand-picked few tools plus search" matches EXT-0009 (keep 3-5 non-deferred). "Success risk: ... core miss" and "unmeasured by the record" are fair. "G's content over vendor deferral is the explicit D4 decision, the optional narrowing and trigger stages" (section 4) is honestly stated; add that at large catalogue G with core skipped and D4 selecting search-only *is* vendor deferral without the hand core.
8. **Cache row.** "A warm cache cuts billed cost per token on some providers but not tokens" is right (EXT-0062 hit rates; EXT-0009). No statement in 03 treats the Toollery multiplier as a price (checked).

**Evidence.** 03 read in full; EXT-0053 l.56-93 (read); EXT-0009 (read); EXT-0062 (read); 02 section 2 (read); RV-0003; STATE caveat (all 19 challenges conceded, from CLAUDE.md STATE text).

**What would resolve it.** Reframe 03 as "G's arms compared with one another" plus "G's procedure compared with a fixed best arm"; remove the vacuous row; correct the EXT-0053 description; give C-A-with-core-skipped the same applicability label as G; delete or define S1. An experiment would be needed to rank the arms (not authorised).
