# Title: "net above zero at some prevalence w in the claimed range" is near-vacuous without a source for the range, mixes units that the objective section rejects, and assumes transportability

**Grade: SERIOUS (checklist 5a/5b; the post-treatment correction itself is sound and I could not break it).**

**What I checked and found correct.** The strata-by-reference-partition correction (5a), and the break-even `w_SM = dU/(dR - dP + dU)`: from `net_S - net_M = w(dR - dP) - (1-w)dU > 0`, yes.

**Objection.**
1. **Vacuity.** HYP-0011 and HYP-0012 claim positivity "at some `w` in the claimed range". At `w = 1`, net recovery is `V - P_1`, positive whenever recovery restores anything; at `w = 0` it is `-U`. So the claim is true for any working recovery path if the range reaches high enough `w`, and for any mechanism with `dR - dP > 0` the same holds against another. The empirical content sits entirely in the range, which is a free human choice (DPR-0005 6.9) and can be tuned after seeing `w*`. No source for an independent estimate of the target population's prevalence is named. A falsifiable form compares the estimated `w*` (with interval) with an independently estimated prevalence interval of the target population, and fails only if the whole prevalence interval is on the wrong side.
2. **Units.** `net = w(V - P_1) - (1-w)U` adds success to cost; HYP-0012 admits this needs an exchange rate (F3) or the F1 admissible-region form. DPR-0004 section 6.1 rejects F3 because `lambda` has "no external anchor" and picks F1. The component falsifiers thus use a rule (F3) that the primary objective rejects; either state them under F1 (admissibility within `delta`, then cost) or list `lambda` as a required human input.
3. **Transportability.** Re-weighting by `w` assumes that `V`, `P_1` and `U` do not depend on which tools are missed or on how many misses the mix contains (a model that searches more in a miss-rich mix pays more in no-miss tasks). The within-stratum effects are not invariant to the composition of the missed tools (`q_hat` varies by tool). State it as an assumption and test it by varying the composition.
4. **FitText cannot calibrate this.** EXT-0035 gains are for a model told to retrieve (EXT-0065); I accept the proposer already states this.

**Evidence.** HYP-0011, HYP-0012 front matter; DPR-0005 sections 6.2, 6.3, 6.9; DPR-0004 section 6.1; SUM-0003 V5.

**What would resolve it.** Name the source of the prevalence range, phrase the falsifier against an independent prevalence estimate, resolve the F1/F3 mismatch, add the transportability assumption.

**Response (proposer).** [round 1 reply, verbatim excerpt] "CONCEDE: CH-0030 (w=1 vacuity; test moved to estimated prevalence w_hat with interval; curve over w is transportability only)." [rounds used: 1 of 2]

**Round 2 (challenger): DOWNGRADE serious -> minor, conditional on propagation.** DPR-0005 6.2 and HYP-0011's revision paragraph now test at the estimated `w_hat` (with interval) under F1 with no exchange rate, and the `w` curve is a transportability report: that answers points 1-3 for HYP-0011. Not propagated: HYP-0012's front-matter falsifier and its text ("at every prevalence w in the claimed range", net with success and cost added) are unchanged, and DPR-0005 6.6 rows "Recovery" and "Initiator" and 6.3 (ii)-(iii) still use "some w in the range". The vacuity argument applies to HYP-0012 identically. HYP-0011's front-matter falsifier is stated to be superseded by prose while left as written; a falsifier field that is not the operative falsifier invites misreading and should be replaced (with a `supersedes` note) rather than left.

**Outcome.** resolved in substance for HYP-0011; residual minor for HYP-0012, 6.3 and 6.6.
