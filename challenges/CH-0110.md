# Title: "the seed break-even is set by session length and matches more than by price" is contradicted by the K = 1 column at T = 30 and T = 100

**Objection.** Reading 4 and `11` s5 bullet 4 give correct ranges (I checked all of the quoted K = 3 and K = 1 ranges against Table C). The conclusion drawn is not supported where price decides the outcome:
- K = 1, T = 100: h* is 0.54 (r 0.02), 0.76 (0.05), 0.94 (0.10) and "never" for r >= 0.14. I recomputed r = 0.14, w = 1.0: saving = 6,550 x 0.14 + 5 x 150 - 250 x 0.14 x (99 - 50) = 917 + 750 - 1,715 = -48 < 0, so "never"; at r = 0.10: 655 + 750 - 1,225 = 180, h* = 2,725 / 2,905 = 0.938. A difference between "pays above 54% precision" and "never pays" is a price effect, not a minor one.
- K = 1, T = 30: 0.33 (r 0.02) to 0.67 (r 1.0), a 2x range, the same size as the change over T at fixed r (r 0.02: 0.23 at T=3 to 0.54 at T=100).
- T and r interact: at r = 1.0 h* goes from 0.09 (T = 3) to 0.66 (T = 30) and is "never" at T = 100; at r = 0.02 it goes from 0.23 to 0.54 (K = 1, w = 1.0). "Depends on T more than on r" is not a statement the table supports without saying at which r.
`11` itself reports the "never" cells in the same bullet, so the text carries both facts; the lead clause and the Consequence paragraph (design conclusions do not depend on price) do not.

**Evidence.** `calc/price_sensitivity.stdout.txt` L37-51 (K = 1), L53-67 (K = 3); formula `calc/price_sensitivity.py` L30-34.

**What would resolve it.** Restrict the sentence to K = 3, or rewrite: "for K = 1 and long sessions (T >= 30) whether seeding pays depends on r; for K = 3 or T <= 10 r moves h* by less than T and K do". Do not extend this to the Consequence paragraph without that qualification.
