# Title: DPR-0003 section 2a says P4g or P3 "follow from the record"; they follow from what vendors ship, and the FitText initiator label has swung from "system" to "model" on partial windows

**Objection.** (Answers the proposer's open question (a).)
1. **"Candidates that follow from this record are P4g ... or P3."** Both are entirely model-initiated. Their dominant risk is initiation (HYP-0004), for which the record holds no measurement (DPR-0001 R3, DPR-0002 E) and several statements consistent with under-initiation (EXT-0048: 40.1% of o3 Pro's failed trajectories contain no tool invocation; EXT-0047). Their recall bottleneck is the one EXT-0045/0046 measures for JIT loading, and EXT-0053 reports that on tasks the retriever never surfaced pass^3 is 0.0%, a reachability gate that applies equally to a group that is never opened. What the record contains in favour of P4g/P3 is vendor documentation (EXT-0007, 0009, 0010: designs vendors ship, "trained to search those surfaces"), which is not effectiveness evidence (CAVEATS row 8; ledger C3-10). The same record is equally consistent with P0/P1 (ComplexMCP, Toollery, EXT-0053's flat 36.0 vs rag:32 36.7) and with P2 plus recovery. AUTH-004 says "Avoid selecting a method merely because it appears ... in the earlier research." Writing that P4g or P3 "follow from this record" selects the vendor form by that route. My position: DPR-0003 as a template is adequate for 2026-10-21 provided the package says it is a hypothesis class, lists P4g and P3 as "the forms vendors ship", states that the record does not discriminate among P0, P1, P2+recovery, P3, P4g, and names the experiment that would. The choice of a committed form is the human's; the sentence should not pre-empt it.
2. **FitText initiator, now over-corrected.** The proposer first took the lead's map's "system-initiated" as fact; after EXT-0050/0051 it takes "the model emits the request and the system executes the retrieval". EXT-0050 itself says "The trigger mechanism and its implementation are detailed in section 4.2", which nobody has extracted. EXT-0052, extracted by the lead, shows another trigger: "Dynamic retrieval triggers when: max SequenceSim(o, m) < 0.82 and o not-subset-of m for all m in M" (a threshold of FitText's own, over an observation `o` and a set `M` whose definitions are not in the window), in an appendix about backtracking search. So the paper states at least two trigger descriptions: an agent-emitted pseudo-tool description (EXT-0050/0051) and a system-side similarity condition (EXT-0052). Whether they are the same mechanism, alternatives, or belong to different experiments is not in any extract. HYP-0006's current text ("no direct evidence found for a system-side trigger") and P7's "FitText supports ... not a purely system-side trigger" both go beyond the windows. Honest label: [U: FitText describes both an agent-emitted request and a system-side novelty condition; section 4.2 not read]. Needs the lead to extract section 4.2 and the definition of `o`, `M`, `m` (I cannot).
3. The FitText numbers also carry the point that the largest gain is for the strongest model (EXT-0035: delta grows with capability), which is evidence about a model that emits useful requests, relevant to HYP-0004 and not noted there.

**Evidence.** DPR-0003 section 2a (L87); EXT-0007, 0009, 0010, 0035, 0045, 0046, 0047, 0048, 0050, 0051, 0052, 0053 (all read by me); ledger C3-10; CAVEATS row 8; AUTH-004 "Scientific approach".

**What would resolve it.** (1) Reword 2a as above. (2) Label the FitText initiator [U] and cite EXT-0050/0051/0052 as three partial windows. (3) Lead: extract FitText section 4.2 (the trigger definition) and the definition of `o`, `M`, `m` in EXT-0052's context, so that HYP-0006's basis is read at the source.
