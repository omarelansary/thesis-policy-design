# Title: the expose-all reference `pi_0` that F1 depends on is not measurable in the regime where the problem is largest, and F1 constrains a mean

**Objection.**
1. F1 is `P(pi) >= P(pi_0) - delta` with `pi_0` "the expose-all baseline" (sections 3 and 6). Expose-all requires the full catalogue to fit in the model's context. The governing record states the flat catalogue on the closest vehicle is about 292k tokens, "over the context of 2 of the 4 matrix models", and that "the all-tools baseline exists only for long-context models". The supervisor's own illustration is 10,000 tools (E7, an example, not a specification). In exactly the large-`n` regime that motivates the thesis, `P(pi_0)` may not exist for the models in question. DPR-0001 does not say what the constraint refers to then. The usual substitute (a curated or gold-derived pool) uses gold information as a reference (acceptable in the scorer) but changes the claim: S4D quality addendum notes that the "curated" reference "does not meet the all-tools baseline". "Maintaining performance relative to all tools" (EXT-0002 s1.1/s1.4) becomes "relative to an oracle-curated pool", which is a weaker and different claim and favours restriction.
2. F1 and F2 constrain mean success. A mean can be maintained while a stratum (tasks needing a rarely used tool) is sacrificed, and the asymmetry (an omitted tool is a hard failure; an extra tool is a soft cost) is exactly what the recovery question is about. The formulation table does not list a stratified or worst-stratum variant, though S4G section 5.1 separates miss and no-miss strata.
3. Over-count of the opposite kind: F3 is marked as contradicting "no arbitrary thresholds unless `lambda` is swept", but F1's `delta` is the same kind of free parameter; only F4 avoids a free parameter, and the draft then notes F4 "does not by itself yield 'a policy'". The trade-off is stated but the table does not say which formulations remain usable when `pi_0` is undefined. [I] Only F4 and a reference-free F2 do; this should be stated.

**What would resolve it.** DPR states, per formulation, what the reference is when `pi_0` is infeasible and what claim "maintained" then supports; adds a stratified variant (miss / no-miss) or states why not.
