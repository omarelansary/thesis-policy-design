# Title: The headline ratios and boundaries (2-30x, 10-75 tools, R-small below 10-25 tools) hold only for small D and one definition size, and are stated without those conditions

**Objection.** (1) The "factor of about 2-30 (D-01 table, T = 10)" in the R-mid row corresponds to D = 1 to 3 discoveries per session; the table prints no D = 10 ratio although D = 10 is used for n*. [O, hand] At n = 100, d = 250, T = 10, r = 0.1, D = 10: deferral 4,882.5 + 25,625 = 30,507.5 against expose-all 53,750, ratio about 0.57, i.e. 1.8x. (2) R-small is "fewer than about 10-25 tools" but n* at D = 10 is 40-74 (`regime_bill.stdout.txt:118-127`); the boundary depends on D and on d (n* is printed only for d = 250), so it belongs in tokens and discoveries, not in a tool count; at d = 100 the table at n = 20 gives ratio 1.00 (D = 1) and 2.21 (D = 3) (`:4`). (3) D has no empirical basis anywhere in V5 (Copilot's 69.0% static coverage would imply a miss rate near a third of tool needs, but that is a vendor number the packet says it does not rely on). (4) Inputs held fixed with no stated source or sensitivity: w = 1.25 (no 2x one-hour write), o = 5, H = 5,000, q = 150, K = 3, core = 5, search = 300; the category index charged in HYP-0019 is absent from the deferral prefix. (5) "Whatever the cache read price" (`04` section 1 point 1) is shown for r only; w and the no-cache case are not varied.

**Evidence.** `calc/regime_bill.py:18, :54-61`; `calc/regime_bill.stdout.txt:4, :21-37, :118-127`; `04-design.md` sections 1, 2.

**What would resolve it.** Print ratios and n* over D in {0, 1, 3, 10}, d in {100, 250, 1,000}, w in {1.25, 2}, and the no-cache case; give D a stated range with its source or label it a free parameter; restate the regime table in tokens and D.
