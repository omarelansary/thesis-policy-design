# Title: claim C10 attributes 13.5% of spend to tool-list changes, but the source attributes it to all cache-bust events

**Objection.** 01 C10 (L48) says "Mid-session tool-list changes re-billed 13.5% of spend in 506 local session logs". The stored text says: "2,766 cache-bust events re-billed 224.4 million input tokens ... 1,399 mid-session tool/skill/MCP-list mutations ... mutations occurred across 191 of 506 sessions (38%) ... measured impact: 13.5% of total Claude spend in the corpus". There are about twice as many busts (2,766) as mutations (1,399), and the 13.5% is attached to the busts. The source does not say what share of the 13.5% is due to mutations. The map row (03 L20) lists the numbers separately, which is closer, but its row title is "Cost of mid-session tool-list changes in production" and its verdict is "REUSE as the failure mode to design against". Further limits not carried into C10: it is a comment in an open issue (status Open, labels bug, area:cost), one user's logs, on a subscription plan (the issue opening says "5x Max plan"), in a client (Claude Code) not an API caller. The same issue shows the full rewrite of about 783K tokens fifteen seconds after `deferred_tools_delta` events (L86-L91), i.e. in the vendor's own client with deferral on, which is in tension with C2 (deferral leaves the prefix untouched, 01 L40). Neither 01 nor 03 mentions this tension.

**Evidence.** L/v5-ccissue-92033-cachebust-20261022.txt L31, L52, L83-L91, L180-L190; 01 L48, L40; 03 L20.

**What would resolve it.** Re-word C10 to "cache-bust events (of which tool, skill and MCP-list mutations were 1,399 events) re-billed 13.5% of spend; attribution to tool-list changes alone is not given". Add one sentence on the deferred_tools_delta observation next to C2, and say whether it is read as a client bug, a different mechanism from the API's `defer_loading`, or unexplained [U].
