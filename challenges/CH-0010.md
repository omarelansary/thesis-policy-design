# Title: the provisional policy is a template with unfilled slots; its N2 is the comparator P2 that DPR-0002 says is not a policy; it is a union for ablation, not a minimum

**Objection.**
1. **Slots, not decisions.** AUTH-004 requires that a proposed policy "make its decisions interpretable and testable. Explain what determines each decision". DPR-0003's table gives, for N2, "A single selector scoring non-core tools on the query ... selector may be lexical, embedding or LLM"; width `m` unset; grade `g` rule unset. For N4, the triggers S16-S19 are readings by a classifier of unspecified origin. The determinants are slots. Any outcome can then be attributed to how a slot is filled, and the policy as a single hypothesis cannot be wrong; only filled instances can.
2. **N2 is P2.** DPR-0002 section C says of P2: "This is not 'a policy' in the sense of REQ-O14-08; it is a comparator." N2 is P2 (query-conditioned top-`m` with unset scorer) plus a graded-list option. The only place where the provisional policy adapts to "context and scenario" (REQ-O14-09/E5) is recovery; width and grade are fixed per run. The DPR does not argue why N2 differs from the fixed top-N the supervisor says is not a policy, other than adding recovery.
3. **Not a minimum.** The brief asks for the minimum number of nodes the logic needs. N3 (model-initiated) and N4 (system-initiated) are both included; the fallback requirement as recorded ("some way for the LM to query the tools that is in the passive registry", EXT-0002 s1.2) is satisfied by one path, model-initiated. N4 is added on FitText evidence (see CH-0012) and the supervisor's stage-five example (E8, class `supervisor-example`). The "Minimal-nodes statement" shows each node is removable by ablation, not that each is needed. N1 contains an undecided element ("cheap-grade list?"). The structure is the union of the pieces the evidence touched.
4. **Absent from the flow.** R-cache forbids removal during a session, so exposure is monotone non-decreasing after D0 and each false-positive re-add (N4) is permanent for the session; "reducing unnecessary exposure" then happens only once, at D0. The cost of this accumulation is in the FP list but there is no node for removal and no argument that removal is never worth a cache miss (see CH-0015 on provider differences).

**Evidence.** DPR-0003 section 1 (diagram and node table, "Minimal-nodes statement"), DPR-0002 section C (P2) and section E; EXT-0002 s1.2 (fallback wording); INT-T-001 E5, E8; AUTH-004 "Design requirements" ("interpretable and testable").

**What would resolve it.** Either fill the slots with a named default per node (not numbers: a family and a stated reason, as in CH-0009 item 1) or state that DPR-0003 is a hypothesis class and not a policy and drop "policy" from its title; say which of N3/N4 the requirement forces and which is exploratory; state what distinguishes N2 from the fixed top-N the supervisor excludes.

**Response (proposer).** [verbatim, round-1 reply, 2026-10-16; rounds used: 1 of 2] "CH-0010 CONCEDE. DPR-0003 section 2a says it is a template, N2 alone is P2 (not a policy under REQ-O14-08), N3 and N4 are not both required; candidate committed forms that follow from the record are P4g or P3, which I do NOT choose between."

**Outcome.** items 1-3 resolved by section 2a, except that the "Proposal" paragraph and diagram header still say "smallest ... minimum nodes" (CH-0017 item 5), and the sentence "candidate committed forms that follow from the record are P4g or P3" is disputed (CH-0019 item 1). Item 4 (monotone exposure) unanswered (CH-0017 item 6).
