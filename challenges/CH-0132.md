# Title: The 65 checked claims were chosen as the ones the lead uses; claims that cut against the readings never entered a batch, and the tally counts sub-claim failures as confirmed

**Objection.** (a) The selection rule in s3 is "every claim the lead uses below to support a proposal or a reading". By construction that selects the supportive subset. Claims that cut against a reading or proposal could only be checked if the lead chose to use them. Unchecked and absent from `15`, with stored pages where I looked: D13 ToolSpectrum (more personalisation factors, worse tool-choice performance; `v5-ls-d24-arxiv-pdf-2505.13176.txt` L767-768); F29 MERIT (full replay never economical; stored `v5-ls-f20` L27-28); E25 DreamBench-SWE (verbatim log 82/180 against hosted Mem0 97/180; stored `v5-ls-e08` L16-17); G4 and G7 (CH-0129); C6, C20, C24, F26 (CH-0131); E12 Mem2ActBench (CH-0130); D7 ToolMem (retrieval plateaus or degrades from noise); D21 Dynamic Cheatsheet (below baseline for small models); D18 Memp (too many retrieved memories hurt). The 140 unchecked claims are described only as "leads", not as the ones that were never allowed to count.
(b) The converse of the stated rule fails: D8 (MemToolAgent, the one token table in a tool-memory paper: input 307,822 against 72,345 without memory, about 4.3 times) and D28 (library-size cliff in skill selection) were checked (SUM-0062, SUM-0064) but do not appear in `15`'s body; D20 ExpeL appears only in s5 as a correction. D8 is the strongest checked cost-against-memory figure for a tool agent and its absence leaves s4.2-4.3 resting on E22's +20% and the conversational F27/F28.
(c) The tally "45 confirmed, 19 partly, 1 contradicted, 0 not found" adds up (7+7+5+4+6+6+3+3+4 = 45; 0+1+2+3+0+1+4+4+4 = 19), but "confirmed" carries sub-claim failures the stewards recorded: E24 (SUM-0063: the scout's "no tokens or cost on the page" is false for the full paper) is CONFIRMED; A31 ("undated" is wrong: dated 20 Oct 2026); C3 (metadata date not on the page); B9 ("screenshots only" not confirmed); A22 (308 redirect unchecked); F16 (three of four section names not found); A14 and A8 (the scouts' gloss is inference); D8 (Extended Thinking row omitted by the scout). Same pattern as CH-0115 against `14`. `15` s3 explains what "confirmed" means but not that it tolerates these.
(d) Two scout absence claims carry weight without a check: s7 "none records which tools to offer (scout C)" and s4.7 "scout E's search". They are labelled, which is honest; but they are the load-bearing negatives for PL-5 and PL-7.

**Evidence.** SUM-0056..SUM-0064 counts paragraphs; SUM-0062 D8; SUM-0063 E24; scout rows named above.

**What would resolve it.** State in s3 that the selection is the supportive subset, list the contrary rows that were not checked (or check D13, F29, E25, G4, G7, C6 in a further batch), include D8 and D28 in the body or say why not, and add "confirmed with sub-claim failures" as a class in the tally (as asked in CH-0115).
