# Title: Section 9 misstates V5 in one place and omits three proposals the independent ranking raised

**Objection.**
1. *Item 2 uses "above n*" inconsistently.* It says "no benchmark has a catalogue above n* with recurring tools across sessions". s4 revision 2 gives n* of 11-14 (D = 1), 19-28 (D = 3), 47-77 (D = 10); ATRBench (74 tools) and VitaBench's 66 are above n* for D up to 3 and in range for D = 10 (DPR-0010 X4). The inspector's finding (I2 item 7) is "more than about 100 tools". The sentence should use the register's working reading ("at least 100 definitions") or it overstates. The threshold itself ("100") is the lead's working reading and decides the rejection of every native multi-session candidate; its sensitivity (what ranks if the cut were 60) is not given.
2. *Omitted: P2's subset mechanism.* `05` P2 says the subset arm runs "via the route P0 found"; STATE and `10` record that `allowed_tools` was rejected on the route. DPR-0010 P2-1 flagged that the subset arm needs another mechanism (the design question P2 itself tests). s7 P2 does not mention this and s9 does not report it, although it determines whether P2 can use AppWorld's or MCP-Atlas's "native" subset (the native subsets are task-specific ENABLED_TOOLS or a predicted 20 APIs, and V5's route cannot enforce them at the provider).
3. *Omitted: the loop's 20 tasks per arm against the pools.* DPR-0010 s8 item 4: `05` s1 fixes about 20 tasks per arm while the candidate pools are 60-165. The human may want to know that the loop size is a cost choice, not an availability limit; s9 item 3 discusses only E2E.
4. *Omitted: R-large and d = 250 have no stated basis.* DPR-0010 s8 items 5-6 (d = 250 sits in one benchmark's range; R-large has no numeric size). s9 item 4 covers tokens per definition partly but not R-large's missing numeric meaning, which changes the candidate set (hundreds of tools against thousands; AppWorld's full block already near or above a 128k window, CH-0159).
5. *Item 1 wording.* `05` already says BFCL's design is "adapted to deferral" and "a candidate design assessed in `17`, not a choice"; item 1 presents the text as if it were a choice of BFCL. The substance (BFCL appends to the tools list; V5-R02 forbids editing tools) is correct, so this is a framing point only.
6. *The lead's own flags.* The frozen ranking listed F7 "floor screening only in exploration" and F9 (memory placement differs in all multi-session benchmarks, no change). F7 conflicts with s4 revision 2 (floor/ceiling as a hard requirement via a baseline pilot) and the register does not say the flag was dropped or why; F9 is not in s9.

**Evidence.** [F] register L94, L251-257; I2 item 7; `05` s1, s2 P2; DPR-0010 s5 (P2-1), s8 items 4-6; lead-ranking-frozen.md L36. STATE (allowed_tools rejected, `10` s7). [I] the consequences.

**What would resolve it.** Correct item 2's wording; add three proposals (P2 mechanism; loop size; R-large and d numeric basis) or say why each is not a proposal; state the sensitivity of the "100" cut; say what became of F7 and F9.
