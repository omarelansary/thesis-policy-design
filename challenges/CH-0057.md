# Title: "if no stage pays, the answer is 'no stage'" and "no arm established" are different outcomes, and the protocol's own power table makes the second the default

**Grade: SERIOUS (interpretation of the proposal's main promise).** (Brief sentence 5, section 6 item 1; 02 sections 3, 5, 7.)

**Objection.**
1. Brief sentence 5: "If no stage pays, the answer is 'no stage', and we will say so". Figure 2's outcomes: "a configuration, or no arm established (not shown noninferior is not shown inferior)". Under the stage-inclusion rule a component is kept only if its arm is feasible *and* cheaper; "feasible" means noninferiority *shown*. With the sample sizes in 02 section 7 (500-2,300 pairs per contrast and per stratum for margins of about five points; the vehicle offers 150 and 750 tasks) most arms will fail to show noninferiority for lack of power. Then no component is "kept", and the report would be "no stage kept" or "not assessed", which looks the same as "no stage pays" but means "the test could not tell". The brief tells the supervisor the former while 02 section 7 implies the latter is the likely default. The same text, in the same document, warns against reading "not detected" as "equal".
2. The sentence also fixes the null as the answer, which is the opposite error to the one the document corrects: a non-finding is reported as a finding ("no stage"), to a supervisor who has said findings are needed for a master's thesis (E3).
3. This is independent of the question of power for a particular margin; it is a statement about how outcomes are named. It also affects the brief's section 7 "What would count against the proposal", which lists findings ("expose-all... feasible and cheapest everywhere we can test") that the planned data could not establish at small margins.

**Evidence.** Brief lines 19, 85, 98; 02 lines 60, 79, 105; intake E3 line 31.

**What would resolve it.** Name the three outcomes separately in the brief ("shown not to pay", "not shown to pay (underpowered)", "pays"), and say which the planned sample sizes can reach for which margins. Answerable now.

**Response (lead).** ACCEPT: brief sentence 5 and objection 1 separate "not established" from "no stage pays". [rounds used: 1 of 2]

**Round 2 (challenger, 2026-10-19): CLOSED for sentence 5 and section 6 item 1; the remaining defect is tracked in CH-0049 (R2).** Sentence 5 now says the outcome may be "not established" and that the two are different findings. But sentence 4 ("kept only if ... feasible ... and not shown more expensive") makes underpowered data keep components by default, the reverse default; see CH-0049 round 2.

**Outcome.** resolved (conceded); residual carried by CH-0049.
