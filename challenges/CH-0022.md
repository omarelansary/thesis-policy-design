# Title: A1 Step 1 is inconsistent with the proposer's own cost expressions (discovery width, cadence)

**Grade: SERIOUS (A1 is the only new decision content; errors in its form bias F in known directions).**

**Objection.**
1. **Discovery width.** Step 1 prices a discovery at `d_s + (w + r*T_rem)*L_t`: one definition. A3 appends the top-`kappa` definitions, and DPR-0005 section 4 (add-only row) prices a discovery at `kappa*L_bar*(w + r*T_rem)`. The `kappa-1` unneeded definitions are written once and read for the rest of the session (the "false-positive appends persist" cost the proposer itself names). Omitting them understates the cost of deferral, so Step 1 defers too much, and the bias grows with `kappa`.
2. **Cadence.** The visible side `r*L_t*T_bar` assumes the prefix is warm when a session starts, i.e. requests arrive within `Lambda` of each other across sessions (DPR-0005 section 3.3 says the stable-surface advantage depends on exactly this; EXT-0061 "depend on cache state and traffic reuse"). `Lambda` is defined in section 1 but is not an input of A1 and has no row in the runtime table. If sessions are sparser than `Lambda`, a kept tool also pays `w*L_t` per session; Step 1 then keeps too many tools. The two biases have opposite signs by regime, so the rule's validity region is not stated.
3. **Independence.** Step 1 treats tools independently with an additive `d_s`; one search that surfaces several co-needed tools shares `d_s`, so co-used tools are kept visible more often than the break-even says. Minor.
4. **`T_bar` is policy-dependent** (discovery adds turns), i.e. a post-treatment quantity used as an input. Minor.

**Evidence.** DPR-0004 section 1 (vocabulary: `Lambda`, `kappa`), section 4.2 rows A1 and A3; DPR-0005 section 3.3 and section 4 (add-only row); EXT-0057 (lifetime refresh); EXT-0061 ("traffic reuse").

**What would resolve it.** Correct the discovery term to the appended width; add a cadence input (`g` vs `Lambda`) with a runtime-table row, or state the regime (warm, `g < Lambda`) as an applicability condition of A1 in A0; state which direction each omitted term biases `F`.

**Response (proposer).** [round 1 reply, verbatim excerpt] "CONCEDE: CH-0022 (kappa-1 extra definitions and cold-prefix probability pi_c from g vs Lambda now in A1)." [rounds used: 1 of 2]

**Round 2 (challenger): WITHDRAW points 1 and 2.** I re-derived the new Step 1: `keep_t = (L_t - c_def,t)[pi_c w + r(T - pi_c)]` equals `pi_c(w + r(T-1)) + (1-pi_c) r T` per unit of definition, correct; `disc_t` now carries `(kappa-1) L_D`, consistent with DPR-0005 section 4. Points 3 (co-needed tools share a search) and 4 (`T_bar` is policy-dependent) were not answered; both were minor and remain as stated limits. `pi_c` has no row in the section 5 table.

**Outcome.** resolved (conceded).
