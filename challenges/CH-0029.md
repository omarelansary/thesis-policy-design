# Title: HYP-0008's falsifiers are satisfiable by imprecision, and the existence and monotonicity parts are partly true by construction

**Grade: SERIOUS (checklist 5c asks for a correct treatment of 'no inversion observed').**

**Objection.**
1. **Part (a) is "no inversion observed" in a sweep.** The falsifier of the existence claim is "no cell has a 95% interval wholly above zero". Interval width decides this: Toollery natural has half-width ~0.0005 against a full-library mean of 0.00075 (EXT-0061, EXT-0062), i.e. ~70% of the mean, and a sweep of such cells would pass the "no inversion" falsifier through lack of precision alone. The AUTH-005 criticism was that absence of observation is not evidence; a sweep with no precision requirement repeats it. A precision requirement (the half-width must be below the smallest inversion that matters, set by the human) is needed for "falsified" to mean anything, otherwise the outcome is "inconclusive".
2. **Existence is arithmetic, not an empirical hypothesis.** Under the linear model of DPR-0005 section 3.1, an inversion exists whenever `h_r < h_f` falls below `h_r*`; the point estimate of Toollery natural already is one. The empirical content is whether the *assumptions* of the model hold: minimum cacheable length, entries shared across sessions, a write premium, extra turns. These are not the subject of the falsifier.
3. **Part (b) (monotone at fixed hit rates)** is true by construction (cost linear in tokens); a failure of the "prediction within interval" test can only come from the omitted terms of section 1 (history, output, distraction, extra turns), so the falsifier tests the omissions, which should be named as the claim. Also, if `r` and `w` are fitted on the same cells whose differences are then "predicted", the test is in-sample; they must be estimated on separate calibration traffic.

**Evidence.** HYP-0008 front matter; DPR-0005 sections 2, 3.1, 6.4; EXT-0061 (CI), EXT-0062; SUM-0003 V3.

**What would resolve it.** State a precision requirement in the falsifier; restate (b) as a claim about named omitted terms; require `r`, `w` from separate calibration requests.

**Response (proposer).** [round 1 reply, verbatim excerpt] "PARTIAL: CH-0029 (conceded imprecision/linearity: equivalence margin and separate calibration of r, w added to HYP-0008; (a) kept only as the lead's verified-instance criterion)." [rounds used: 1 of 2]

**Round 2 (challenger): DOWNGRADE serious -> minor.** The substantive fixes are in HYP-0008's "Revision after CH-0029" paragraph (equivalence margin; separate calibration). The front-matter falsifier, which is the operative field of the record, is unchanged, and DPR-0005 6.6 row "Cost response" still reads "no verified inversion in the sweep (a); prediction outside the interval (b)" without the precision requirement. Keeping (a) as the lead's verified-instance criterion is acceptable if the record says that the sweep result is "inconclusive" when half-widths exceed the margin; the paragraph says so, the falsifier field does not.

**Outcome.** resolved in substance; residual minor (propagate to the falsifier field and 6.6).
