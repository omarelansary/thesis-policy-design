# Title: The "unchanged" list asserts things the record never examined, and conflicts with PL-6 and the cache-mechanics material it did collect

**Objection.** (a) "The regimes and break-evens (S2, D-01)" are listed as unchanged, while PL-6 proposes extending D-04 with write ratios 2.0 and 2.5 and a storage price, and PL-2/PL-6 add a W term. The arithmetic has not been rerun, so "unchanged" is a prediction. `11` says price matters at T = 3 (r moves n* by 1.5-2x), so a w of 2.0-2.5 may move short-session break-evens. The list should say "not rerun".
(b) "Deferral with append-only discovery" is unchanged, but the record collected, and `15` does not weigh, rows that bear on it: F5 (stored `v5-ls-f02-claude-tool-use-tool-search-tool.txt` L244: "You still send every tool's full definition in the tools array on every request, including the deferred ones"; a deferred tool cannot carry `cache_control`, per the scout row), A4 (without tool search a deny removes the definition and invalidates the cache), B19 (OpenCode v2 makes Code Mode the default and moves MCP tools off the native list), F3/F13 (a prefix below the minimum cacheable length, or a Gemini append under 2,050 tokens, silently is not cached). All unchecked. "No change" may be right; the record gives no basis for it.
(c) "Observation masking as an arm only" has no evidence in `15` s4. The scouts' rows F8 (Anthropic context editing: clearing invalidates the prefix, `clear_at_least`), F30 (a published break-even within 10 turns for trimming under caching, a 76-session single-user case study), B23 (SWE-agent `polling`), B25 (OpenHands threshold-triggered condensation, vendor claim) are the landscape's evidence on exactly this and are not mentioned, checked, or used to support or weaken the "arm only" position.

D28 was checked and dropped.
(e) "Nothing found argues for adopting Mem0 ..." sits in the same list; see CH-0131.
