# Title: Brief row heading and scope omissions on ToolRet, Mem2ActBench, CacheRouter, adaptive retrieval

**Grade: MINOR.**

**Objection / checks.**
1. `01` row heading "Models call tools when none fits, and cannot yet be assumed to ask for the right one unprompted": the second half has no figure in the row (none of the cited rates is about requesting a tool); the row's own note says "not as a rate of search initiation". The heading states more than the cells. Also the row merges three constructs (nonexistent, irrelevant visible, similar-wrong visible) that `05` section 1 B insists be kept apart (CH-0061), and "1.0%-96.3% ... hallucinated a tool" inherits CH-0069.
2. **ToolRet** 62.00 (oracle) vs 50.60 (retrieved), "11.4-point drop": numbers verified (arxiv-2503.01763.txt:1539-1541, 1625); scope omitted: GPT-3.5 only, ToolBench-G1, retriever bge-large; G2 oracle 64.20 shown. The drop is one model and one retriever, not "the effect of retrieval quality" in general. NV-Embed-v1 33.83 nDCG@10 verified (:142, 850, 1489).
3. **Mem2ActBench** candidate sets N in {1, 2, 5}, each query paired with N-1 distractors (arxiv-2601.19935.txt:1034-1036): verified. Result relevant to scale: hard negatives lower tool-selection accuracy 94.50 to 69.75 at N=5 (:1059-1062) while random negatives leave it at 93.50-95.50 (:1047-1049, 1082-1084). With at most 5 candidates it says nothing about catalogue-scale memory; `05` says so ("Gap unchanged"). Fine.
4. **CacheRouter** 90.99% (EXT-0112:171-173) over 55 queries (:81) and 95.2% (227,200 / 238,701, :234); 12.0% and 8.0% of no-cache cost under hit:miss 1:30 reproduce arithmetically ((0.9099 + 0.0901 x 30) / 30 = 12.0%; (0.952 + 0.048 x 30) / 30 = 7.95%). The cost is computed from DeepSeek's stated ratio (:174-176), as `05` says. Note the hit rate is dominated by a fixed prefix over many queries; it is not a contrast of loading policies.
5. **Adaptive retrieval** 7.0% (GSM8K) and 38.8% (MATH-500): verified (arxiv-2602.07213.txt:135-137, 204); model Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct, zero-shot prompted (:61-62, 89-90). Retrieval is over a text corpus for maths, not tool discovery. The appendix prompt (A.2) was **not read**, so "prompted" versus "told when to search" is unchecked.

**What would resolve it.** Edits only (items 1, 2); item 5 needs a read of the appendix prompt.
