# Title: s6 verdicts mean "the quote matches", the closing reading treats them as measured error rates, and the audited set is selected by the search for criticism

**Objection.**
(1) *Verdict meaning.* s6 defines "Verdicts: C confirmed, P partly, U unconfirmed" and the heading says "checked against stored pages". The stewards' verdicts (SUM-0067..0078) test whether the scout's wording is on the stored page, not whether the criticism is true. Examples: "tau2-bench ... a stream of wrong-ground-truth issues (#708-#716)" is C, but the stored page is a title list; SUM-0077 E11 says "'Wrong ground truth' is the scout's characterisation", and seventeen of the listed issues were opened on one day by one user (Anirudhaagrawal). MCPMark #272 is C, but it is one reporter's computation on deprecated v1-0905 logs. Toolathlon #98 is P and the reporter says the list was assembled with agent help and may be inaccurate. s1.1 promises to keep criticism apart "from what this workspace has confirmed itself", but s6 has no column for what the workspace confirmed (nothing was re-run; the inspectors confirmed counts, not defects).
(2) *Reading [I].* "Every benchmark that has been audited shows a scorer or ground-truth error rate of roughly one in ten to one in two of the inspected items." The numbers are not one measure: Tool-Veritas counts evaluator-human disagreement for one agent model per benchmark (Kimi-K2.6 on tau2 Retail, MiniMax-M2.7 on the others) over unequal samples of 112, 200, 89, 95, and the 18.5% is a pooled figure (SUM-0067 A9; SUM-0077 E1). Epoch counts "defects that could affect accuracy", with a rating rule at 20% accuracy impact and a stopping rule ("We stopped inspecting once we discovered errors that exceeded our published threshold", SUM-0077 E12); its category rates (20% to 70%) rest on 5, 5, 5, 10, 10 and 15 items (SUM-0070 B8), so "70% (memory)" is 7 of 10. SWE-bench's "floor of 16.4%" is a floor derived from 27.6% audited. LoCoMo's 6.4% is below one in ten. The s6 row for BFCL omits the denominators.
(3) *Selection.* The audited set is the set a criticism search surfaces (scout E started from "a seed list drawn from the workspace", s2.1) plus a few audit papers whose authors sell replacements (Tool-Veritas, Harness Lab). "Every benchmark that has been audited" is therefore a statement about benchmarks that attracted an audit, and the audits that exist are not a sample of benchmarks. The register's own caveat (absence of criticism is absence of scrutiny) applies equally to the inference "all audited ones are bad".

**Evidence.** `17` s6 L124-153; SUM-0077 E1, E11, E12, E17; SUM-0070 B8; SUM-0067 A9; SUM-0078 E18, E23; SUM-0074 C36.
[F] the numbers and denominators. [I] my reading of the verdict column and of selection. [U] whether the lead's table header was meant as quote-fidelity; if so the closing reading still goes beyond it.

**What would resolve it.** (a) Rename the verdict column "wording on the stored page" and add a column "reproduced or checked by this workspace: yes/no/how". (b) Give each rate with its numerator, denominator, number of models, what "error" means, and a version. (c) Replace the closing sentence by "audits found disagreement or defects in 6% to 48% of inspected items in the cases listed; the cases are those that attracted an audit and the measures differ", and keep the consequence (check the scorer on the subset used).
