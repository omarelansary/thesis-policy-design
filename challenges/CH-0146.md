# Title: The triage mixes "assessed and unsuitable" with "not assessed", applies the synthetic-data and licence criteria unevenly, and rests two exclusions on claims the stewards could not confirm

**Objection.** The triage ends with "'Out of scope' is for V5's constructs only, not a judgement of quality". Many reasons are not construct reasons.
1. *Not assessed, labelled out of scope:* ComplexFuncBench ("details not found"), MCP-AgentBench and DynamicMCPBench ("snippets only"), ScaleMCP ("release not stated"), COLT/ToolLens ("release not found"), MCPToolBench++ ("candidates per query not stated"; SUM-0069 A41: absence by grep patterns only, and the table shows 87 tools, so the outcome may be right while the reason is not). CC2 asks for each to be triaged "in or out with a reason"; "not found" should give a third state, "unassessed", with what would be needed. s1 itself says "not found" is not evidence of absence.
2. *Synthetic data as a reason, applied unevenly.* MCPEval is out because it "relies entirely on synthetic data"; Mem2ActBench, PEToolBench, PTBench, APOLLO, Evo-Memory are out for "offline generation". s4 says synthetic and constructed options are candidates under the same rules, and ATRBench, kept as the closest P1 design, has synthetic personas, GPT-5.4-written learning sessions and a simulator with "no persistent preference state" (SUM-0072 C12; SUM-0073 C14; Table 9 of the paper, "adjudicated failure rates in the 100-rule human audit", 19/100 for behavioural recurrence, `c03` L1210-1216). The criterion should be documented construction and checked ground truth (INT-T-021 s2), applied to all.
3. *EvoMemBench's CrossEp-Tool* (BFCL multi-turn base reconstructed with implicit references so that a later episode needs an earlier one; 800 tasks, manually verified reconstruction; SUM-0074 C27) bears on P1's "later sessions need earlier tools" and gets one line of the triage. I do not claim it passes (its catalogue size is not in the register and BFCL domains are small, so it may fall in R-small [U]; "thin validation" is the scout's judgement). It needs a card or an explicit rejection reason.
4. *MetaTool: "CC BY-NC-SA, selection only".* SUM-0075 D14: UNCONFIRMED; the stored file is the PDF and the licence line is on an abstract page not stored. A non-commercial share-alike licence is also not a bar to a thesis's research use, and MPT (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0) has a card. Tool-selection benchmarks are the nearest family to P3's "which tool does the model reach for".
5. *NESTFUL: "repository archived 2026-10-29".* Archiving is not a construct reason; the single-turn reason alone would do.
6. *API-Bank: "contamination risk [S]".* A speculation label as a triage reason.

**Evidence.** `17` s5 L121; SUM-0069 A38, A41; SUM-0074 C27; SUM-0075 D14; SUM-0071 B26; SUM-0072 C12; SUM-0073 C14; INT-T-021 s2 (L41).
[F] stewards' verdicts. [I] my reading of the inconsistency. [U] whether any of the unassessed benchmarks would pass.

**What would resolve it.** Split the triage into: rejected on a stated hard requirement (with the item); unassessed (with what is missing); kept as a methods precedent. Drop the archive and [S] reasons. Apply one synthetic-data criterion to ATRBench and to the excluded ones. Verify or delete the MetaTool licence reason.
