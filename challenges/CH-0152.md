# Title: s1.4 names the properties that drive V5's bill, and the card columns do not carry them; the human's own list of attributes is also only partly covered

**Objection.** s1.4 says: "The properties that drive V5's bill (tokens per definition, tool-result sizes, turns per task, sessions, volatile content in tool results such as timestamps) are rarely reported and must be read from data or code." The s5 columns are T, D, E, S, Sessions, Cost or cache reported, Licence, Open-weight range, Stored. Against the list:
- *Tokens per definition:* only MCPVerse (44k for 220+ tools, 140-147k full) and the catalogue-sources row (PolicyLayer median 1,864 tokens per server; ToolRet 174.56 per document) give it. No card gives tokens per definition for MCP-Atlas, Toolathlon, MCPMark, AppWorld (the one candidate with an offline definitions file), LiveMCPBench (`tools/all_config.json` exists), BFCL, tau2.
- *Tool-result sizes:* MCPMark (input 64K to 1.4M tokens per task) and Toolathlon (15-35% overlong, truncation at 100K characters) only.
- *Turns per task:* partial (Toolathlon 20-27, MCPMark about 16, AppWorld 42-47 calls).
- *Volatile content:* "live" is noted for E; timestamps in results are not.
INT-T-021 s1 item 3 lists "catalogue scale, task-visible tools, prerequisite dependencies, execution state, multi-session behavior, and costs". *Prerequisite dependencies* between tools (a call needs another call's output, which matters for what must be discoverable together under deferral) are absent from the cards and from s4 hard requirements and scored properties; MCP-Atlas's "2-8 required tools from two or more servers" and NESTFUL's nested sequences are the only dependency information in the register. *Task-visible tools* (what the model is shown versus the whole catalogue) is given for MCP-Atlas and LiveMCP-101 and ToolSandbox's distractor knob, not elsewhere.
Missing fields that a hard requirement or a scored property in s4 relies on: closed-model dependencies of the simulator, judge and router (P1 H4); endpoint type and code change to run on the route (CH-0148); public-private or train-test split and measured gap (the contamination property; CH-0139); tool-call record in history (P1); scorer's reliability on the route's judge rather than the published judge.
CC3 says "every field filled or marked 'not found in the places read'". Many cells say "not established" or "-" without saying where it was looked for.

**Evidence.** `17` s1.4 L29, s5 L94-117; INT-T-021 s1 item 3; SUM-0068 A24, A20; SUM-0067 A12; SUM-0075 D1-D8; inspector I1 item 6.
[F] the columns. [I] that the bill drivers are the construct for P5, P4 and E2E. [U] values for most rows.

**What would resolve it.** Add columns (or a second table) for the bill drivers and for route-fit, split and dependency fields, filled from the stored data where the inspectors already hold them (AppWorld's definition files and LiveMCPBench's `all_config.json` could be sized without running any evaluation, if the human permits the data download, which I have not checked is within scope) and otherwise "not found in the places read, with places".
