# Title: The request-level selector and the system trigger are dropped for "no evidence, and each costs a request", an argument not applied evenly and partly contradicted inside V5

**Objection.** (a) Asymmetry: memory-informed offering has "NONE FOUND" in prior art (`03` row 69) and is kept; V4's selector (P1) and trigger (P3) are dropped for lack of evidence. The difference is REQ-U21-04 for M2; but REQ-O14F-05 (semantic narrowing as a later stage) and REQ-O14F-06 (staged graph, a filtered tool may return) are also requirements, and `04` section 1 point 4 defends the drop only by evidence. (b) "Each costs a request" is true of an LLM selector, not of an embedding-guided router: in the Copilot post that V5 cites as the closest shipped analogue, routing is done by comparing query and tool embeddings before any group is expanded, with 94.5% tool-use coverage against 87.5% (LLM-based) and 69.0% (static list), and online 72% pre-expanded against 19% (EXT-0228:41-55). These are vendor figures and I do not rely on them; the point is that V5 neither argues them away nor tests a no-extra-request stage, and the V4/brief staged form is the supervisor's stated novelty (REQ-O14F-03). (c) V5-R07 cites EXT-0228's "cache miss, an extra round trip" for the drop, while `04` section 1 point 2 says slot-2 offering removes the cache break; the remaining cost is then the round trip of a group expansion, not a selector request. The evidence for dropping is therefore thinner than stated, and the evidence that would favour keeping a routing stage (a local ranker with measured recall at no model request) is not looked for in `05`.

**Evidence.** `04-design.md` section 1 point 4, V5-R07; `06` V5-R07 row; EXT-0228:33, :41-55; `03-reuse-map.md:53, :58, :69`; `requirements/REGISTER.md` REQ-O14F-03, -05, -06.

**What would resolve it.** State the drop as "not in the default, kept as a P-arm" with the actual reason (LLM selector cost), add one arm of a local ranker stage (cost counted per REQ-O02-02) to `05` or say why not, and reconcile V5-R07's cited reason with section 1 point 2.
