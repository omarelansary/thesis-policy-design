# Title: Section 9 gives a resolvable difference that ignores the clustering rule the lead accepted, and s7 omits the pools and resolvable differences s1.2 promised

**Objection.**
1. *Clustering ignored.* s9 item 3: "With AppWorld's test_normal (168 tasks) and an assumed 20% discordance, the paired 95% interval is about +/-7 points." This treats 168 tasks as independent. s3 R11, accepted under CH-0150, says task counts are not treated as independent where tasks share structure. AppWorld's 168 Test-N tasks are 56 scenarios of 3 variants (`v5-bm-b13` L713-715), and the paper reports a 30-50% drop from task to scenario completion (L865-867), so variants of a scenario are correlated. With a design effect of 1 + 2*rho for three tasks per cluster, rho between 0.5 and 1 gives intervals of about +/-9.6 to +/-11.8 points instead of +/-7 [I, arithmetic; rho is not measured and 20% discordance is itself assumed]. The same applies to P3's 165 train+dev tasks (55 scenarios) and to the "dev 60 tasks" the proposer suggests for larger loops (DPR-0010 s5). The figure sits in a proposal to the human who decides between "estimation only" and "a margin", so an optimistic interval biases that choice.
2. *The comparison count is not mentioned.* `05` s3 lists at least five arms, two or more models and three regimes; R12 (one primary comparison named before the run) is accepted but s9 item 3 does not apply it.
3. *Promised columns missing.* s1.2 (L19) says "The register reports, per candidate, the pool available for a held-out set and the difference resolvable at that size". s7 gives pools inconsistently (AppWorld 165/168; MCP-Atlas about 90 "if the sample holds"; VitaBench 56 users; MCPMark about 51; ATRBench 284 rule/test pairs, 20 personas) and the resolvable difference only for AppWorld in s9. The effective unit is not named per candidate (users for P1: R11). For VitaBench 2.0 and ATRBench the independent unit is 56 users and 20 personas, which is the more informative number for P1 and is not stated in s7 for ATRBench.
4. *MCP-Atlas judge noise against the effect size.* v3 reports cross-judge ranges of 2.1-4.6 points (register L107); `05` s1 names 2-5 points as the size the stored sources report for the accuracy loss of interest. For the E2E #2 arm the judge noise is the same size as the effect, and s7 does not say so.

**Evidence.** [F] register L19, L77, L107, L239, L253; `v5-bm-b13` L713-715, L865-867; `05` s1, s3. [I] the design-effect arithmetic (n = 168 gives SE about 3.5 points at 20% discordance; times sqrt(1.5 to 3) gives the range above).

**What would resolve it.** Recompute s9 item 3 with a stated cluster size and a range of rho, say that rho is unmeasured, and name the unit of independence per candidate in s7 with the pool in units of that cluster. Apply R12 to the E2E plan or mark it a duty of the protocol.
