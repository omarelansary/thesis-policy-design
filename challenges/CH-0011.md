# Title: the recovery signals S16-S19/D5 are not leak-free as specified: S17's "without success evidence" is undefined, S5-derived signals inherit vehicle contamination, and N4's termination re-open confounds recovery value with a second attempt

**Objection.** (Answers the proposer's question 1 and part of 3. CH-0003 is resolved as to its core; this is the residue.)
1. **S17 has an undefined predicate.** "The model ends or answers without success evidence" (DPR-0001 R3). At runtime the system does not have success evidence; success is X (S14). Two implementations: (a) fire at every termination, in which case the false-positive rate on the no-miss stratum is 100% by construction, so the unused price of N4 is one check per task plus whatever it appends, never "rarely fires" (HYP-0003's premise); (b) fire when a verifier says the answer is unsupported, in which case the verifier is an S11-type judge whose access to anything resembling the scorer (S14, a benchmark judge, a reference) would make it P. The DPR must say which, and the permitted inputs to the verifier.
2. **S16-S19 inherit S7's contamination through S5.** R5 marks S2 and S7 "vehicle-contaminated" on the closest vehicle (cache-miss and unknown-tool messages "steer ... toward the reference route in every arm", S4G L26). S16, S17, S18 and S19 are computed from `h_s` (S5), which contains those messages. The rule in R5 is stated for S2 and S7 only; it should be stated for every derived signal that reads S5 or S7.
3. **Classifier provenance.** S16/S17 are "S5 + classifier (S11-type)". If prompted zero-shot they are R inputs with error; if trained or threshold-tuned on labelled trajectories, labels such as "tool was missing" come from U1 (gold) and the classifier is class D with the leakage routes of R5. The DPR does not say which, and its claim that the signals "can be implemented from R information alone" (DPR-0003 Falsifier and assumptions) therefore depends on a choice not made.
4. **N4 at D5 re-opens a finished attempt.** A system that, after the model terminates, appends tools and gives another turn gives a second attempt. Any success gain then mixes the value of the added tools with the generic value of a retry. PlanBench-XL, as recorded in the governing assessment, found that enforced extra exploration after a wrong termination added "less than 5 percentage points" (S4F-PA L32), so the generic part may be small, but it is of the same order as the effects the validation wants to detect (ComplexMCP's iterative gain for Claude was +0.70, inside one task's resolution: S4G L63). DPR-0003 section 3's ablations (N3 only, N4 only, N3+N4, none) have no arm that gives the extra turn without adding tools.
5. **S19 as a class.** "System re-ranking of `K` with the evolving context" is R as an input but its ranker is a trained or prompted model (S10 or S11); its class is the class of its ranker. The row says "R input; ranking quality unmeasured outside FitText", which understates that the initiator question for FitText is itself unverified (CH-0012).

**Evidence.** DPR-0001 R3 (S16-S19 table), R5; DPR-0003 N4 row, section 3, "Falsifier and assumptions"; HYP-0003; S4G L26, L63 (read); S4F-PA L32 (read).

**What would resolve it.** DPR states S17's firing rule and the verifier's inputs; extends the vehicle-contamination rule to all signals derived from S5/S7; states classifier provenance; adds a no-tools extra-turn control to the validation arms. Answerable now (specification); rates need an experiment.

**Response (proposer).** [verbatim, round-1 reply, 2026-10-16; rounds used: 1 of 2] "CH-0011 CONCEDE. S17 defined as the model's own end-of-attempt event; if it fires at every termination, no-miss FP is 100% by construction. Classifier provenance rule (zero-shot = R; tuned = D with U1 leakage). Contamination rule extended to S16-S19. Extra-turn-without-tools control added."
