# Title: "observation masking halved cost" is carried into a cached-prefix bill without the paper's own pricing caveat

**Objection.** 01 C8 (L46) and 03 L75 give masking "REUSE as the simple default for long single runs", with V5-R08 and stage H built on it. Three limits in the source are only partly carried over.
1. The paper's headline is that both masking and LLM-summary "more than halve the cost compared to the Raw Agent" (L96-L97). Halving is a property of reducing context against a raw agent, not specific to masking. The specific claim is only that masking matches LLM-summary on solve rate (L24-L25, L97-L98).
2. For the Qwen runs the cost was computed post hoc at a price that "does not distinguish between cache hit and miss input tokens. This leads to an inflated cost" (L823-L827). The map says "priced post hoc without a cache-hit distinction" but omits "inflated". A cache-blind price makes any token reduction look larger than under a cached-prefix bill, which is the bill REQ-U21-07 asks for.
3. Masking with window M = 10 rewrites older turns on every turn once the window is full, so each turn changes the prefix beyond the window. The paper itself says a tuned hybrid lost efficiency through "compounding KV cache inefficiencies" (L602-L603). V5's own rule (stage H: clear only when the cleared tokens pay the rewrite) is the vendor context-editing rule (L/v5-anthropic-context-editing L71), which differs from the masking rule the paper measured. So the REUSE verdict combines the paper's rule and the vendor's rule without a result for the combination.
The map's verdict may still be right for Gemini-priced rows; the text does not show it.

**Evidence.** L/v5-complexitytrap-2508.21433.txt L24-L25, L96-L101, L529-L535, L602-L603, L820-L829; L/v5-anthropic-context-editing-20261022.md L71; 03 L75, L76.

**What would resolve it.** Read the Gemini (API-reported cost) rows of the paper's tables or its released dataset and state the reduction for those alone. Re-word the verdict as "direction established for long coding-agent runs; size under a cached-prefix bill unknown".
