# Title: P4g is the vendor's model-driven hierarchy, not the system-run cascade that the section-D model and the supervisor's staged idea describe; HYP-0002 as rewritten tests the wrong cascade

**Objection.** (Answers the proposer's open question (b).)
1. P4g (DPR-0002 section C): "No system relevance decision; the group descriptions are the selector ... the model loads a group ... then upgrades individual tools". Every stage is a model act paid in turns and in prefix tokens (the group headers every turn). The section-D model (`b_j`, `a_j n_{j-1}`, `r_j`) describes stages that are run by a decider reading candidates. P4g has no such decider, so the model does not apply to it, and HYP-0002's "specified multi-stage cascade (group-then-tool disclosure P4g, optionally with a selection step on top)" reduces to "model-driven hierarchy vs model-driven flat disclosure (P3/P4)" unless the optional step is taken.
2. The system-run cascade exists in the vehicle and is named in the lead's extract: "hier is a two-stage router that picks servers and then tools" (EXT-0053, Appendix N), compared with `rag:k` at ±8-point intervals. It is also the structure of REQ-O14-12 (steps that filter), of ER-R's retrieve-then-rank, and of MCP-Zero's hierarchical routing. A complete comparator with system-run stages (what each stage reads, which scorer, how many survive, what the second stage is allowed to see) is still not written. The proposer's own evidence (EXT-0053) is about that structure, not about P4g.
3. P4g's headers also scale with the number of groups per turn (at `n` large and groups of fewer than ten tools, hundreds of header lines every turn). The DPR says only `g_max` is unset; the feasibility range of P4g, as for P4 (CH-0009 item 3), is not stated.
4. Consequence for HYP-0002: a falsifier satisfied by P4g beating P2 would be read as "cascade pays", though P4g's advantage could come entirely from model-side initiation and namespace authoring quality (S2 dependence the DPR notes), neither of which is a property of staging.

**Evidence.** DPR-0002 section C (P4g), section D; HYP-0002 falsifier; EXT-0053 (read in full by me); S4F-PA L15 (ER-R), S4G L72 and vehicle report L55 (`hier` = one-server hierarchical exposure; I read both).

**What would resolve it.** Write one system-run two-level comparator (names the scorer for each level, the survivors per level, the input to level two), keep P4g as the model-driven one, and let HYP-0002 name which it tests; or state that HYP-0002 covers model-driven hierarchy only and the system-run cascade remains untested.

**Response (proposer).** [verbatim, round-2 reply, 2026-10-16; rounds used: 2 of 2] "CH-0018 CONCEDED. Added P2h (system-run two-level: scorer s_1 over groups keeps g_1; scorer s_2 over tools inside surviving groups keeps m; level 2 sees nothing from cut groups; cost (b_1 + a_1 #groups) + (b_2 + a_2 #tools in kept groups)). Section D's model describes P2h, not P4g. HYP-0002 covers both, each against the best single-stage comparator of the same driver type. P4g feasible range stated (header cost grows with number of groups)."

**Outcome.** resolved for DPR-0002 (P2h specified, section D and falsifier updated, checked) and DPR-0003 (P2h arm and falsifier). HYP-0002's own falsifier text had not been updated at the time of checking (residue 1 in CH-0017).
