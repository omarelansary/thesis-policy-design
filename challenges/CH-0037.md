# Title: the knapsack counterexample is right; "no approximation ratio" and the heuristic framing are unnecessary, and the incremental cost still omits several terms

**Grade: MINOR. I tried to break the counterexample and could not (see RV-0004).**

**Objection.** (design/package-v3/01-errata-AUTH-005.md section 3.)
1. **Exact solution is available.** With additive, fixed `m_t` and `s_t = keep_t - disc_t`, the problem "choose the core `C` with `sum_{C} m_t >= M_total - epsilon` at minimum `sum_C s_t`" (tools with `s_t < 0` forced into the core at negative cost) is the minimum-knapsack (covering knapsack) problem. It has a pseudo-polynomial dynamic programme and standard approximation results (greedy by ratio with the best-single-item fix is a constant-factor approximation; recalled from the literature, **not in the record and not checked here; no search is authorised**). "Even that greedy is a heuristic ... Established properties: none" is accurate for the rule as written in v2, but implies the obstacle is the solver. It is not: the obstacle is the inputs (noisy `p_t q_t`, unknown `q_t(D)`, the missing terms in CH-0035). Solving the knapsack exactly on noisy estimates would also overfit (optimiser's curse); that, not the NP-hardness, is why the end-to-end sweep is needed. The errata should say that.
2. **The "3,000 random six-tool instances (1,713 vs 1,023)" cannot be checked by me** (no Bash) and the generator is described only as "uniform integer parameters". It shows non-optimality (already shown by the 4-tool example), not frequency under any realistic distribution. Either drop the counts or give the generator and seed.
3. **Coupling (a) direction.** For fixed-score rankers the errata says `q` estimated on a larger deferred set is conservative. That holds for *rank-based top-kappa retrieval of a fixed query*; it does not hold when the model's query depends on the visible set and scope statement (acknowledged), and it assumes the number of competitors is the only change in `D`. Fine as written; I note only that the "conservative" label is a property of the ranker, not of the policy.
4. **Co-needed tools and `kappa`.** The errata flags non-additivity of `disc_t` across co-needed tools but gives no corrected form; with `kappa` results per search, co-needed tools *reduce* the marginal cost of the second tool (shared exchange), so the additive `disc_t` overstates deferral cost for clustered tools and the greedy would wrongly prefer to keep them. The direction of the bias is determinable and is not stated.

**Evidence.** Errata section 3 read in full. Counterexample recomputed by hand (RV-0004 section 2).

**What would resolve it.** Edit the sentences; state the direction of the co-needed bias.
