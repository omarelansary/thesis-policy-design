# Title: as written, the validation falsifiers make a component easy to justify and hard to reject, two hypotheses are near-unfalsifiable or mis-stated, and nothing fixes the decision rule before the data

**Objection.** (Answers the proposer's question 4. AUTH-004: "Identify falsifiers rather than writing a plan that can only confirm the proposal." I think the plan, as written, can mostly confirm.)
1. **Asymmetric thresholds.** DPR-0003 section 3: a component is "not justified if" it is "dominated ... in every regime tested" (N2); "add overhead in the no-miss stratum exceeding their measured value in the miss stratum" (N3/N4); "dominated by P2" (cheap grade). HYP-0003's falsifier also needs the price to exceed the margin "in every regime tested". So a component survives if it is not dominated in any one regime, and is dropped only if it loses everywhere. With several regimes, several models, several conventions and a vector of outcomes, some regime where a component is not dominated exists by chance alone. No multiplicity control, no pre-specified regime weighting, no requirement that the surviving regime be one of interest.
2. **The decision rule is not fixed in advance.** The falsifiers say "dominated on the chosen decision rule", and the rule (F1-F7) is "not decided here". Without a requirement that the rule, the reading of "pays" and the regime set be pre-registered before any run, the falsifier can be re-read after the data. The governing record already identified this failure: S4D quality addendum 2.1 requires that C6 "pre-register which reading of 'pays' it uses" (five admissible readings (a)-(e)), and S4G records that a "pays" reading "chosen after seeing results" invalidates the claim (S4G L82). DPR-0003 does not carry the five readings or a pre-registration requirement; DPR-0001 F5 carries one reading.
3. **HYP-0002 is mis-stated.** The hypothesis is "a policy with one selection step is not dominated by a cascade". The falsifier is "a cascade ... is not dominated by the best of the single-stage comparators ... in at least one regime". Non-domination of the cascade does not mean the cascade dominates the single step; and the "best of the single-stage comparators" (P2, P3, P4, P7) is a different object from "a policy with one selection step". In a space of success, several token categories, cost and turns, mutual non-domination is the common outcome, so the falsifier fires on nearly any cascade; the "justified" criterion is then only Pareto-incomparability, which the addendum's reading (e) ("removing it yields a policy dominated") is stricter than.
4. **HYP-0005 can only confirm.** "Fewer exposed tools gives equal or higher cumulative or billed cost" in "some regimes" counts ties, and the falsifier requires "the same ordering ... in all regimes" under each convention. With measurement noise, identical rank order across all regimes and conventions has near-zero probability, so the hypothesis is confirmed by noise. The informative content (how often, how large, under which cache regime) is not in the claim. Also part of it is true by construction (an added discovery turn adds tokens).
5. **HYP-0004's falsifier is not decidable.** "fail to invoke the interface in a substantial share" has no reference (a share relative to what? to the miss rate? to a pre-registered fraction?), and "the models tested" has no quantifier (all? any?).
6. **HYP-0001 is untestable on the vehicle the validation names.** It needs early cache-read share or request repetition across a grid of regimes. The closest vehicle has "no cache fields", single-task runs and session length "not available" (CAVEATS row 3; S4G L74). The DPR admits "the closest vehicle records no cache fields" (HYP-0001 Assumptions) but does not draw the consequence: it is falsifiable only on a different harness.
7. **The whole-proposal falsifier** ("some comparator among P0, P1, P3, P4 dominates the provisional structure in every regime tested") omits P2 and P7, the comparators closest to the structure, and requires strict dominance in every regime. If P2 equals the structure minus recovery and ties it, the whole-proposal falsifier does not fire.

**Evidence.** DPR-0003 section 3 and "Falsifier and assumptions"; HYP-0001..0005 front matter `falsifier:` fields; S4D-QUALITY-ADDENDUM L10-L17 and L19; S4G L82, L74; CAVEATS rows 3 and 5. All read by me.

**What would resolve it.** Falsifiers restated in a form that can fail in the proposer's own direction: a pre-registration requirement (rule, "pays" reading, regime set, comparator set, decision on ties) before any run; a "not justified" criterion that does not require losing in every regime; HYP-0002, HYP-0004, HYP-0005 rewritten with an explicit quantity, quantifier and reference. No numbers needed; symbols suffice.

**Response (proposer).** [verbatim, round-1 reply, 2026-10-16; rounds used: 1 of 2] "CH-0013 CONCEDE (blocking). Pre-registration of 'pays' reading, decision rule, strata, convention and the claimed regimes; a component is justified only in a stated regime after multiplicity correction. HYP-0002 falsifier rewritten as the exact negation; HYP-0005 ties no longer confirm; HYP-0004 'substantial share' removed (threshold human-set); HYP-0001 flagged untestable on the closest vehicle; whole-proposal falsifier now lists P2, P4g, P7."

**Outcome.** substance resolved (DPR-0003 section 3 first paragraphs, whole-proposal falsifier, HYP-0002/0004/0005 checked). Not resolved: the old falsifier list is still in DPR-0003 section 3 and DPR-0002; HYP-0003 and HYP-0006 falsifiers still read "in every regime tested"; HYP-0005's falsifier is not the negation of its hypothesis (CH-0017 item 1). I lowered the severity to should-fix on that basis. Round 2: the proposer rewrote DPR-0003 section 3 and HYP-0003/0005/0006 (verified: negations in claimed regimes, pre-registration, ties handled); residues are in CH-0017. Note: a claim "survives only where it is non-dominated" is a strong criterion under a scalar pre-registered rule and a weak one under F4 (Pareto reporting); the pre-registration should fix a scalar rule or define dominance and ties.
