## Items that may contradict or sharpen current ledger wording [O]

1. The CAPC paper's mechanism is prompt compression plus explicit caching. "Lazy tool catalog (USE_LAZY_TOOLS=True)" belongs to the author's internal assistant and is cited only to explain a gap between a simulator (-51.7%) and a production-path run (-45.5%). Wording that treats lazy tool loading as a mechanism evaluated by the paper is not supported by lines 1-1260 (EXT-0191).
2. 496 is described as "tasks" in the abstract, §4.2 and conclusion, and as "executions" in the contributions (EXT-0159 region). Both are in the paper.
3. The audit's sample selection is not stated; §3.3 says "each flagged instance", §4.1 says "each task" (EXT-0184, EXT-0186).
4. The false-negative/false-positive split is given only for tau2-Bench Retail (8 / 3 of 11).
5. The 18.9 pp LiveMCPBench spread is, by the authors' statement, end-to-end (agent plus rubric generation plus judge), not judge noise alone (EXT-0190).
