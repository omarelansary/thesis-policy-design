## 0. How the versions were found (method, one paragraph)
- arXiv PDFs in `evidence/literature-20261019/` carry a stamp such as `arXiv:2607.09493v2 [cs.AI] 15 Sep 2026` on the left margin of page 1 (cited `file:line` below). The stamp is the version of the PDF **at retrieval** and its date is that version's date; the retrieval time is in `evidence/literature-20261019/MANIFEST.jsonl`. Most MANIFEST URLs have **no version suffix** (they fetched "latest"), so the version is recoverable only from the stamp. Origin-set texts: version from the stamp in the first lines (read with `origin_extract.py --no-write`, lines 1-14) and URL/retrieval time from `evidence/sources.json`.
- Web pages and vendor documents have no version; the only handle is the retrieval time (and `last_modified` where `sources.json` has one).
- `05`, `04`, `02` almost never state a version. Under the ledger criterion "every quoted number carries version and date", the verdict column therefore reads **VERSION MISSING IN THE DOC (recoverable: ...)** for nearly every row; **VERSIONED** is used only where the document itself names the version/date (for example `04` says "as of their 2026-10-11 retrieval"). UNKNOWN is used where I could not recover it.
- Rows group the numbers of one source on one line of the document (a deviation from one-row-per-number; the verbatim numbers are listed inside the cell). "Claim type": paper = the authors' own result; vendor = vendor/blog claim; lead = the lead's own arithmetic (excluded from the map, listed only where it sits beside a quoted number).
- Numbers were mapped to sources; I did **not** re-verify numbers against the texts in this batch (that is C-R03, reviewers R1/R2).
