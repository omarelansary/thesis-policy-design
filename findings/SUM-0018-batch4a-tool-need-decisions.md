## Row verdicts

### C-S08/VOCAB: PARTLY
- [F] None of the three texts, at the lines read, reports a rate at which an agent starts a search/discovery request for a tool it cannot see, without being told to. Tulip: search is prescribed (TUL:1193; pipeline TUL:363-400). WHEN2TOOL: the tool is visible and no search exists (W2T:712-713). Closed-world: no search action; the setting is calls to unregistered or colliding names (CW:201-213, 853-858).
- [F] Nearest quantities: W2T tool-call necessity decisions on visible tools (overcalling counts W2T:393-396); CW hallucinated-call counts and rates (322 and 154; per-model 0.13-0.57 raw-JSON, CW:459-461, 0.27-0.60 MCP, CW:863); TUL cost and precision/recall of tools used under a search tool (TUL:515-518).
- [I] The three texts supply decision-style and hallucination-style numbers in different settings, but none is a discovery-initiation rate. The row's "not done" can be restated as "done for these three texts; no direct measurement found".
- [U] Whether other texts in the 61-entry manifest report such a rate: not read by me.

### C-S08/UNINSTR: PARTLY (nearest evidence only)
- [F] W2T gives unnecessary-call counts only as totals: Qwen3-1.7B 864 calls over 750 easy tasks, Llama-3.3-70B 1,482 (W2T:395-396); the paper's own definition of easy tasks: "The model can mostly solve without tools" (W2T:240). Models: six open-weight. No closed models.
- [F] CW reports hallucination rates, defined as H1-H5 emissions divided by all 60 probes (unconditional) or by probes on which a call was emitted (conditional) (CW:505-510); a hallucination is a call to a non-registered tool or with undeclared arguments (CW:201-209). These are not "unnecessary" calls.
- [F] TUL: no rate.
- [O] EXT-0121 (BFCL irrelevance definitions) is the in-repo anchor for a related definition; I did not compare it against these texts.
