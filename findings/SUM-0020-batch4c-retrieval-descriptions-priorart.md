## Contradictions and caveats to carry
- [F] AS-01 as worded ("always beats") versus the stored texts: ToolRet rows in EXT-0181 show BM25s at or near the top of the rows shown; ToolDreamer Table 6 shows BM25 above DPR and QZhou-Embedding and equal to MiniLM; StackOne shows embeddings 38% vs BM25 14% Top-1 (but 85% vs 87% Top-5). ToolDreamer's authors write that "dense models are fundamentally necessary" (TD:1683-1685). All of this is retrieval-benchmark evidence; none of the four texts is an agent-with-hidden-tools experiment. I do not conclude for or against the supervisor's claims.
- [F] AS-03 ("keyword ... will never work"): the stored texts show BM25 giving 14% Top-1 and 87% Top-5 in SO, the TD average N@10 29.55 vs 38.43 for the best dense, and ToolDreamer lifting BM25 by +14% N@10 to 33.80 with generated descriptions. [U] Whether the symbolic/keyword search the supervisor means (e.g. regex/exact-name search) is the same as BM25 is not settled by these texts; LangChain's `search_tools` uses substring match (LC:45-49) and has no measurement.
- [O] The StackOne blog is internally inconsistent on the test-split size (540 at SO:297; 543 at SO:310).
- [O] StackOne states Anthropic's tool search uses BM25 (SO:274, 280). The stored Anthropic pages are EXT-0131/EXT-0074; I did not re-read them in this task, so this is not verified here.
- [I] On AS-05, ToolDreamer itself documents prior work generating tool descriptions for retrieval (Kachuee et al. 2025) and generating queries from descriptions (Chen et al. 2024b); the cited papers themselves are not stored, so what they do is known only through ToolDreamer's description of them.
