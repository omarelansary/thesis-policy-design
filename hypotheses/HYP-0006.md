# A system-side trigger from the evolving context recovers misses at lower unused price than model initiation

**Hypothesis.** [hypothesis, not a finding] A system-side re-add trigger (the system, not the model's explicit request, decides) based only on runtime-observable signals (model text of inability, termination, repeated failure, re-ranking with the evolving context) recovers more of the missed cases, and at a lower unused price in the no-miss stratum, than relying on the model to call a search interface.

**Why it is plausible (weakened after CH-0012 and the lead's EXT-0050/0051).** In FitText the agent emits a pseudo-tool description in its reasoning ("the agent may emit a pseudo-tool description dt", EXT-0050) and the system then triggers retrieval (EXT-0051). That is an agent-emitted request with system-executed retrieval; EXT-0052 (per the challenger) shows a system-side condition as well, and whether they are one mechanism is unknown (FitText section 4.2 not extracted). Direct evidence for a system trigger built on S16-S19 was not found in the extracts read; FitText is an adjacent mechanism. Its multi-turn rate for one model is 70.7 vs 62.1 for single-pass (the +8.6 is the largest of three models: +1.7, +2.5, +8.6) and 57.6 for a confounded raw-query static baseline (EXT-0035); EXT-0036 is top-5 availability, not success. Limits per the map (raw-query static baseline, LLM judge, simulated server, token cost of retrieval not in the passage). Model non-initiation is unmeasured for discovery (HYP-0004). The trigger's false-positive cost is not measured anywhere in the stored corpus.

**What would falsify it.** The falsifier above.

**Assumptions.** S16-S19 can be computed without gold information (DPR-0001 R3); classifier error is counted.

**Alternatives it competes with.** HYP-0004 (model initiation); no recovery.

**Not claimed.** That the FitText result transfers to the vehicle or to any other setting.
