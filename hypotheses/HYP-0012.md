# The preferable initiation mechanism depends on prevalence: a system trigger is preferable to model initiation only above or below a break-even w

**Hypothesis.** [hypothesis, not a finding] If a system trigger over runtime-observable signals (S16-S19, DPR-0001) recovers more of the misses than model initiation but at a higher unused price, it is preferable only where the prevalence of misses is high enough; if it has both higher recovery and lower price it dominates; any other pair is a trade-off, reported as such.

**Correction of HYP-0006 (AUTH-005 item 5b).** HYP-0006's falsifier was a conjunction ("recovery higher **and** unused price lower, each beyond a margin"). A mechanism with higher recovery and higher unused price is not dominated, so failing the conjunction does not show it is not preferable; the conjunction tested dominance, not preference. The corrected comparison is on the net rule above, with the break-even w, and with dominance a special case. Evidence base: the stored FitText passages show prompt-prescribed model emission with system-executed retrieval (EXT-0065, EXT-0050/0051), not a system-side relevance trigger (EXT-0055 reading in the map; steward V5 SUPPORTED for EXT-0065); no stored source measures a system trigger's false-positive cost.

**What would falsify it.** The front-matter falsifier. A required control: an extra-turn-without-tools arm, since a re-opened attempt mixes tool value with a second attempt (enforced extra exploration in a planning benchmark gave under five percentage points for most models, EXT-0063, V4; not a tools setting).

**Units.** R_m is in success units and U_m, P_m in cost units; the scalar net needs an exchange rate (a human decision, formulation F3). Under a constrained rule (F1) the same comparison is made on the population means: arm m is admissible at prevalence w iff its population success is within the margin of the reference, and among admissible arms the lower population cost is preferred; the admissible region in w is then reported instead of a single w_SM (DPR-0005 section 6).

**Assumptions.** Triggers computed from runtime text only (class R if zero-shot; class D if tuned on labels, with its training data stated).

**Alternatives it competes with.** HYP-0010 (model initiation); no recovery.

**Not claimed.** That any trigger works.
