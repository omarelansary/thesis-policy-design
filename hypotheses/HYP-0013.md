# The rule-derived core plus prompted search (candidate C-A) is not dominated by the simple comparators in some regime

**Hypothesis.** [hypothesis, not a finding] The candidate C-A of DPR-0004 (core F chosen by the cost-break-even rule with a recoverability constraint, the rest deferred behind a prompted search interface, add-only in session) is non-dominated by expose-all, the static core, the vendor-rule deferral, and the listed-grade variant in at least one regime that can be named before the run.

**Why it is plausible.** Vendors recommend a small non-deferred set plus search (EXT-0009: "Keep your 3-5 most frequently used tools non-deferred") but give no rule; OpenAI states deferral suits "A large catalog where each task needs only a few functions" and that discovery "adds a step" (EXT-0012) and advises comparing "task completion, input-token usage, and latency" before choosing a default. Reachability is a hard gate in one benchmark (EXT-0053), so the cost of a miss is high, which is why the rule has a recoverability constraint. The break-even form follows the accounting form of EXT-0041 (skills, not tools; transfer assumed [U]).

**Evidence against / unresolved (kept visible).** OpenAI: mixing eager and deferred functions "is supported but generally not recommended" (EXT-0012), against the core-plus-deferred shape. Toollery found no billed-input-cost advantage for restriction when the candidate list varied per query (EXT-0061); the candidate avoids per-request variation, but that is HYP-0007, unmeasured. Flat exposure cost 8 points on one model/one draw (EXT-0053 V6), but rag:32 tied flat; the evidence base does not show that the full library loses in general.

**What would falsify it.** The front-matter falsifier.

**Assumptions.** Usage is concentrated enough that a core exists (HYP-0014); initiation holds (HYP-0010); stable surface keeps hit rate (HYP-0007).

**Alternatives it competes with.** Each comparator in the falsifier; candidates C-0, C-B, C-C of DPR-0004.

**Not claimed.** That the candidate is novel, optimal, or better than any comparator anywhere.
