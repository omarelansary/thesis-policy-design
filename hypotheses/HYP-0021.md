# Observation masking lowers the bill of long single runs on our provider

**Hypothesis.** The established result that masking old observations halves cost at equal solve rate (coding agents; L/v5-complexitytrap-2508.21433.txt:23-25) carries over to long single runs of our agent, provided masking is applied only when the cleared tokens pay the cache rewrite (L/v5-anthropic-context-editing-20261022.md:71).

**Why it is plausible.** The study's own cache findings (summaries get little cache reuse; a tuned hybrid lost efficiency through KV-cache effects) and the vendor's `clear_at_least` advice describe the same trade (FND-0040) [F].

**What would falsify it.** The falsifier above, from provider usage records.

**Alternatives it competes with.** No clearing; LLM summarisation; provider compaction.

**Not claimed.** Transfer from coding agents is not assumed; it is what this tests.

## Result from P4 (2026-11-10, AUTH-033; status unchanged: proposed)
- **Test** (`design/package-v5/inputs/p4/RESULTS-P4.md`): Anthropic's `clear_tool_uses` applied by the client with the vendor's
  documented example (30,000 / keep 3 / at least 5,000), GLM-5.3 on OpenRouter GMICloud, AppWorld dev, 20 tasks in 7 scenarios,
  2 repeats, against keep-everything (P2b's runs on the same tasks).
- **Falsifier met on this setting:** billed +$0.064 per task (95% scenario-bootstrap interval +$0.024 to +$0.107), x2.06;
  calls +2.8 per task (+0.7 to +5.2). On the tasks where clearing fired: billed x2.66, calls x1.72; where it never fired, no
  difference (x1.00). Accuracy −0.025 per task (−0.175 to +0.125).
- **Mechanism [I]:** after a clear the model called tools again; the cache rewrites and extra calls cost more than the cleared
  tokens saved. A replay with behaviour held fixed (`P4-REPLAY-A.md`) already gave +1.0% to +7.5%.
- **With the vendor's defaults** (trigger 100,000) clearing never fires on our tasks.
- **Not shown:** long tasks (where clearing is meant to pay), other models, other rule settings, a provider with a cache-write
  premium (which would make clearing costlier).
