# Observation masking lowers the bill of long single runs on our provider

**Hypothesis.** The established result that masking old observations halves cost at equal solve rate (coding agents; L/v5-complexitytrap-2508.21433.txt:23-25) carries over to long single runs of our agent, provided masking is applied only when the cleared tokens pay the cache rewrite (L/v5-anthropic-context-editing-20261022.md:71).

**Why it is plausible.** The study's own cache findings (summaries get little cache reuse; a tuned hybrid lost efficiency through KV-cache effects) and the vendor's `clear_at_least` advice describe the same trade (FND-0040) [F].

**What would falsify it.** The falsifier above, from provider usage records.

**Alternatives it competes with.** No clearing; LLM summarisation; provider compaction.

**Not claimed.** Transfer from coding agents is not assumed; it is what this tests.
