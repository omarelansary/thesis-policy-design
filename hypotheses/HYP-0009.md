# Add-only exposure after the first turn is adequate in the claimed regimes (replaces the R-cache constraint of DPR-0003)

**Hypothesis.** [hypothesis, not a finding] Within a session, changing the surface only by appending definitions after the cached prefix is not beaten on billed cost (at non-inferior success) by rewriting, withdrawing by reference, or a stable meta-tool, in the regimes where the candidate is claimed (short to moderate horizons, discoveries per session small, a provider that supports append).

**Status change.** In DPR-0003 add-only was a "design constraint, not a fact" (R-cache) adopted for comparability. Here it is a hypothesis with a break-even analysis and with the alternatives as arms (DPR-0005 section 4). It is not forced where by-reference withdrawal exists (EXT-0058: "tool_removal ... the tools array itself never changes, so the cached prefix stays intact").

**Why it is plausible.** Appending after the breakpoint leaves the prefix intact (EXT-0009 vendor claim, read; EXT-0059; EXT-0014 guidance); the appended tokens are written once and read at r thereafter; a mid-session rewrite re-writes the whole conversation prefix because the tools array sits earliest in the hashed prefix (EXT-0058).

**What would overturn it (beyond the falsifier).** (i) Withdrawn or deferred definitions turn out not to be billed or model-visible while withdrawn **and** accumulated unused appended definitions cost more than withdrawal saves (billing of withheld definitions is **not stated** in EXT-0058..0060/0066: steward V2 CANNOT DETERMINE); (ii) the appended tokens lower task success (distraction) when they persist; (iii) the provider lacks append support.

**Assumptions.** Provider-reported cache fields; stated cache lifetime (EXT-0057); r and w estimated, not assumed.

**Alternatives it competes with.** Rewrite at conversation boundaries; free rewrite when the cache has expired; withdraw-by-reference; stable meta-tool (EXT-0014).

**Not claimed.** That add-only is optimal, or that it is necessary on any provider.
