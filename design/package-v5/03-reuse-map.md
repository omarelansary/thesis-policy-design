# 03. Reuse map: established components per policy slot (V5, AUTH-020)

Status: provisional, lead, 2026-10-22; checked by three stewards in thread T-20261022-115 (`findings/SUM-0042`, `SUM-0043`, `SUM-0044`; FND-0040..0042, unverified until a human checks); their corrections are applied in this revision (change log in section 11). Nothing here is validated, approved or final. The principle applied is the human's (REQ-U21-03): what research, large companies or widely used open-source projects have already built and tested is reused, not re-invented or re-tested; only the remainder goes to the proof-of-concept loop (`05`).

**How to read a row.** "L/" = `evidence/literature-20261019/`, stored with URL, date and hashes in `MANIFEST.jsonl` (fetched 2026-10-22 under AUTH-020 unless older). Labels: [F] the stored source says it (a vendor statement of its own mechanism or price counts as established; a vendor performance number does not); [O] observed by the lead in the stored text; [I] interpretation; [U] unknown. *Scout-reported* = from a scout's discovery report with no stored text behind it; a lead, not evidence. Star counts are from the GitHub API on the retrieval date and say nothing about quality. "Not found" means not found in the places listed in `inputs/search-log-20261022.md`.

**Origin-derived rows.** Some rows rest on records the earlier clean-room project selected or wrote: EXT-0228 (Copilot, a public vendor page), FND-0037 and FND-0039 (built on origin extracts EXT-0085..0152), the "uninstructed search initiation" negative (an origin ledger statement, FND-0037 row C-S08/UNINSTR, not re-searched by V5), and the Spring AI lead. None is the student's earlier retrieval work (REQ-U21-02); they are marked here so that independence from the earlier selection can be judged (CH-0104).

**Verdicts.** REUSE = established mechanism, adopt as is; REUSE+CHANGE = adopt with a stated change; OURS = the effect in our combination is untested anywhere we looked, so it goes to `05`; NONE FOUND = nothing established in the places searched.

[I] This slot changes the design question. With per-turn offering at a fixed prefix available from two providers, a staged policy no longer has to choose between "small prefix" and "warm cache" on those providers: definitions can sit cached in `tools` while a stage decides what is *offered*. What that saves is not the tool-block read (still paid at the read price on every request) but wrong-tool risk and, where tools are deferred, the definitions themselves. Whether offering fewer tools raises accuracy at a fixed prefix is OURS.
