## 1. What a loop item of about 20 samples can and cannot show (CH-0088)
At about 20 tasks per arm, a single success rate has a standard error of roughly 10 points and an unpaired two-arm difference roughly 15-16 points (less when both arms run on the same tasks and only discordant pairs count, so every comparative item below is paired on the same tasks); a loop item can therefore show a **large** loss or gain, or a **direction**; it cannot separate "nearly the same" from a loss of a few points (the size the stored sources report, for example Copilot's 2-5 points). Bill components, by contrast, are read per request from usage records and are nearly deterministic given the requests made, so bill **mechanics** (cache hits, writes, which tokens are billed) can be established in the loop; bill **differences that depend on behaviour** (how many searches, how many seeds used) are estimated with their spread.

**Decision rule for the loop (stated in advance, no threshold value).** A stage is (a) dropped when its own mechanism fails (for example a cache break where none should occur) or when it shows a large loss beside its baseline; (b) kept as a candidate when its effect is in the expected direction or unclear; (c) never declared "established" by a loop item. Accuracy effects that a loop item cannot resolve go to the end-to-end study. The asymmetry is deliberate: a small loop is allowed to remove parts, not to prove them.

- **Q-U30 (open, explained).** Lowering each session's bill per turn usually lowers the total bill per turn, but not always, because the two averages weight sessions differently. Example: a 10-turn session costs 100 (10 per turn) and a 1-turn session costs 5 (5 per turn); total 105 over 11 turns = 9.5 per turn, mean of the two sessions' rates = 7.5. A policy that turns them into 120 and 1 makes the total 121 over 11 = 11.0 per turn (worse) while the mean of session rates drops to (12 + 1) / 2 = 6.5 (better). The total over turns is what the bill-payer pays per turn; the mean of session rates gives every session, short or long, equal weight. **Lead's recommendation:** total bill over total turns as the primary figure (it is the bill), the mean of per-session rates reported beside it so returning-user sessions stay visible. Please confirm or choose otherwise.
