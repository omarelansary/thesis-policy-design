## 1. What a loop item of about 20 samples can and cannot show (CH-0088)
At about 20 tasks per arm, a success-rate difference has a standard error of roughly 10 points, so a loop item can show a **large** loss or gain, or a **direction**; it cannot separate "nearly the same" from a loss of a few points (the size the stored sources report, for example Copilot's 2-5 points). Bill components, by contrast, are read per request from usage records and are nearly deterministic given the requests made, so bill **mechanics** (cache hits, writes, which tokens are billed) can be established in the loop; bill **differences that depend on behaviour** (how many searches, how many seeds used) are estimated with their spread.

**Decision rule for the loop (stated in advance, no threshold value).** A stage is (a) dropped when its own mechanism fails (for example a cache break where none should occur) or when it shows a large loss beside its baseline; (b) kept as a candidate when its effect is in the expected direction or unclear; (c) never declared "established" by a loop item. Accuracy effects that a loop item cannot resolve go to the end-to-end study. The asymmetry is deliberate: a small loop is allowed to remove parts, not to prove them.

## 3. End-to-end study (after the loop; outline only)
- **Arms:** K0 expose-all with caching and a summary; DPR-0008 (the rival core); V5-R as the loop leaves it (DPR-0008 plus S1 plus M2 if its gate opened); DPR-0009; Executor (REQ-O02-06), defined from P0; the all-tools baseline (REQ-O02-01) where it fits the window; V4 unchanged only if the human or the supervisor asks for it.
- **Axes:** catalogue size across R-small, R-mid and R-large; session pattern (one long run; returning users with gaps shorter and longer than the cache lifetime, REQ-U21-10); at least two models (REQ-U21-08).
- **Measures:** the bill per turn by DPR-0006 under both averagings (the human chooses the primary one), its components, requests per turn, response time, task success; repeated runs so variance is shown (REQ-O21-09); the sample size is chosen so that the accuracy differences the loop could not resolve can be resolved.
- **Not decided here:** sample sizes, repeats, models, the vehicle mix, any margin (there is none, REQ-U21-07).
