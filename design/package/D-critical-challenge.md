## D2. The strongest objections that are **still live** (lead's ranking, [lead])
1. **The sign of every component is unmeasured.** No stored source shows a second filtering stage paying or failing to pay (needs E), that models call a search interface when a needed tool is absent (E), that a system trigger beats model initiation (E), or that any restriction lowers billed cost under caching (E; ledger C3-19 bounded negative). The proposal is therefore a hypothesis class whose components are justified or not by experiments not yet authorized.
2. **Reduced exposure lowered success in most controlled settings read** (ComplexMCP, ToolMenuBench keyword filters, LiveMCPBench depth, DynamicMCPBench retrieval conditions) while the cost benefit under caching is undemonstrated. The simplest alternatives (expose all, static core, deferred loading) may dominate in many regimes; the proposal keeps them as arms and does not rank them.
3. **Initiation is the unmeasured risk of every model-driven form** (P3, P4, P4g, N3). Under-invocation evidence concerns visible tools; whether models search when a tool is absent is [U] in the stored corpus.
4. **The cache argument rests on vendor documentation.** Add-only exposure is a design constraint for comparability; its claimed benefit is a vendor assertion with no measurement in the corpus, and providers differ (rewrite breaks the cache; one vendor reports a withdraw-without-rewrite route, ledger C3-27, unread).
5. **Evaluation-vehicle threats apply to any recovery or discovery result:** single-reference replay scoring (direction of bias unknown; may inflate recovery's value), forced absence as failure by construction, stub schemas as cues, the turn budget (whose effect EXT-0042's exploratory control calls into question in one cell), evaluator error, reachability as a hard gate (DMB: pass^3 0.0% on tasks whose tools the retriever never surfaced).
6. **Selecting the objective is a scientific choice with consequences:** conclusions may flip with the accounting convention, and the Q1 reading (count of tools vs tokens) changes what "better" means.

## D3. Corrections made by the lead during the thread
- The lead's first evidence map mislabelled FitText's re-retrieval as "system-initiated". The challenger caught the open question; the lead read FitText's method (EXT-0050/0051/0055/0056): **the model emits a delimited pseudo-tool description; the system executes retrieval and only de-duplicates near-identical intents** (the 0.82 similarity condition in the appendix is that duplicate filter). ## D5. Simpler alternatives and prior-art overlap
- **Simpler policies that the evidence does not exclude:** P0 expose all (where it fits), P1 static core, P3 deferred loading with a search tool (shipped by Anthropic and OpenAI, EXT-0008/0010, vendor claims), P4 two-grade exposure, P5 stable meta-tool. A multi-stage design is *not* shown to beat them.
- **Overlap (no novelty claim):** vendor deferred tool search with cache-preserving append (EXT-0008..0011); namespace/MCP-server first disclosure (EXT-0010); FitText's mid-execution re-retrieval (EXT-0035/0036/0050/0051); JIT schema loading (EXT-0045/0046); Skill Blocks' regime map for skills (EXT-0039..0041); CE-MCP and programmatic tool calling (EXT-0014, EXT-0044); ComplexMCP iterative retrieval (EXT-0026); DynamicMCPBench `hier` (EXT-0053); also, via governing records and unread at the original, Executor passthrough, MCP-Zero, ITR, Toollery. What the stored corpus does not show (bounded: stored corpus and these extracts only): a controlled comparison of staged vs single-stage selection on tools with success and cumulative cost categories; recovery's value and unused price together; initiation of discovery as a measured variable. "Not found" is never "does not exist". The supervisor's statements that the staged graph is "your novelty" (REQ-O14-16) and "the main nobility" (REQ-O14-07) are assertions and are not checked here.
